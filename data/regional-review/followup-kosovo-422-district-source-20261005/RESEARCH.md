## Findings

The immutable geoBoundaries source file retained by merged #422 / PR #994 is `data/regional-review/regional-review-3c4fe25a21fa428d/source/gb/gb-XKX-ADM1.geojson` (1,898,578 bytes; SHA-256 `9b07b08fe0f9ca5a26ddb6de2008ac235b38ffaf069063df9851711c2d358315`). Its source is the geoBoundaries Git commit `9469f09592ced973a3448cf66b6100b741b64c0d`, committed 2023-12-13, with the original upstream LFS SHA-256 and retrieval path preserved in the parent source manifest and pointer. The companion original metadata JSON is 799 bytes, SHA-256 `a912dcaefa1096a6c0af067af9723b2a67c32f5b0147b8198f99751eb591b49c`. Its dates distinguish represented boundary year 2021, source-data update 2023-01-19 and geoBoundaries build 2023-12-12. Preserve all three dates; the built release is not a 2021 retrieval date.

The retained GeoJSON has seven unique `shapeID`s and the seven verbatim names `District of Prishtina`, `District of Mitrovica`, `District of Peja`, `District of Gjilan`, `District of Gjakova`, `District of Prizren` and `District of Ferizaj`. All seven IDs match Atlas features. The source metadata simultaneously says `ADM1`, canonical `Municipalities`, and `admUnitCount=48`; the source citation/use note defines ADM1 generically as an admin tier, but warns that levels below ADM1 vary by country. The count is not the number of records in the retained collection. The metadata and the actual payload therefore disagree (48 vs 7); the archived metadata does not explain the 48, and it must not be silently corrected in place or interpreted as a present-day municipality count.

The Kosovo Agency of Statistics (KAS) April 2022 report supplies a substantive role distinction. It describes 38 municipalities as administrative units and the seven named NUTS III regions as non-administrative territorial units grouped from municipalities. Its Table 3 assigns 7, 7, 4, 4, 5, 7 and 4 municipalities to Prishtinë, Mitrovicë, Pejë, Prizren, Ferizaj, Gjilan and Gjakovë respectively; the extracted list contains 38 distinct municipalities. The seven geoBoundaries `District of …` labels correspond one-to-one by name to those seven KAS regional labels. This supports a **seven-region/economic-statistical grouping** interpretation and contradicts the Atlas metadata's `source_role=Municipalities`. It does not demonstrate that the geoBoundaries polygons are geometrically identical to KAS regional boundaries: no authoritative same-vintage vector was obtained.

Law 03/L-041 (officially published 2008-06-02; consolidated by the Official Gazette on 2025-07-08) establishes municipalities as the basic local self-government units and sets their boundaries through cadastral zones. It enumerates 38 municipalities. The KAS 2024 census publication independently uses 38 municipalities and its map credits municipality boundaries to the Kosovo Cadastral Agency (KCA), 2024. This is authority for current administrative role/count and identifies KCA as the boundary source, but the map is not a reusable vector boundary dataset and its credit line is not a reuse license.

The three #999 subjects are Prishtina, Ferizaj and Gjilan. In the pinned Atlas they point to the existing same-name, single-child province parents and carry the pinned `geographic_area_code=YUG` and `geographic_region_code=13` reference assignments; the parent hierarchy labels that area "Yugoslavia." The other four members of the seven-feature collection are assigned to #1008. The issue contracts explain the work-item partition. This describes the existing Atlas references only: codes, a name match, a 100% stored reference overlap and an area label do not validate the outer area, political status, parent purpose, or historical applicability. Retain the stable IDs and do not reinterpret the area label as a sovereignty claim.

## Source and reuse assessment

The geoBoundaries commit is reproducible by immutable commit URL and source-file hash; the parent #422 packet already retains the actual GeoJSON, exact LFS pointer, metadata, geoBoundaries citation/use file and retrieval record. This child reuses those baseline bytes by hash and adds restoration instructions rather than copying the geometry into a second packet. geoBoundaries' own citation/use notice says its generated products are CC BY 4.0, while directing users of individual boundary files to additionally honor their source metadata. For this file that metadata says CC BY-SA 2.0 and points to the OpenStreetMap copyright page.

The current official OSM copyright page says OSM **data** is ODbL 1.0 (attribution and share-alike for adapted data) and separately says OSM's **documentation** is CC BY-SA 2.0. The source metadata may have conflated these notices, but that explanation is not proven. OSM-Boundaries' documentation says its downloaded data follows the OSM license and describes monthly imports of Planet extracts; its separate current website terms prohibit copying or redistribution of OSM-Boundaries material. The exact 2021 OSM snapshot, relation IDs, OSM-Boundaries database identifier and applicable historical service terms are missing from the geoBoundaries metadata. Direct retrieval of the two OSM-Boundaries policy pages returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-05; the indexed public documentation pages were inspected, but their contents could not be byte-hashed from the endpoint. The API's downloads are account/credit gated, and no credentials were available or used.

**Reuse conclusion: unresolved.** The ODbL attribution/share-alike requirement, geoBoundaries CC BY 4.0 notice, metadata's CC BY-SA 2.0 assertion, and OSM-Boundaries service terms are not enough to determine whether this exact derived file may be redistributed under the Atlas terms. Do not assert compatibility or incompatibility, or issue a new copy of the polygons. Use the already-retained #422 evidence for internal research and ask geoBoundaries/KCA/OSM-Boundaries for source provenance and written reuse clarification before any new redistribution. The source lineage is restorable; the historical OSM database vintage and legal chain are not yet restorable from source metadata alone.

## Parent, neighboring scale and recommendations

The same-name parents are single-child containers for these district-named features (see `source-crosswalk.json`); their labels do not transform the child into a municipality. The same source batch's regional neighbors demonstrate why level codes are not comparable globally: North Macedonia's geoBoundaries `ADM2` source is 84 features (canonical Opštini, reported in #422 as EuroGeographics/CC BY 4.0); Serbia's `ADM2` source is 145 features (canonical Unknown, OSM/Wambacher, ODbL); Montenegro's `ADM1` source is 23 features (canonical Unknown, OSM/Wambacher, ODbL). These source vintages/licenses differ, and counts alone do not identify legal tiers. Kosovo's seven groupings are coarser than its 38 current municipalities and must not be consumed as municipality boundaries.

1. Preserve all seven source shapes, geoBoundaries IDs, Atlas IDs, original source metadata and current geographic-area pins as history. For these three, retain the named district/statistical-region correspondence and do not split, replace or rename IDs without a separately sourced, reviewed crosswalk.
2. Propose a new source-role record for the seven features as named **non-administrative regional groupings** (the original source calls them districts; KAS calls the same seven municipality groups economic/statistical regions). Do not describe them as the 38 municipalities. Record source canonical/count as conflicting upstream metadata, rather than changing the immutable historic metadata.
3. Engineering handoff: coordinate #999's three exact IDs with #1008's four IDs and the relevant regional integrator before changing any shared source-role resolver, parent/area assignment or release pin. Obtain KCA's authoritative municipal vectors and explicit terms; dissolve the 38 municipality features by KAS's 2022 grouping table, pin source vintages and compare every one of the seven source polygons with documented CRS/precision and per-region results. Keep all disagreements and unsupplied boundaries explicit. No shared geography or metadata registry was changed here.
4. Keep the current same-name one-child province parent associations as provisional identity links for integration review. Do not claim they are confirmed administrative parent units; KAS explicitly calls the named regions non-administrative. Review the current `YUG`/region-13 grouping under its own fixed macro boundary evidence and preserve its pin unless a coordinated, sourced correction is approved.

## Reproduction and limits

Run `python3 data/regional-review/followup-kosovo-422-district-source-20261005/reproduce.py --root .` from the repository root. The script verifies all nine issue-declared pins against the exact baseline commit, checks the three #999 IDs against its captured contract and the other four against #1008's exact captured scope, reads the original #422 geoBoundaries source and metadata from that commit, discovers all seven Atlas IDs and parents, and verifies the KAS extract's seven-region/38-unique-municipality totals. It writes the deterministic `source-crosswalk.json`. This is an identity/metadata reproduction, not a geometry comparison or legal determination. It does not certify the territorial meaning of any polygon.

No same-vintage lawful official vector was available during this review; geometry, topology and legal-boundary accuracy for all seven remain unverified. The origin of `admUnitCount=48`, the 2021 OSM snapshot, relation IDs, and exact redistribution compatibility remain unresolved. No regional approval, political-status decision, publication, import or production operation is requested or implied.
