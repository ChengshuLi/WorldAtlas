## Roster and results

`assessment.csv` has one individual outcome for every location, source ID/feature name, same-parent comparison, source role/vintage/license, workload area, and sourced reason. Results: 225 `insufficient-evidence`; five `correction-needed`. The five correction-needed location findings are Mount Athos (source role conflicts with constitutional territorial status) and the four Kosovo district features whose pinned source collection conflicts with the “Municipalities/48” metadata. `province-scope.csv` independently lists all 17 exact parents; eight are correction-needed (the four Kosovo same-name/single-child parent tiers, Agion Oros, and the three Greek one-child fallback parents Poros, Ydra and Elafonisos), nine remain insufficient-evidence. `area-scope.csv` covers the three areas: Greece 201/281 (partial), Kriti 25/25, Yugoslavia 4/1169 (partial). Counts are exact-member inventories, not evidence that boundaries are complete or valid.

The matching source IDs/names establish lineage only. The pinned Greek ADM3 release contains 326 source features; all 226 scoped records map to a retained source feature (one Atlas multipart record assembles two source IDs). A current Ministry of Interior structure paper says Greece has 332 municipalities; Law 4600/2019 records establishment of 12 new municipalities through splits in five prior municipalities. The six-feature numerical difference between the 2010 release and the present count is not localized or explained by a count alone. The exact Gazette/legal boundary record, current coded 332-member roster, and reliable 2010-to-current identity crosswalk remain needed. Geographic boundary disputes and source completeness remain open.

The Greek release metadata calls ADM3 `municipality`, represented year 2010, but combines a CC0 declaration with a `licenseDetail` attributing some underlying sources under CC BY 3.0. The ancestor packet `regional-review-ef67318527f5f3a3` retains the exact compressed ADM3 GeoJSON and metadata under its recorded hashes; this packet references and preserves it unchanged. A new, lawfully retained ADM2 parent source ZIP and its metadata are in `sources/`. That source has 14 features (2016), a blank canonical-role field and CC BY 4.0 metadata. Its exact feature names/IDs match many current parent nodes, but the source schema does not establish local-government parent semantics. The Greek Ministry describes municipalities and regions as separate levels of local government and says they have no hierarchical relationship/control; spatial parent groupings here still need a sourced, explicit geographic purpose. Internal parent source correspondence is not a legal parent determination.

The Greek Constitution (Article 105) describes the region of Aghion Oros (Mount Athos) as a self-governed part of the Greek State. The 2010 GeoBoundaries ADM3 source labels the scoped `Mount Anthos` feature as a municipality, and the atlas nests it under a single-child `Agion Oros` parent. This is a clear source-role/repeated-tier adjudication item, not permission to alter the label, parent, geometry or ID.

The retained XKX ADM1 archive is the exact 2023-built release asset at GeoBoundaries commit `9469f09592ced973a3448cf66b6100b741b64c0d`, retrieved 2026-10-05. It contains seven polygon features named `District of …`; its same-release metadata says 2021, canonical role `Municipalities`, and `admUnitCount: 48`. The archive feature count and metadata count therefore disagree (7 vs. 48), as does the feature role/name versus its canonical label. Kosovo Agency of Statistics' 2024 census release describes 1,469 settlements in 38 municipalities. Kosovo Law 03/L-041 is a primary legal lead for municipal boundaries but this screen did not establish its exact geometry-to-feature correspondence. The four scoped districts are duplicated by name under four single-child Atlas parents; do not treat them as municipal polygons or infer status from the `XKX` code. Shared source lineage/count/license work for the seven-feature collection is already in follow-up [#999](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/999), which covers the other three Atlas IDs from the same collection; this packet recommends coordinating the four scoped IDs after that source restoration rather than duplicating its asset work.

## Geometry and granularity screens

`geometry-screen.csv` records individual polygon component counts, equal-area diagnostic areas and source-to-baseline IoU for each exact member. `geometry-method-and-neighbor-summary.json` screens the full retained 326-feature Greek source layer: among its 227 source features represented by this packet, 192 have a same-layer shared edge longer than 0.1 m, 35 do not, and no pair in the full source layer intersects by more than 1,000 m². Among the 230 Atlas records, 22 have multiple polygon components; 90 have diagnostic IoU below 0.95. Those values are prompts for sourced investigation only. The non-edge list includes named Greek islands and coastline-isolated units; it cannot establish that an island is present or omitted. There is no independently validated physical-island, municipality-boundary, city-territory or settlement-completeness source in this packet.

`parent-scale-screen.csv` records exact-subset area contrast, not an administrative tier rule. Attikis has 64 locations and a max/median equal-area ratio of 34.304; this is an oversized-group and urban granularity review trigger, not an automatic error. The pinned hierarchy records 13 Attikis children with <80% source-footprint retention and explicitly says to distinguish marine coverage, clipping and source defects. Within this subset, those IDs include Metamorfosi, Agistri, Vrilissia, Lykovrysi-Pefki, Kallithea, Agioi Anargyroi-Kamatero, Korydallos, Agia Paraskevi, Dafni-Ymittos, Zografou, Pireas, Irakleio and Agia Varvara. `assessment.csv` keeps these rows insufficient-evidence; a planar ratio cannot resolve coastline or legal-boundary causes.

Fragmented city/municipality extents, parent containment, neighboring 2021 municipal granularity and omitted islands are not certified. Oraiokastro is a single Atlas multipart record assembled from two source IDs/features both called Oraiokastro; that is preserved explicitly. The source has no anonymous names among scoped rows. No official census polygons were copied because the existing Greece packet records unresolved reuse terms. ELSTAT states its 2021 Kallikratis boundary files are census-only, prepared for its purposes, and not proof of administrative boundaries. The prior Greece screen covers 19 different IDs; it is not substituted for this packet’s 226-member audit.

After this packet’s baseline pin, neighbor packet #422 merged in PR #1009. That packet crosswalks 84 legacy North Macedonia polygons to 80 State Statistical Office 2019 municipality codes (79 one-to-one plus five Kichevo-related polygons) and reports eight statistical-region parents. Its source geometry has a 2016 vintage; the roster crosswalk does not establish current legal boundaries or any Greek/Kosovo-adjacent border. It is useful neighbor-vintage context only.

## Reproduction

From repository root, with Python 3.12.14, Shapely 2.1.2, pyproj 3.7.2 and pyshp 2.3.1:

```sh
python3 data/regional-review/regional-review-3f920fc5cb99166e/reproduce.py
PYTHONPATH=scripts python3 data/regional-review/regional-review-3f920fc5cb99166e/geometry_screen.py
python test/evidence-geography.py
python test/ellipsoidal-area.py
```

The row generator joins the exact issue scope, world-index parts, membership inventory, hierarchy parents, and retained source feature IDs/names. Both scripts read baseline bytes through the shared immutable `Baseline` verifier using the hashes in `baseline-inputs.json`. The geometry script uses EPSG:6933 and Shapely only for labeled planar diagnostics. Invalid input is repaired in-memory solely for that diagnostic clone; all source and baseline bytes are unchanged. Positive/negative measurement controls are preserved in `geometry-positive-control.json` and `geometry-negative-control.json`; baseline-verifier positive/negative controls and equal two-run generator hashes are in the roster control JSON files. Exact generated metric ledgers are in `reproduction-summary.json` and `geometry-run-summary.json`. `validation-runs.json` records actual reproduction, test and workspace-check results. The shared WGS84 helper tests are run separately. `evidence-quality.json` binds 230 subjects, baseline/source/output hashes, metric values and limits; its `limited` status is expected because current legal boundaries, source reuse and completeness remain unverified. None of these checks establishes legal boundaries, completeness, a correct parent, source licensing compatibility or regional approval.
