# West Africa interior batch 13 source and scope assessment

This is a source-only packet for [issue #479](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/479). It covers exactly the 49 pinned IDs: 12 Sierra Leone districts and 37 Togo ADM2 locations. `scope.json` retains the issue's original 49-ID digest and published v5 release pins; its `published_region_baseline` records the source snapshot used from main at `cf6598d9f6b7a03a0953006ba47bc0af565ac6e8`. The v5 baseline is the region context and is not represented as a full region approval.

## Assessment coverage

`assessment.csv` and `assessment.json` give an individual sourced assessment for all 49 IDs, including the original parent chain, current OCHA ADM2 role, source code, current OCHA parent, all settlement counts, source-vintage overlap metric, and unresolved evidence. `admin-groups.csv` assesses both area scopes and all eight province scopes. There are 47 `insufficient-evidence` rows and two `correction-needed` rows (Kambia and Port Loko). That is a completion classification for this packet's source review, not a conclusion that the entire geography is justified or approved.

The assigned roles are sourced as Sierra Leone districts and Togo ADM2 units; the 2017 Togo source calls its legacy units prefectures, while current OCHA package notes summarize all 40 ADM2 units as prefectures and explicitly names the assigned `Lome Commune` feature as a commune. Its statutory role remains unresolved. Togo's current regional parents match the five atlas province labels. For Sierra Leone, current COD-AB has five admin1 regions; two assigned districts, Kambia and Port Loko, now sit in North Western while their atlas province parent is Northern. This requires a coordinated source/role decision. #478 owns Sierra Leone's two Western Area IDs, which are outside this packet's 49-member scope.

The 2017 source inventories contain 14 Sierra Leone and 37 Togo ADM2 features. Current reviewed OCHA COD-AB v02 snapshots contain 16 Sierra Leone districts (valid from 2023-12-15) and 40 Togo ADM2 units (valid from 2021-01-07). The new current units outside the pinned legacy names are Sierra Leone's Fabala and Karene, and Togo's Agoe-Nyive, Naki-Ouest and Oti-Sud. They remain external context, not new atlas members. `source-vintage-crosswalk.csv` preserves all 253 positive-area old/current polygon intersections. Its equal-area measurements screen source vintages; they do not choose or approve boundaries.

The current OCHA WCA CC BY settlement layer contains 13,371 point rows grouped under the 49 current ADM2 codes. All 49 codes have point rows. A complete spatial screen identifies 593 same-code points outside their matched current COD-AB polygon; every row and its candidate containing current polygon is in `settlement-point-anomalies.csv`. Those are unresolved code/vintage crosswalks, not automatic transfers. `settlement-validation.json` records the separate Togo workbook comparison: 5,276 of 5,394 OCHA WCA Togo points matched the temporary workbook by normalized name and ADM2 code; 5,273 nearest coordinate pairs were within 10 m. The workbook is cataloged under license `Other`, so only its route, hash, method and aggregate result are retained. The WCA layer's `LAT` and `LONG` property values are reversed relative to conventional field names; the GeoJSON geometry is used for spatial checks.

All 181 disconnected components in the 49 current ADM2 geometries are listed in `geometry-components.csv`. Bonthe has 83 components, Port Loko 22, and Kambia 20. Point names are attached to the components where the source records place them. These administrative-source components do not establish named island identity or complete land coverage. The `city-role-screen.json` separately assesses Lomé Commune, Golfe and external-context Agoe-Nyive: all are in Maritime; they have 1, 2 and 1 polygon components, with 137, 121 and 156 current-code WCA settlement points and shared boundaries recorded. This does not establish a continuous urban footprint; an authoritative city/metropolitan extent was not established, and the adjacent distribution remains unresolved. The official NOAA GSHHG information page was reachable, but its linked data directory returned HTTP 404. `physical-source-access.json` records the routes and follow-up request; no physical coastline, omitted-land, or named-island completeness conclusion is made.

`admin1-source-comparison.csv` screens every current Sierra Leone and Togo parent region against the OCHA WCA 2026 edge-matched ADM1 layer (10 of 10 same-code features). Both sources retain the same parent names and codes; measured differences are retained by region and are not treated as approved borders. The OCHA WCA 2026 edge-matched ADM0 screen covers Sierra Leone with Guinea and Liberia, and Togo with Ghana and Benin. It finds shared country edges with no positive-area overlap in that source pair. It does not establish sovereignty, approve the shared macro envelope, or certify all regional boundaries. No shared-boundary change is proposed by this packet.

## Sources, vintages and reuse

`sources/acquisition.json` is the full source register: canonical catalog/feature/download references, retrieval routes, catalog timestamps, source represented dates, stated source role, license, exact original-byte length and SHA-256, and retained compressed-byte hashes. Lawfully reusable original GeoJSON/COD-AB/WCA source archives and metadata are gzip-retained under `sources/`; exact restoration and decompression steps are recorded there. The full licensed OCHA WCA settlement archive is preserved, along with the extracted Sierra Leone/Togo feature subset. National OCHA settlement packages labeled `Other` are metadata-only unless a temporary analysis is explicitly documented; no unclear-license source rows are redistributed.

Main source roles and vintages:

- geoBoundaries 2017 Sierra Leone ADM2: Government of Sierra Leone and OCHA ROWCA; canonical role `Districts`; CC BY 3.0 IGO.
- geoBoundaries 2017 Togo ADM2: OpenStreetMap/Wambacher; canonical role `Prefectures`; CC BY-SA 2.0. It is not a national government boundary source.
- OCHA HDX COD-AB v02 Sierra Leone and Togo: CC BY-IGO; their validity, last source edit, review date, unit counts and catalog notes are retained verbatim in the packet.
- OCHA West and Central Africa 2026 edge-matched ADM0/ADM1 and settlement inputs: CC BY; the point source reports `last_modif` dates in 2021. The settlement layer is not a census or a completeness proof.
- NOAA GSHHG: authoritative physical-coastline candidate; no data bytes or reuse terms were verified because the linked download route was unavailable. See `physical-source-access.json`.

All 49 assigned subjects are source ADM2 units, not province-level units. Across current OCHA COD-AB, their areas range from 104.57 km² (Lome Commune) to 6,933 km² (Koinadugu); area size is recorded as evidence, not used as a role threshold. The named current OCHA unit counts and point counts are metric vintages, not quotas. EU5 counts are not used as thresholds. Political affiliation is not inferred from an administrative boundary source. Antarctica is outside this issue.

## Follow-up proposals

`correction-recommendations.json` provides bounded recommendations with affected issue IDs, names, evidence and limits. They cover the Sierra Leone current source/parent and point crosswalk (coordinated with #478), Togo's 37-ID 2017 OSM to 2021 official source crosswalk and three new current units, Lomé statutory role and city extent research, and NOAA coastline/island source restoration for all 49 subjects. These follow-ups must preserve source vintages and leave shared outer boundaries to coordinated engineering review. No recommendation changes a shared parent or footprint in this packet.

## Reproduction

Run `python3 acquire_sources.py` only when intentionally refreshing source snapshots; a changed upstream hash must be reviewed as a new vintage. `python3 extract_neighbors.py` regenerates the six-country edge-matched subset using GDAL/OGR. `python3 build_assessment.py` reads only retained source bytes and regenerates the complete assessment, geometry-component inventory, settlement anomaly rows, positive-overlap source-vintage pairs and Lomé role/continuity screen. `verify.py` checks hashes, exact scope cardinality and deterministic regenerated outputs. The packet was built with Python 3.12, Shapely 2.1.2, pyproj 3.7.2, GDAL/OGR 3.10.3 and openpyxl 3.1.5. No atlas geometry or live data is modified.
