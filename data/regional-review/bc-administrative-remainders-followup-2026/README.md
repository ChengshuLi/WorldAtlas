# British Columbia ten-CD administrative remainder audit (#609)

Reviewed 2026-10-03. This packet accounts for all ten assigned 2021 Statistics Canada Census Divisions (CDs) and all 335 current CSDs inside them. It is source evidence and a correction proposal for engineering; it does not alter shared hierarchy, land geometry, or regional boundaries.

## Findings

None of the ten CDs is an empty jurisdictional void. Statistics Canada partitions them into 335 CSDs. Their CSD type rosters, per-CD 2016 source overlap, settlement evidence, current province boundary candidates, and neighboring CD contacts are retained in `cd-assessments.json` and `csd-crosswalk.csv`.

For 18 of 19 RDA-typed CSDs, the best current provincial electoral-area candidate is a named feature in the official legal EA layer; the Stikine CSD is the exception. This is a geometry/name candidate crosswalk, not proof of statutory identity: per-row overlap in `csd-crosswalk.csv` must be read in both directions. All four Columbia-Shuswap incorporated CSDs (Golden, Revelstoke, Salmon Arm, Sicamous) now have current legal municipal candidates after correcting the layer's exact group spelling and querying all selected group features.

The complete union of 2021 CSD polygons covers each CD polygon to reported 100.0% (projected symmetric difference rounds to 0.0 km² at four decimals). This confirms census-subdivision accounting, not legal/cadastral coverage. Each of 335 CSD rows has a 2016 geoBoundaries ADM3 source-feature union candidate covering at least 98.39639% of its 2021 CSD geometry. That is vintage/shape lineage evidence only; source roster matches do not certify jurisdictional equivalence or prove every source member is already an Atlas location.

The 2016 roster separately records all legacy ADM3 shapes intersecting each CD envelope, plus the full set differences where a 2016 feature intersects a target CSD union but not the CD envelope or vice versa. These small inconsistencies are cross-vintage geometry evidence and remain unresolved; they are not removed by clipping. Every mapped Atlas source member is accompanied by its full current hierarchy parent chain from the #485 baseline. Some links terminate in physical ecoregion locations; those are preserved only as historical source-lineage references and do not count as administrative representation. The CD/source-roster difference is retained explicitly in both directions: CDs 5933, 5955 and 5957 have respectively one, one and two 2016 features touching a CSD union but not the CD envelope; CD 5957 also has one feature touching the CD envelope but not any target CSD union. Such slivers are not silently clipped.

The retained settlement context records Statistics Canada population-centre polygons and NRCan Canadian Geographical Names populated-place points intersecting each CSD and each CD. They are independent positive-place references. A missing hit is not proof that settlements are absent: Population Centres apply a population/density threshold, and the names layer is not an exhaustive rural settlement inventory. Several CDs have dozens to hundreds of named places; consult the complete lists in the assessment JSON rather than treating the summary counts as settlement totals.

## Required bounded follow-ups

1. Keep #5939 (Columbia-Shuswap) crosswalked to its legal regional district and coordinate only its measured cross-vintage/statistical slivers with adjacent #5901, #5933, #5941, #5953 before any shared-edge proposal. The Census CD envelope and legal regional-district footprint are highly aligned but not identical; neither replaces the other.
2. Ask BC and Statistics Canada to reconcile CSDUID 5957022 “Stikine Region” (type RDA) against the province's distinct `Stikine Region (Unincorporated)` administration and absence of a Stikine EA feature. Do not assign it to an unrelated regional-district EA based on tiny accidental overlap.
3. Reconcile CSDUID 5933803 “Hamilton Creek 2” (IRI), for which the scoped NRCan Aboriginal Lands Legislative Boundaries service returned no reserve candidate. This federal product's catalog definition and CSD/federal geometry roles differ; the no-hit is unresolved, not evidence of absent land.
4. Compare every IRI row's federal candidate IDs/names and coverage in the ledger. Many CSD statistical polygons do not exactly match federal legal reserve polygons. Do not use overlap alone as legal attribution or interpret differences as missing land.
5. Preserve NRCan's explicit exclusion of Indian Settlements and Indian Communities. `S-É` rows need their own province/First Nation authoritative sources if exact settlement extents are required. Likewise separately resolve the one `NL` unit using Nisga'a treaty/official land sources before any ownership characterization.

These are evidence coordination recommendations, not unilateral boundary changes. Parent regional integration is tracked in #484. Neighbor CD IDs and shared-edge screens are in `cd-assessments.json`; they are statistical adjacency screens, not proposed border edits.
