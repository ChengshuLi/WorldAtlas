# Additive completion review: West Africa batch 8

Issue [#474](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/474), packet `regional-review:f834434be524dfdb`. This supplemental register preserves the first packet and its `README.md`, `assessment.json`, sources and reproduction outputs. It adds a row-by-row synthesis at `completion-review.json`; it does not replace the prior evidence or certify a region.

## Scope and determinations

The frozen packet owns 228 locations: 204 Niger 2012 ADM3 units, 13 Mauritania 2020 ADM2 units, 10 Mauritania RESOLVE ecological fragments, and one Niamey composite. It spans 56 province groups and partial workload slices of Mauritania (23/69 locations) and Niger (205/271). The completion register contains a row for each of the 228 exact IDs, each of the 56 province IDs, and each of the two area IDs. Every location and province remains `insufficient-evidence`; each area remains a partial-scope insufficient-evidence determination. The generated counts and classification are not administrative validation.

The per-subject rows bind the original source class and source feature identity to its frozen baseline GeoJSON file, identify parent context and neighboring tier, summarize vintage, role, completeness and license evidence, and link any relevant source/role or boundary screen. The province rows enumerate their exact scoped members and summarize only this packet's parent evidence. The area rows enumerate their owned subset and explicitly state why botanical-country grouping cannot establish geographic area purpose or ownership.

## Source-specific results

- **Niger communes.** The inspected official INS 2012 table has a 266-row commune reference organized by region and department. In this packet, 190 source names and department labels normalize to the INS row; 10 spelling or special-city role aliases are supported; four Tibiri (Doutchi) rows retain unresolved candidate name/parent assignments. The 2012 statistical reference is not a current legal gazetteer. The exact captured INS response hash and retrieval/restoration details remain in `sources.json` and `niger-ins-crosswalk.json`. The [official INS projection route](https://www.stat-niger.org/projections/) is the recorded retrieval source.
- **Niger parent geometry screen.** The earlier reproducible WGS84 ellipsoidal-area comparison tested all 204 scoped ADM3 subjects against all 67 retained 2018 ADM2 candidates: 172 have a maximum subject-area overlap of at least 90%; 32 are below 90%. These are cross-vintage diagnostics. High overlap cannot prove a legal parent, and low overlap cannot alone identify a bad boundary. Five of the low-overlap subjects belong to the separately bounded special-city question; the remaining 27 low-overlap units and four unresolved INS rows are the exact 31-member child [#833](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/833). The [Niger special-city review #806](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/806) owns its six exact Atlas subjects.
- **Niger source rights.** The recorded 2012 geoBoundaries metadata asserts CC BY 3.0 while the upstream chain and CityPopulation geospatial terms leave geometry reuse unresolved. The original ADM3 geometry is not retained in this packet. The exact asset SHA, byte count, immutable URL and restoration steps are recorded; do not republish the geometry based on the metadata label alone. Because the same source collection is split across the active #475 packet (51 subjects) and this packet (204), shared rights research is isolated in the blocked 255-subject child [#834](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/834), dependent on both parent packets.
- **Mauritania administrative districts.** The retained 2020 ADM2 layer has 57 features. The packet's 13 subjects each have at least 95% overlap with a same-name 2015 ADM1 candidate, which supports the historical source crosswalk only. Government evidence reports 61 Moughataas in the 2021–2023 strategy and DGAT reports 63 attributed to RGPH 2024; six districts elevated in 2021 are absent from the 57-feature layer. This is a dated roster/completeness gap, not a license to synthesize boundaries. The all-69 member current crosswalk remains with [#804](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/804).
- **Mauritania ecological fragments.** The 10 source-derived fragments refer to five RESOLVE 2017 ecoregions and four administrative-source units. The retained ArcGIS item identifies an ecological role and CC BY 4.0 item terms; the role is not administrative or political. Reproduction on diagnostic geometry clones finds over 97.8% coverage for each expected intersection, but symmetric differences range up to about 3.4%. Invalid nested shells required `make_valid` on clones; no original bytes were rewritten. These checks establish approximate geometric correspondence, not exact clipping, complete physical-area coverage, or a suitable Atlas purpose. The source-purpose and fragment review is tracked by [#801](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/801).
- **Niamey aggregation.** The frozen baseline metadata lists six 2012 ADM3 source members for one city composite, while Maradi I–III and Tahoua I–II appear as five separate current Atlas subjects. This documents a neighboring-granularity inconsistency to investigate, not which representation is correct. Current legal-city boundaries, municipality roles, and urban extent remain unresolved in #806.

The source layer snapshots and official counts have different vintages and purposes. The register makes no boundary correction and does not claim that the two areas, their political ownership, or the West Africa region's interior is approved.

## Reproduction and controls

From repository root:

```sh
python3 data/regional-review/regional-review-f834434be524dfdb/reproduce_review.py
python3 data/regional-review/regional-review-f834434be524dfdb/complete_second_pass.py
python3 data/regional-review/regional-review-f834434be524dfdb/verify_completion.py
```

The first command rehashes all 44 pinned files from frozen commit `0c6232db2b2f9531a479f0c752f66c4d12013f3b`, resolves all 228 IDs, checks class counts, and exercises digest/unknown-ID negative controls. The second reconstructs the subject, province and area register from those immutable baseline blobs and prior packet outputs. The third checks exact issue scope, unique/complete rows, source-class totals, province membership partitions, area membership totals, evidence references and follow-up assignments. These reproduce membership and diagnostics; they do not validate every source boundary or establish current law, source completeness, rights, or regional correctness.
