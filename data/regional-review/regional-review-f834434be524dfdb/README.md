## Result and limits

`assessment.json` catalogs every scoped location, province, and partial area. Locations and provinces are conservatively classified `insufficient-evidence`: source identity and frozen membership are reproducible, but the issue requires a per-subject determination of territorial role, parent, boundary relationship, completeness, license, and fit to the neighboring scale. Those checks have not all been completed. The five findings below are actionable leads, not a claim that any regional interior or location is approved. No hierarchy, footprint, release, or production data was changed.

1. **Niger unit-role crosswalk and special-city granularity.** The official INS [2012–2026 population projection reference](https://www.stat-niger.org/projections/) is based on the 2012 census and presents 266 rows organized by region, department and commune. The root route currently returns the exact captured 64,074-byte body (SHA256 `a8833e9460c1fb42c0647c42b99b518144ca34923e8e1de3296bae1ee153b6a7`). The older nested file route and a mistakenly tested nested `/statistique/file/Annuaires_Statistiques/projections/` route return non-table pages; re-fetch the root route and verify the hash before parsing. The full HTML is not committed because redistribution terms were not reviewed, but the exact crosswalk and restoration route are recorded. The 2012 ADM3 source also contains 266 units. A reproducible join matches 190 scoped names and parents exactly after normalization, confirms 10 source spelling/special-city aliases, and leaves four units under the `Tibiri (Doutchi)` department with candidate names but a parent-role mapping still unresolved. The official table identifies four special city departments; in the Atlas, Maradi I–III and Tahoua I–II appear as separate locations while Niamey is one composite with six ADM3 members. This is evidence of uneven neighboring granularity; it does not establish current legal equivalence or which representation is correct. A current statutory crosswalk and an explicit product-purpose decision are still needed.
2. **Mauritania ADM2 completeness and vintage.** The pinned geoBoundaries source is a 2020 vintage and contains 57 features. DGAT’s current home page reports 63 Moughataas. A Mauritanian government SNGM/Plan d’Action 2021–2023 [strategy PDF](https://dgat.interieur.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2025-06/SNGM%20ET%20PA%202021%2020230_0_0.pdf), dated 2022-01-25, reports 61 Moughataas on page 6; DGAT’s later current page attributes 63 to RGPH 2024. The two official snapshots establish a count discrepancy but do not reconcile the roster transition. A [Mauritanian government news-agency notice](https://ami.mr/fr/archives/213579) says six arrondissements were elevated to Moughataas on 2021-09-08: Adel Bagrou (Hodh Ech Chargui), Touil (Hodh El Gharbi), Lexeiba (Gorgol), Male (Brakna), Tékane (Trarza), and Wompou (Guidimaka). These six names are absent from the 57-feature source and from the Atlas’s Mauritania source classes inspected here. This establishes a specific post-vintage source-coverage gap; no new boundary shapes or statutory decree texts were retained, so do not synthesize or import boundaries. Obtain legal texts and official current polygons, then reconcile effective dates, memberships, and any affected names/parents.
   The 13 scoped ADM2 members have a separate positive role/parent signal: every subject has at least 95% overlap with a 2015 geoBoundaries ADM1 candidate whose name agrees with its Atlas parent. This supports the present district-to-Wilaya grouping in the pinned source snapshots, not current legal parentage or boundary equality. The exact subject candidates are in `mauritania-parent-overlap.json`.
3. **Mauritania physical fragments.** Ten scoped Atlas records use five RESOLVE 2017 ecoregions and four source Moughataas. RESOLVE’s [ArcGIS item](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=37ea320eebb647c6838c23f72abae5ef) describes ecological ecoregions, with CC BY 4.0 item terms and a 2022 data-last-edit date. An ecological boundary is not an administrative boundary. The reproducible exact-source comparison in `mauritania-fragment-reproduction.json` finds all ten existing fragments cover over 97.8% of their diagnostic source intersections, but symmetric differences range from about 0.001% to 3.4%. The three relevant raw RESOLVE polygons have invalid nested shells; only diagnostic clones were passed through Shapely `make_valid`, while retained raw bytes were left unchanged. These figures show approximate correspondence, not exact clipping or legal validity. The intended tier/purpose and reviewed treatment of source geometry must be resolved.
4. **Niger parent-boundary diagnostics.** `niger-parent-overlap.json` compares the 204 scoped 2012 ADM3 units against all 67 units in the pinned 2018 ADM2 source using the shared WGS84 ellipsoidal-area helper. The maximum candidate parent overlap is at least 99% for 54 units and at least 90% for 172; 32 fall below 90%. Notable cases include Agadez (77.6% against Tchirozérine), Maradi I (38.4% against Madarounfa), Maradi III (59.3% against Maradi), and several 80–90% overlaps. These are differently dated source geometries, not legal parentage or proof of errors; they flag units needing a role/vintage crosswalk and boundary review. A special city or changed boundary can make geometric containment an invalid semantic test.
5. **Niger geometry license chain.** geoBoundaries metadata describes the 2012 NER ADM3 and 2018 ADM2 extractions as CC BY 3.0 while naming CityPopulation as an underlying source. The [CityPopulation terms](https://www.citypopulation.de/termsofuse.html) distinguish population-data terms from third-party map/geospatial data. The geometry rights therefore remain unresolved. Exact source SHAs, immutable URLs, metadata, and private-cache restoration directions are recorded in `sources.json`; raw NER geometries were removed from this packet and should not be redistributed without an authoritative license chain.

The geoBoundaries release commit is `9469f09592ced973a3448cf66b6100b741b64c0d`. Mauritania’s retained ADM2 and ADM1 files have source metadata declaring CC BY 3.0 IGO and CC BY-SA 3.0, respectively; the exact file hashes and source attributes are in `sources.json`. The release-wide geoBoundaries citation instructions are retained alongside the metadata. `issue-snapshot.json`, `work-contract.json`, `claim-receipt.json`, `issue-scope.json`, `baseline-inputs.json`, source metadata, and the exact five-feature RESOLVE response provide the immutable audit trail.

## Reproduction

From repository root, run:

```sh
python3 data/regional-review/regional-review-f834434be524dfdb/reproduce_review.py
```

It verifies all 44 pinned input sizes and SHA-256 values, resolves all 228 exact scoped IDs from the frozen geometry parts, checks source-class counts, and runs positive/negative digest and ID controls. To repeat geometry diagnostics, restore the two NER GeoJSON files to a private cache using the immutable URLs and hashes in `sources.json`, then run `reproduce_niger_parent_overlap.py --adm3 /private/cache/adm3.geojson --adm2 /private/cache/adm2.geojson`. Restore the official INS page by its URL/hash in `sources.json` and run `reproduce_niger_ins_crosswalk.py --ins-html /private/cache/ins.html --adm3 /private/cache/adm3.geojson`. Run `reproduce_mauritania_parent_overlap.py` and `reproduce_mauritania_fragments.py` with the committed pinned inputs. Passing confirms repeatable membership and source-overlap measurements only; it does not certify boundary accuracy, legal role, source completeness, licensing, or hierarchy semantics.
