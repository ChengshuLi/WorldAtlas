# Southern South America batch 4: scoped source review

Retrieved 2026-10-05. This packet covers exactly the 215 `member_location_ids` in `scope/embedded-workload-scope.json`, within the issue’s declared owned path. It is a source and identity review, not a boundary certification.

## Scope and reproducible matches

The issue’s machine scope lists 215 records: 167 `gb:CHL:ADM3` communes, 47 `gb:PRY:ADM2` districts, and the aggregate `atlas:city:PRY-4837` (Asunción). All 215 IDs occur in baseline parts 2, 20, or 28. There are 39 distinct baseline `parent_id` values, although the issue prose says 32; the machine scope was preserved without edits. The scope’s exact list and hash are retained in `scope/embedded-workload-scope.json`.

The 167 Chile records match one-to-one by `shapeID` and exact name against geoBoundaries CHL ADM3; the 47 Paraguay records likewise match geoBoundaries PRY ADM2. Per-row source and baseline geometry types and vertex counts are in `crosswalk.csv`. This proves identity/name linkage to those source files only. Lower baseline vertex counts, geometry-type differences, and structural validity are not evidence of positional correctness.

The baseline records label Chile as commune / ADM3, reference year 2020, geoBoundaries gbOpen, CC BY 3.0 IGO; the metadata describes 345 national features, compiled from BCN and OCHA ROLAC, with source update Jan 2023/build Dec 2023. Paraguay is labelled district / ADM2, reference year 2012, geoBoundaries gbOpen, CC BY 4.0; the metadata describes 247 national features and DGEEC source material. The exact original source bytes were retrieved and inspected during this review, then removed from the packet because each full-country decompressed source exceeds the 32 MiB evidence input cap. The source register preserves immutable commit, URL, exact byte length, full-file SHA-256, retrieval date, metadata-file hash, license basis and deterministic restoration instructions. The geoBoundaries source licenses permit redistribution with attribution. The per-row crosswalk is therefore independently reproducible only after restoring and hash-checking those inputs.

## Chile

SUBDERE’s official 2023 DPA publication describes updated commune/province/region polygon cartography using SUBDERE interior limits, DIFROL international limits, and IGM coastlines, checked periodically against the legal framework. This is a more current authoritative source lead than the 2020 geoBoundaries snapshot. The official archive was retrieved and hashed, but the local RAR5 extractor failed and no explicit reuse terms were found. It is therefore not retained, and no per-commune comparison to its polygons is claimed. The 2022 SUBDERE/IGM archive is linework with stated digitizing limitations and no located explicit license; it is restoration-only and cannot validate the baseline polygons.

Required engineering follow-up: obtain and reproducibly extract the official 2023 polygons in an environment/tool that supports this archive; establish reuse/attribution permission from the publisher; match all 167 scoped communes and their 33 baseline parents; compare boundaries and international/coastal edges, document any legal-version differences, and only then propose sourced geometry corrections. The national geoBoundaries count (345) is not equated to the 346 communes reported by SUBDERE; this packet has not established a scoped omission.

## Paraguay

The official INE 2022 cartography portal says its DPA lines are for statistical field operations and merely referential. Its district metadata calls boundaries approximate and without legal scope. The INE public-information license allows reuse with attribution and no implied endorsement. An attribute-only response, with geometry omitted, was retained as a row crosswalk. Forty-one legacy district names normalize directly to the 2022 INE names. Six scoped rows were manually crosswalked to a different expanded/orthographic current label in `crosswalk.csv`; those candidate matches include General Diaz / GRAL. JOSÉ EDUVIGIS DÍAZ, Los Laureles / LAURELES, Mariscal Estigarribia / MARISCAL JOSÉ FÉLIX ESTIGARRIBIA, Mayor Martinez / MAYOR JOSÉ DEJESÚS MARTÍNEZ, Tte 1Ro Manuel Irala Fernandez / TTE. 1° MANUEL IRALA FERNÁNDEZ, and Tte. Esteban Martinez / TENIENTE ESTEBAN MARTÍNEZ. All 47 rows now have a candidate 2022 name/code/department match. These support statistical roster identity only; they do not resolve formal legal identity or boundaries.

The National Cadastre’s technical study documents inter-agency disagreement and legal limits that often lack adequate technical descriptions. It reports especially material problems in the scoped departments: in Ñeembucú, 12 of 16 districts have no legal delimitation document; in Presidente Hayes, only four of ten have laws with enough technical elements; in Alto Paraguay, Fuerte Olimpo has no boundary law and Puerto Casado’s law gives only natural/cultural descriptions. The report also describes the 2012 geoBoundaries label Mariscal Estigarribia as a district while its annex identifies the 1980 instrument as a community classification. Those are direct reasons not to certify district geometry or, for that row, administrative role from name matching alone. The report and its precise restoration URL/hash are listed in the source register; it is not redistributed because no reuse terms were located.

Required engineering follow-up: review each scoped row’s constitutive law and the current INE/municipal/Cadastre crosswalk; resolve role and legal parent identity; obtain licensed, legally defensible geometry and compare all 47 polygons plus shared boundaries and neighboring-country edges. Statistical cartography may help locate candidate identities but must not be used as legal boundary truth.

## Asunción aggregate and granularity

`atlas:city:PRY-4837` is an Atlas-source aggregate attributed to undated modern Natural Earth admin-1 material, described as a capital district, and names four 2012 district source members. Existing semantic audit overlap figures for those members vary from 0.72124 to 0.96308. The 2022 INE statistical roster contains Asunción (code 0000), but this does not validate the aggregate’s extent or prove equivalent administrative meaning. Preserve the record pending engineering review of source entity, tier, component overlap, parent, and legal status. Its relation to city-level neighboring or metropolitan granularity remains unresolved.

## Reproduction boundary

The evidence establishes exact workload scope, baseline presence, source identity/name matches, source vintages and stated licenses, and documented limitations. It does **not** establish legal completeness, current legal roles for every unit, correct parentage in law, positional boundary accuracy, topological correctness, neighboring-country consistency, or whether the Asunción aggregate should remain. No geometry changes are proposed in this research packet. The exact scoped source-restoration and legal-boundary follow-ups are [#932](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/932) and [#933](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/933). The Asunción engineering handoff is the existing [#930](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/930), under #446. The exact city ID is absent from #446’s subject scope, so #930 is a related handoff only and needs coordination with #445; it does not cover or replace these source findings. A successful reproduction means the row-to-source and scope checks pass, not that the region is geographically correct.
