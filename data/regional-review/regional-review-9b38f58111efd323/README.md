## Findings from independent administration sources

The Yunnan Civil Affairs Department's administrative setup page reports its current county-level divisions and gives lists for each prefecture. At its 2025-09-30 table date it reports 13 for Honghe, 9 for Qujing, 11 for Zhaotong, and 8 for Wenshan. The four pinned Atlas parents contain 13, 8, 11, and 8 respectively. The 40 rows are all present in the retained 2017 source, but the Qujing count is one below the current official list. In the same official list, Qujing has Qilin, Zhanyi, and Malong districts; Xuanwei City; and five counties. The retained Qujing-parent names instead include `Qujingshi`, while the pinned eight-member set has no explicit Qilin or Zhanyi name. This is a sourced identity/completeness discrepancy; it does not identify the missing or misrepresented polygon without original maps/code crosswalks.

The 2023 Yunnan local standards for administrative-village codes provide tables of county-level names and codes under each prefecture. The official Qujing standard lists nine units; the Honghe and Wenshan standards list 13 and 8. These standards support administrative tier and current list membership, but contain no polygon geometry and cannot alone certify source boundaries. The Provincial Civil Affairs Department's 2010 annual report says Mengzi and Wenshan completed county-to-city conversions in 2010; its 2013 notices and Mile's official history document Mile's 2013 conversion. These predate the geoBoundaries layer's declared 2017 year, yet the retained source still calls them `Mengzixian`, `Wenshanxian`, and `Milexian`. The retained `Jianshanxian` is not present in the current Wenshan list (which has Yanshan County, 砚山县). These labels need an identity-preserving crosswalk and source review. The post-2017 conversions of Malong County (2018) and Shuifu County (2018) explain their older source names and do not by themselves show changed county-tier geography.

The official Qujing municipal history says that in 1997 the prefecture-level Qujing City was established and the former county-level Qujing City was divided into Qilin and Zhanyi districts. This makes the 2017 feature named `Qujingshi` incompatible with a county-level label absent a demonstrated source-specific meaning. `Zhaotongshi` is likewise absent from the current official county-level list, which includes Zhaoyang District; `Ludingshi` is absent where the official list includes Ludian County. The exact feature-to-official-unit crosswalk for those shapes remains unresolved, so their IDs or geometry must not be reassigned from a name/count inference alone.

## Reproduction and scope controls

Run from this checkout with Node 24:

```sh
node data/regional-review/regional-review-9b38f58111efd323/verify_packet.mjs
```

`reproduction-results.json` is regenerated from the retained source bytes and immutable Git blobs at the baseline. It verifies the exact 40-member subject roster, unique links to 40 source features, each feature's source name and recorded parent, the 13/8/11/8 parent counts, all 125 Yunnan inventory IDs' presence in this same source file, coordinate ranges, geometry type and ring closure. It records containing-file hashes and exact inputs. These checks do not assess polygon topology, gaps/overlaps, completeness of islands, agreement with official county polygons, or territorial meaning. A successful run is not geographic approval.

Per-location preliminary dispositions and name evidence are in `assessment.json`. Rows marked `justified` are only supported as named county-equivalent subjects in the current official lists and the pinned source; their boundaries still need primary-map comparison. `correction-needed` and `insufficient-evidence` rows must retain their original IDs and geometry until a sourced crosswalk/restoration is reviewed. See the linked official records below and the exact subject rows in `reproduction-results.json`.

The 40 individual classifications are 33 `justified`, 5 `correction-needed`, and 2 `insufficient-evidence`. These are label/role and current-roster dispositions only. All 40 boundary assessments remain unverified. Scope register, exact URL/access/retention records, source bytes/hashes and restoration limits are in `scope.json` and `sources/source-register.json`.

Two bounded, non-overlapping follow-ups were created from these findings and are blocked behind this packet: [#914](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/914) covers the seven identity/Qujing-count discrepancies; [#915](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/915) covers source provenance and boundary validation for all 40 exact IDs. They have distinct owned directories and evidence-v1 contracts. Their dependencies prevent competing with this reservation; neither authorizes geography changes or regional approval.
