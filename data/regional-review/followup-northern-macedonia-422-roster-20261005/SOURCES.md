# Source register and restoration instructions

Retrieved 2026-10-05 (UTC). This packet retains the exact 2016 HDX/RIMWGE archive because its dataset is marked CC BY. Official SSO and AKN geometry bytes are not retained: the source pages/layers did not state reuse terms sufficient to redistribute them. Their byte lengths, SHA-256 values, retrieval locations and restoration procedures are recorded here. `official-roster.json` is the limited 80-municipality/8-region transcription used in this issue; it is an output, not a retained copy of the official workbook.

The exact #995 issue title, labels and reviewed body were captured through the GitHub REST API in `issue-scope.json` on 2026-10-05. The evidence manifest cross-checks its IDs and baseline pins against that issue contract.

## Retained source: HDX / Regional IM Working Group - Europe (2016)

- Dataset: [North Macedonia - Subnational Administrative Boundaries (RIMWGE)](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/2762c3c6-fa93-4dc6-9e82-5af6e5133c18). HDX package metadata labels the dataset license “Creative Commons Attribution International (CC BY)” (`cc-by`); the metadata response inspected on 2026-10-05 was 7,895 bytes, SHA-256 `791b039ac8c1921c715b6704b4a8d56da0c6c8fcafbe71c82c00e61415ae6f52`. Metadata identifies the publisher as Regional IM Working Group - Europe and describes its inputs as EuroGeographics and the State Statistical Office’s NTES.
- Retained download: [`mkd_admn_adm_py_eurogeographics-ntes_pp.zip`](source/hdx-rimwge/mkd_admn_adm_py_eurogeographics-ntes_pp.zip), 224,800 bytes, SHA-256 `dabdc4f606584f9df0410fd3faaaa1a0b932a944d0a9b3015078fc4e1a54d7fb`. HDX lists the resource as created 2016-04-14, modified 2016-05-12, and as the level 1-4 EuroGeographics/NTES shapefile set. Attribute EuroGeographics and RIMWGE and recheck the source page’s license statement before downstream redistribution; the dataset metadata does not specify the CC BY version or separately resolve third-party input rights.
- The retained ADM4 shapefile is ETRS89 (`.prj`), UTF-8 (`.CPG`), 84 polygon records. Its Esri lineage XML identifies `EGM_v80_Extract_2.gdb/EuroGlobalMap/PolbndA` and an append process dated 2015-10-27; the shapefile export dates are April/May 2016. This is source-production lineage, not proof of the legal effective date represented by every boundary.
- The shapefile’s `SHN4` field is not a unique feature key: `MK00307` repeats on Vraneshtitsa, Drugovo, Zajas, Kichevo and Oslomej; `MK00809` repeats on all ten Skopje city-municipality polygons. Do not join or collapse Atlas features on `SHN4`. The per-feature name, retained geometry and Atlas location ID are separately recorded in `crosswalk-results.json`.

## geoBoundaries collection retained on the immutable baseline

The [geoBoundaries release source](https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/raw/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/MKD/ADM2/geoBoundaries-MKD-ADM2.geojson) is also in the merged #422 packet at baseline commit `5b72dc3adf48b089c3319b18c3a447468196176a`, path `data/regional-review/regional-review-3c4fe25a21fa428d/source/gb/gb-MKD-ADM2.geojson`, 426,003 bytes, SHA-256 `0a0d7340810fb353c3faa37ab9afea20830ce6124083ffe6321182277da61d01`. Its accompanying metadata file is 988 bytes, SHA-256 `596f6646f659023b3e4be7f62a076ae69c6401c09682b8197088d241b510cd82`.

The retained geoBoundaries metadata says boundary year 2016, ADM2, source “EuroGeographics, Regional IM Working Group - Europe,” 84 units and CC BY 4.0. It also records a source-data update on 2023-01-19 and file build on 2023-12-12. Its source/terms URL is malformed (`https//...`, missing `:`). An update/build date is not a boundary epoch.

Reproduction compares all 84 geometries in the retained 2016 HDX ADM4 shapefile to the geoBoundaries GeoJSON, matching by the source’s English `Name4`/`shapeName`. After ETRS89/EPSG:4326 to EPSG:3035 transformation, all 84 are equivalent within 1 mm Hausdorff distance and `1e-8` symmetric-difference fraction; observed maxima are emitted in `crosswalk-results.json`. This confirms geoBoundaries reproduced the 2016 HDX geometry, not that the 2016 “representative year” is legally correct.

## Source/date interpretation and scope

- The 84 feature count and named units in the geoBoundaries/HDX source match SSO’s documented 2007 NTES roster, while SSO’s 2014 amendment and 2017/2019 sources establish an 80-municipality roster and the four Kichevo mergers. Thus the source’s “boundary year 2016” cannot certify a current legal 80-unit roster. The exact legal/coordinate epoch of each source polygon remains unproven.
- AKN’s service name includes “2022” but does not expose a per-feature vintage. Its 80 unique municipality codes agree with the official 2019 NTES codes after normalization of two Cyrillic `МК` prefixes. AKN is the official geometry comparator in this packet, but unknown license terms and epoch limit conclusions.
- No SSO/AKN source geometry has been copied to this packet. For AKN geometry, only derived area-intersection results and exact response hashes are retained. They support identity/crosswalk research and identify discrepancies; they do not certify boundaries, rights or publication fitness.
