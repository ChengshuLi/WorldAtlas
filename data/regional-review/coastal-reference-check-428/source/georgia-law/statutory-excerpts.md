# Georgia county boundary law excerpts

Source version: Official Code of Georgia Annotated 2025 supplement, current through the 2025 Regular Session, as transcribed by Georgia Commons. CC0-1.0 applies to the statutory text; this file excludes editorial annotations. Original page response hashes, retrieval times, and restoration URLs are in `retrieval.json`.

- **O.C.G.A. § 36-1-1:** Georgia's 159 county boundaries and limits are ascertained by the Acts laying out each county and amendatory Acts.
- **O.C.G.A. § 36-1-2:** When a stream forms a county boundary, county jurisdiction extends to the center of its main channel, subject to the State-boundary exception in the section.
- **O.C.G.A. § 36-3-1:** A county-boundary change petition describes existing marks/monuments and the proposed line's direction, location, and length.
- **O.C.G.A. § 36-3-25:** A survey and plat established and recorded through the boundary-dispute procedure can make the disputed line final and conclusive.

These provisions establish framework and process only. They do not resolve the offshore water convention, island completeness, or the present coastal county lines in this packet.
