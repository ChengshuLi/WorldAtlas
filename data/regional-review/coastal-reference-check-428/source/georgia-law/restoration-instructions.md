# Georgia county-line statutes: restoration and reuse limits

The captured sources were Georgia Commons copies of O.C.G.A. §§ 36-1-1, 36-1-2, 36-3-1 and 36-3-25, current through the 2025 Regular Session: `https://georgiacommons.org/code/36-1-1`, `/36-1-2`, `/36-3-1`, and `/36-3-25`. `retrieval.json` records each exact fetched response SHA-256, bytes and retrieval date. The full HTML is not retained because it contains editorial Code annotations with no license established here. To restore it, fetch each exact page URL; the hashes identify the observed response. Georgia Commons states its statutory text corpus is CC0-1.0. For legal adjudication, confirm against the official state Code/session laws linked from the Georgia General Assembly; the captured reproduction is not itself the official publisher.

The short `statutory-excerpts.md` file retains only the relevant public-law text and identifying section references. These sections give general framework and process; they do not identify the exact present shoreline/offshore lines of Chatham, Glynn, McIntosh, Camden, or Liberty counties. The founding and amendatory local Acts and dispositive recorded plats remain an engineering/research follow-up if a legal-boundary correction is proposed.
