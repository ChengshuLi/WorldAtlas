## Assessment

- The whole named emergent island is the supported location granularity. OSM coastline way 130970566 (v27, 2025-12-20) is a closed `natural=coastline`, `place=island` feature. The retained candidate dry-land polygon is about 1.456 km² after subtracting one mapped reservoir ring. JMA describes a small low coral island; a peer-reviewed source places its surrounding waters in the western North Pacific. This is a location identity and physical description, not a unique continental-geology conclusion.
- JMA says its meteorological station is continuously staffed; the retained mapping also shows an airfield and public/government facilities. That establishes facility occupancy, not a distinct civilian settlement, community, or additional atlas location. No separate settlement is sourced in the audited material; resident/community status is unresolved.
- The current bottom-up chain is location → province “Marcus Island” → area “Marcus isolated island” → Northwestern Pacific → Micronesia → Oceania. The first two containers each have one child and are coextensive with this location. The prior single-island exception rationale (no sourced subdivision or wider local island group) is plausible but cannot be accepted as settled: OSM relation 11775500, explicitly tagged `place=archipelago`, `name:en=Bonin Islands`, directly includes this island's coastline way as an outer member. This conflicts with interpreting Marcus as physically detached from the Bonin/Ogasawara named group. The relation's semantics and regional parent consequences require coordinated source review. The separate OSM administrative boundary relation 8004038 and land-area relation 8004037 are not evidence of physical grouping or a license to infer sovereignty-based placement.
- Preserve the current macro route as the published baseline pending that review: Northwestern Pacific → Micronesia → Oceania, with Japan as the affected neighboring regional context. No release pin or hierarchy/geometry was changed. Do not move shared boundaries or recast the OSM relation as definitive physical truth without review.
- Source-profile file `data/macro-foundation/new-location-source-profiles-v4.json.gz` cited by the issue is absent in this checkout; expected hash in the issue is `cc782e4ad59694f37b29f484ad427e26997f9c077b856f1fdf770335e83fe9da`. The original OSM, JMA, and other retained source bytes in the prior source packet are available and independently hashed here. The missing profile is a provenance gap, not a reason to infer evidence.
