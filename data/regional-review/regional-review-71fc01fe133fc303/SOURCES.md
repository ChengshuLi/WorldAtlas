## Source identity, administrative meaning, vintage and license

The issue's starting source is geoBoundaries gbOpen TUR ADM2, `boundaryCanonical=Districts`, from OpenStreetMap / OSM Boundaries at commit `9469f09592ced973a3448cf66b6100b741b64c0d`. Its exact original GeoJSON and metadata remain retained in the already merged #72 source packet; this packet references those immutable baseline files by path and hash instead of creating duplicate source copies. The ADM2 metadata declares boundary year 2021, source data updated 2023-01-19, build 2023-12-12, and 999 units. The retained raw source contains 973 features, leaving a 26-feature metadata/file discrepancy. `shapeID`, `shapeName`, `shapeType=ADM2`, and `shapeGroup=TUR` identify source rows; the features have no named province-parent field. The metadata's OSM copyright URL and OSM Boundaries URL are malformed (`https//` lacks a colon). Source version, stated year, role and license do not prove that every geometry is a complete 2021 legal district.

The ADM2 source declares Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 and OpenStreetMap contributor attribution. The source and database obligations must be retained for any further copying or database adaptation. The 2021 ADM1 comparison layer is also OSM-derived and separately declares Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0; it contains 81 province features. Its metadata says source data updated 2023-08-31 and build 2023-12-12. Keep the original layer terms distinct. The packet includes source IDs/names and comparative results from both layers; it does not make a legal determination about downstream licensing of combined derivatives.

Turkey's legal hierarchy places provinces above districts: Law No. 5442 Article 1 describes central administration as Turkey divided into provinces and provinces into districts; Article 2 assigns the establishment/abolition, names, attachments, centers and boundary changes of provinces/districts to specified statutory or executive procedures. The official TBMM Constitution Article 126 page describes provinces as divided into lower-level administrative districts. These general rules make `Districts` a plausible role for the source layer, but do not identify the 2021 status or province attachment of any given OSM-derived source row. No authoritative same-vintage row roster, parent-code crosswalk, or official 2021 district-boundary layer was retained here.

TurkStat's official *Türkiye in Statistics 2021*, table 1.2, reports 81 provinces and 922 districts for 2019–2021 and explicitly footnotes that province centers (central districts) are excluded from the district count. Its counts establish an official convention, not the roster of these 228 IDs. The 973-feature source count is numerically 51 higher than 922, but count arithmetic cannot establish which rows are central districts, prove that the datasets describe the same convention, or certify source completeness. The separate geoBoundaries metadata count of 999 remains unresolved under source-release follow-up #817. The current Interior Ministry directory also describes 81 provinces and 922 district-governor offices, but it is current-vintage context, not a 2021 list. TurkStat's 2021 ABPRS metadata identifies 2021-12-31 as the statistical reference date; that is not a boundary vintage.

The Turkish General Command of Mapping (HGM) 2022 notice says its province/district boundary vectors are indicative/display data and not official. A current HGM archive was retained in prior packet #72 with its terms and hash, but it was not used as proof for 2021 boundaries here. Do not substitute those current illustrative boundaries for legal records.
