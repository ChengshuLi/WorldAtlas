# Southeastern North America, South Atlantic packet 3 — source and semantic review

Research date: 2026-10-05 (UTC). Baseline: `f7b45a2d491ced539ca520979e182bcec9e0e90b`. This is a review-only packet for issue #429, limited to its exact 268 member IDs and owned path. It does not approve the whole South Atlantic, its regional envelope, or historical imports. Issues #427 and #428 own the other workload partitions; they remain outside this packet.

## Findings

- **County meaning and tier:** All 268 Atlas members use the retained geoBoundaries USA ADM2 source role `Counties`. The U.S. Census Bureau describes counties and county-equivalent entities as primary state divisions, while county subdivisions sit below counties and may be legal or statistical. This supports the Atlas administrative role for the named entities; the label `ADM2` alone was not used as evidence. The four scoped state parents are Florida (67), North Carolina (100), South Carolina (46), and West Virginia (55). The current hierarchy assigns all 55 West Virginia counties to one current West Virginia parent. The issue’s older province summary has a 54+1 duplicate-name split under two former IDs. The present hierarchy has one West Virginia parent with all 55 counties; `province-assessments.csv` records the extant parent and missing old singleton ID separately. The packet cannot replay that old 54/1 partition from the retained v3 source release, so it preserves this as stale-scope uncertainty rather than guessing at the historical singleton membership.
- **Source identity and completeness:** The exact 2018 geoBoundaries source IDs for all 268 issue members were recovered from the preserved, full 3,233-feature national GeoJSON. Each member’s displayed name and current state parent resolve exactly once to a 2025 Census TIGERweb county feature and GEOID. Census feature totals by state equal the scoped counts. No source ID, current GEOID, name/state crosswalk or geometry validity failure was found. Census-reported 2026 land area shows that the largest county is no more than 4.33% of its state land total, supporting county-scale rather than province-scale role. Seven counties have reported water area greater than land area; all seven are in the under-0.95 overlap cohort. The cohort median Census water/land ratio is 0.357 versus 0.0168 for all scoped counties. This makes water depiction a plausible explanation for some differences, not a boundary adjudication. Nine 2018 geometries are multipart (maximum nine parts); three 2026 geometries are multipart (maximum three). This is an island/discontinuity screen only, not evidence that parts are missing or extra.
- **Vintage and boundaries:** The 2018 GeoBoundaries source is too old to serve as the current boundary authority. The independent comparison uses Census TIGERweb ACS 2026 Counties layer 82 (January 1, 2026 vintage) as the latest available layer on the research date; the complete ACS 2025 layer 41 inputs are also retained as the preceding-vintage comparison. All 268 old and current geometries are valid GeoJSON; their planar EPSG:4326 intersection-over-union (IoU) has a median of 0.9886. Fifty-seven coastal-state counties fall below the 0.95 screening mark between 2018 and 2026 (Florida 35, North Carolina 16, South Carolina 6; West Virginia 0). Comparison of the complete 2025 and 2026 county layers shows same GEOIDs for the scope; nine county geometries have IoU below 1.0 across those vintages, but all are at or above 0.9949. The intervening-vintage changes are recorded per unit and do not independently prove a boundary error. The lowest are Monroe, FL (0.2850) and Dare, NC (0.2985). These large differences may reflect water and shoreline treatment or vintage/generalization; the overlay does not establish which polygon is legally or operationally correct. The Census documentation explicitly says TIGER boundaries are for statistical collection/tabulation, carry no positional/attribute warranty, and are not legal land descriptions or jurisdiction determinations. Therefore no geometry is declared corrected, approved, or publication-ready.
- **Individual classifications:** `individual-assessments.csv` records every exact Atlas ID, original source ID/name/role, current parent and Census 2025/2026 GEOIDs/names, land/water area attributes, multipart counts, an individual semantic/parent finding, overlap values and boundary screen. `province-assessments.csv` records each of the five issue-summary parent rows, including the two West Virginia rows. The county role, identity and state parent are classified `justified`; whole-geometry status remains `insufficient-evidence` for all rows pending boundary adjudication. A low IoU prioritizes follow-up only; high overlap does not certify a boundary.
- **Granularity and neighboring areas:** The Atlas state/province parent is the level above the county/equivalent entities; official Census County Subdivisions are a lower level. Census county-equivalent exceptions include independent cities in Virginia, but none is one of these four states. The South Atlantic area has 587 total members, so this packet’s 268 records do not certify the combined area or neighbors.
- **License and retention:** GeoBoundaries’ retained source metadata states Public Domain and names U.S. Census Bureau MAF/TIGER as its source. The complete original 2018 GeoJSON and metadata are preserved byte-for-byte. The 2025 TIGERweb state extracts, layer metadata, and count-query responses are retained with file-byte SHA-256 values in `source-register.json`; each state extract is individually below 32 MiB. Census 2025 documentation says U.S. government works are not copyright-protected and asks for Census attribution. Official citations, source URLs, access date, license basis, hashes, and restoration paths are in the register.

## Reproduction

Run from the repository root at the pinned baseline commit and install the disclosed geometry dependency in a temporary environment:

```sh
python3 -m pip install --target /tmp/worldatlas-geo-429 shapely==2.0.7
PYTHONPATH=/tmp/worldatlas-geo-429 python3 data/regional-review/regional-review-e5d9b45b0ad3ed31/audit_scope.py
```

The script verifies the exact scope hash, complete ID matches, state counts, per-member county/state-parent crosswalk, geometry validity, and the 4326 planar IoU screen, then writes `individual-assessments.csv`, `province-assessments.csv`, and `reproduction-results.json`. The saved baseline hashes and source hashes make this reproducible without replacing any pinned or prior evidence.
