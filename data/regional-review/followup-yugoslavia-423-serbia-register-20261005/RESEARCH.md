# Serbia ADM2 register crosswalk (issue #1017)

Retrieved/reproduced 2026-10-05 from the merged #998 SORS evidence packet and #423 source ledger. The exact scope is the issue's 67 IDs. The independent reproduction joins every ID to #423's pinned source shape ID/name, then matches normalized names to SORS's 2017-12-31 workbook and the current-state workbook retrieved 2026-10-05. The latter workbook does not declare a reference/effective date. Both rosters yield one unique code/name/parent match for all 67. The 12 parent code sets agree with the current SORS primary city/municipality roster for those district codes.

This is an administrative-register roster result, not a boundary comparison. geoBoundaries metadata identifies a 2017 boundary year, 2023-01-19 source-data update and 2023-12-12 build; it does not establish legal/effective date, official source lineage, or equivalence to RGA polygons. Its 145-feature file contains the 67 subjects plus #998's disjoint 78, while the SORS national summary includes 174 local self-government units and 29 Kosovo-and-Metohija region rows; those aggregates are different classifications and must not be interpreted as territorial-status or omission conclusions.

## Role and name findings

The 2017 workbook nests administrative-city codes alongside component urban municipalities. The reproduction therefore compares city names and repeated city codes for city subjects, and primary unit names/codes for municipality subjects. Current SORS type codes classify the scoped city rows separately. Bor (70327) and Prokuplje (70998) change from municipality to city while retaining their unit codes; SORS's 2018 notice attributes this to amendments effective 20 June 2018. Pirot and Vršac had city code equal to their local-unit code in the 2017 workbook and remain `град` (city) in the current workbook. Consequently, the geoBoundaries English “Municipality” suffix is not sufficient evidence of their register role. The crosswalk contains source and Atlas labels side by side rather than silently editing IDs or names.

The scoped cities have nine current urban-municipality roster rows. These are adjacent register granularity, not additional issue subjects or independently matched Atlas features.
