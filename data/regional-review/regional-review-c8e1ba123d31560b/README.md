## Findings and sourced recommendations

1. **Sovereignty/admin distinctions:** Falkland Islands Government describes the islands as a self-governing UK Overseas Territory; [Argentina’s official policy](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/defensa/malvinas) claims the Malvinas/Falklands. [UN treaty-depositary records](https://treaties.un.org/pages/ViewDetailsIII.aspx?Temp=mtdsg3&chapter=27&clang=_en&mtdsg_no=XXVII-7&src=TREATY) preserve Argentina’s notification of its claims to Malvinas, South Georgia and South Sandwich Islands, and refer to the parties’ sovereignty dispute. This packet makes no adjudication. Existing Falklands `reference_polity_evidence` already says “Disputed” and cites UK administration/Argentine claim. The South Georgia/South Sandwich Atlas reference should be corrected by engineering to preserve the UK administration/current government reference while representing the Argentine claim and uncertainty. Do not replace the current administrative reference with a sovereignty conclusion.
2. **Current territorial function:** [GSGSSI’s official description](https://gov.gs/about-sgssi/) identifies a UK Overseas Territory, with administration from 1908 and separate BOT status from 1985; [UK SI 1985/449 §3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1985/449/pdfs/uksi_19850449_en.pdf) uses a coordinate-defined set of islands/territories to constitute that territory. Its coordinate window is legal jurisdiction context, not a polygon/coastline. [Argentina’s official claim](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/defensa/malvinas) includes both South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands. Official sources do not establish a province nested inside this named whole territory.
3. **Named island geography/completeness:** [Falkland Islands Government describes](https://www.falklands.gov.fk/our-home) East and West Falkland plus 778 smaller islands. This is a useful competing completeness statement, but the definitions and minimum mapped feature size are not tied to Natural Earth’s 1:10m shoreline. [GSGSSI’s 2023 protected-features description](https://gov.gs/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Description-of-the-protected-features.pdf) enumerates satellite islands/rocks around South Georgia and the eleven volcanic islands in the South Sandwich chain. This thematic list does not establish a complete legal polygon layer. [GSGSSI’s GIS page](https://gov.gs/gis/) says its commissioned BAS GIS was comprehensively updated in 2016–17, but no downloadable boundary dataset or reuse terms were identified from the inspected public entry point. Uncaptured coast/islet completeness remains open.
4. **Pinned source limits:** [Natural Earth’s terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) state all versions are public domain and disclaim completeness/accuracy warranties. Its 10m Admin-1 collection (commit `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19`, 2022-06-02) has no feature-level admin `type` or `type_en` for either selected record in this source revision. Feature names and ADM1 file placement do not prove a genuine “province” tier. Natural Earth states its data are public domain but disclaims completeness/accuracy warranty. Source vintage is pinned; Atlas’ “Undated modern reference” should not be read as a legal effective date.
5. **Hierarchy recommendation:** Maintain both location IDs, existing member list and frozen macro envelope. Submit a hierarchy-focused engineering proposal to determine whether each area/province tier serves a real geographic reference function. If a tier is removed, merged or renamed, retain stable IDs as the migration protocol requires and coordinate the shared parents with #401. Do not resolve the question by importing current claims into a world grid or by inferring tiers from ADM1 labels.
6. **License:** Natural Earth is Public Domain under its terms. UK legislation PDF is retained under the Open Government Licence v3. FIG, GSGSSI, Argentina and UN pages/PDF were not redistributed because an explicit reuse license was not identified in this audit; source restoration URLs and response hashes where TLS retrieval succeeded are recorded in `source/remote-response-hashes.json`. GSGSSI hosts failed local TLS chain verification, so no source-byte hash is claimed for those pages; this is an explicit provenance gap, not a reason to bypass certificate validation.

## Other scope checks

The feature is appropriately disconnected/island-based (15 Falklands components and 17 SGSSI components in both representations); this is expected archipelago structure, not proof that all islands are represented. There are no city territories or anonymous administrative remainders in the two scoped records. The area and province tiers each have one child, so oversized-group review is not applicable; their repeated same-name role remains open. The parent chain reaches the pinned South American Atlantic Islands region and Southern South America subcontinent. Neighboring granularity cannot be confirmed until a licensed source at comparable feature size is identified. The Falklands Government defines “Camp” as the countryside/outside Stanley, not a province-level unit ([Camp definition](https://www.falklands.gov.fk/22-visiting-the-falkland-islands/visiting-the-falklands-list/277-camp)).
