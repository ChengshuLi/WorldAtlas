## Fiji

The issue scope contains 14 named provinces plus Rotuma. The statistical source reports all 15 together, but the legal/administrative category for Rotuma differs as described below. The selected source tier is the province-like tier at ADM2; the adjacent source registry level is ADM1 Divisions. This tier comparison describes datasets and reporting, not a legal endorsement of the census polygons.

Fourteen current Fiji province identities and Rotuma all match the 15-feature 2007 census boundary source by native shape ID and name. The official 2017 census report's province table corroborates all labels, including Rotuma. However, the source catalog calls the boundaries those used for the 2007 Census and warns that census boundaries may differ from official administrative boundaries; repository metadata identifies a 2020 vintage. The retained downloaded geoBoundaries bytes hash to `a9cd94789cb5eb66cfbcbaf32a21bcbceba16b9a76adacba9ac675b950ca1ccd`, while the repository registry's recorded hash is `0339f90794264719b98c93a9a7c2d71fdb473c0a2f9c1e9b74ecd98a67ddb93f`. A hash/vintage mismatch remains open, so the 13 other province outlines are **insufficient-evidence** for exact current boundaries. For 13 Fiji subjects the shared helper calculated Jaccard values from 0.769 to 0.979; Macuata and Cakaudrove were skipped because their retained inputs contain coordinates slightly above 180° and the helper rejects them. These are source-comparison screens, not a threshold for declaring correctness.

## Solomon Islands / Santa Cruz

The native ID and name match the Temotu feature in the ten-unit Natural Earth ADM1 source. Solomon Islands' Ministry of Provincial Government identifies Temotu as an existing province, formerly named Santa Cruz Islands Province, consisting of multiple island chains; Santa Cruz/Nendo is only its largest island. Natural Earth is a generalized public-domain reference and cannot prove current provincial boundary placement, island inclusion or completeness. The parent relationship to the full `Santa Cruz Islands` area therefore remains unresolved; the name/history alone does not demonstrate equivalent territory. **Insufficient-evidence**; engineering should first confirm intended meaning of the geographic-area parent and then compare against an authoritative Temotu province source.

Source dataset metadata says it was updated 2024-10-28; service/layer metadata and query were retrieved 2026-10-05. The full query response was 45,177,077 bytes with SHA-256 `c5784de18d93c94383189f83d7a0e2cb2314159a3ffd18ef9a8ea9e9c6e616eb`. Because that single response exceeds the 32 MiB review bound, three lossless single-feature partitions preserve the exact original feature JSON objects; each partition is under the bound and has an independent SHA in `sources.json`. All three raw polygons are invalid with nested shells. In disposable diagnostic `make_valid` overlays against current Natural Earth, Jaccard values are 0.917 (Nord), 0.819 (Îles Loyauté), and 0.911 (Sud); symmetric difference is 816, 394, and 666 km² respectively. The official source metadata also says geometries are generalized at smaller scales. These overlays cannot say which source or repair reflects the legal boundaries.

**Engineering/source handoff:** request source-owner clarification or valid, suitably detailed province geometry from GeoReP; preserve the current public-domain Natural Earth source and official invalid GeoReP original. Compare source commune memberships with Organic Law 99-209, especially Poya's divided membership, before recommending a replacement. No silent repair or geometry swap is proposed.

## Scope completion and limits

All 25 subjects received row-level disposition. A current geography-authority source-to-geometry overlay was possible for 20 subjects; five outlines were not measured by the shared helper (three invalid official New Caledonia source features and two Fiji antimeridian-coordinate rejections). There are no repeated administrative levels within a country in this workload. No subject is an urban/city territory or anonymous remainder; 11 of 15 Fiji units, all six Vanuatu units and eight of ten Solomon Islands units are multipart in retained sources. The cohort screen finds no positive-area overlaps among source-feature pairs accepted by the shared helper, but cannot establish omitted islands or gaps against an authoritative coastline. No region-wide correctness, completeness, release compatibility, or neighboring-island coverage is certified. The available sources do not settle exact offshore/islet completeness for Fiji or Vanuatu, source-vintage validity for Fiji, boundary authority for Temotu, or legal interpretation and repair of New Caledonia's invalid polygons. Do not use administrative counts or structural controls as substitutes for those questions.
