## Neighboring granularity and source gaps

The government roster lists fifteen named islands, while Cook Islands-owned location features in the current geography parts include the eleven exact issue subjects only. No Cook Islands-owned feature named Nassau, Palmerston, Suwarrow or Takutea exists in any `data/geography/part-*.json` file at this baseline. Exact-name features exist for Palmerston in Australia and Nassau in the United States; they are unrelated homonyms and do not establish Cook Islands coverage. The OPM source treats Pukapuka/Nassau as one inhabited Island Government, but the Marae Moana and LINZ sources name separate physical islands/maps. A distinct physical entity must not be inferred to be a separate administrative government, and vice versa. The issue's exact subject list must stay unchanged; issue creators/engineering need a separate reviewed scope to decide whether to represent the four omitted named islands and where to parent them.

The current Cook Islands parents in `subject-crosswalk.csv` are single named island parents nested under either the Cook Islands area or Northern Cook Islands area. They should remain untouched here. Southern/Northern shipping clusters are current service geographies; outer-island governments are a different administrative unit; physical islands/atolls are the location meaning being reviewed. Ministry of Justice's survey boundary custody is the authoritative route to resolve any intended legal territorial extent.
