# Cook Islands island boundary source review

**Issue:** #1058. **Baseline:** `548c5f89f00271050823076a84695bb41e1b8454`, fresh `origin/main` at reservation. **Scope:** exactly 11 existing IDs in `subject-crosswalk.csv`. **Disposition:** source suitability remains unresolved for every boundary; no geometry or parent change is proposed. This packet records what the available evidence establishes, what it cannot establish, and the bounded follow-up needed to resolve it.

## Findings

- The eleven records are named physical islands or atolls. Cook Islands government sources establish island identity and distinguish outer-island governance and shipping services from island territory. The 2026 shipping policy is an operating schedule; it does not define island limits.
- Their current parent IDs and record IDs match the issue-pinned baseline. Preserve the prior #502/#512 corrections for `COK-4951` (Manuae) and `COK-4956` (Aitutaki); the 2022 Natural Earth source retains the older labels `Aitutaki` and `Palmerston` on those respective physical features. The mismatch is already reconciled in the baseline and is not a new correction proposal.
- The retained Natural Earth Admin 1 vintage is pinned to commit `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19` (2022-06-02), is public domain, and is generalized 1:10m cartography. Its `ADM1 fallback` tag and single-feature count do not establish current legal island boundaries, all dry-land components, or lagoon-water treatment.
- A component-level mismatch is visible for `COK-4950`: the Natural Earth `Atiu` row is a three-part MultiPolygon; the current `Atiu` record is one Polygon. Official Cook Islands environment sources identify Takutea as a separate, uninhabited island 22 km northwest of Atiu, while also describing its administrative/custodial association with Atiu. It has its own LINZ map sheet. The current record is therefore consistent with the *physical island* meaning of Atiu, but it does not cover every land feature administered by Atiu. Do not add Takutea to this footprint solely to match the ADM1 feature. Takutea has no location record in the current geography parts; a separate identity/coverage decision belongs in a coordinated follow-up outside this exact 11-ID issue.
- Official LINZ Pacific topographic sheets list a map for each of the eleven named islands (ten at 1:25,000; Penrhyn/Penryn at 1:50,000). LINZ publishes these map images under CC BY 4.0. The update-history page dates this Pacific topographic series to September 2014, version 1.00. These are lawful, high-detail cartographic comparators, but they are not a contemporary legally authoritative complete shoreline inventory; the GeoTIFF bytes were not retrievable here, so no map-byte hashes or pixel-by-pixel completeness results are claimed.
- The Cook Islands Geoportal states that recent LiDAR products cover 14 of 15 islands and include elevation, imagery and near-shore topobathymetry. The Prime Minister's Office says the project launched on 2026-09-25 and the data are managed through the national portal. This is the strongest current island-scale candidate located, but its dataset-level capture dates, license/reuse terms, per-island inventory and original bytes could not be verified: portal API requests returned HTTP 403 from this environment. The portal's public UI says download is available; that statement does not establish redistribution permission. No portal data were copied or used as geometry evidence.
- The Marae Moana official roster describes fifteen islands and reef-fringed landforms. Four named islands have no location feature anywhere in the current geography parts: Nassau, Palmerston, Suwarrow and Takutea. These are adjacent coverage gaps, not subjects that this bounded issue may create or repair. The exact source maps exist for those neighboring islands too. Request a separate issue to assess stable identities/parents and coordinate engineering coverage.
- The Ministry of Justice Land Administration division identifies itself as the repository and primary source for all official Cook Islands land boundaries, but its public Survey page exposes no machine-readable boundary release. No lawful current island-scale vector with declared completeness, vintage and reusable terms was found. Thus the 11 current outlines remain unverified; this packet does not certify them.

## Per-subject review and controls

`subject-crosswalk.csv` gives each exact baseline ID, unchanged parent, physical/geographic meaning, current and Natural Earth geometry form, official LINZ map sheet/scale, available government service context, and the specific unresolved boundary/completeness question. Counts and shape types are diagnostics only. `neighbor-screen.json` records the 15-island national roster and missing current IDs without expanding the accepted subject scope.

`verify.py` reads the exact issue-pinned files and retained #405 source files, checks one-to-one subject coverage, parent preservation, source crosswalk identity, current/source geometry type and component summaries, and the neighbor roster. It runs positive and negative controls; it does not calculate area/overlap, assert that a source row is correct, or establish geographic completeness. Original hashes and source restoration instructions are in `source-notes.json` and `references.md`.

## Determination and handoffs

No sourced evidence supports changing any of the 11 IDs, current parents or footprints in this packet. For atolls, the difference between dry land, fringing reef, lagoon water and a cartographic/admin envelope is unresolved; multipolygon counts and same-name matches cannot decide that distinction. The lawful source route is to request access and reuse terms for the Government's 2026 LiDAR products and/or official Ministry of Justice survey material, restore the LINZ GeoTIFF sheets under CC BY 4.0 for visual checks, then compare each named component against the exact baseline. Do not use a marine reserve, shipping group, administrative count, or coarse overlap ratio as a shoreline substitute.

Engineering/issue-creation handoffs: (1) create a separate coverage task for the four missing island records (Nassau, Palmerston, Suwarrow, Takutea), with Takutea coordinated with Atiu's physical-versus-administrative parent semantics; and (2) after current authoritative source bytes and reusable terms are available, scope a later boundary-restoration task only for subjects with a supported mismatch. These proposals do not certify the Cook Islands region or authorize geography publication/import.
