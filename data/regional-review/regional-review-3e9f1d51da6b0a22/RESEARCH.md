Chile ADM3 rows are communes; Subdere's 2023 DPA explicitly provides commune polygons and province/region codes, so the inspected source supports the commune-to-province tier for present-day administrative reference. These source-role findings answer what the polygons claim to represent; they do not prove that every polygon boundary or the six-tier atlas placement is correct.

Raw archive: 262,380,302 bytes, SHA-256 `4c8dd01ca4ca7d8b111dac78b88cc8ac64c1af7b8ebe0c85a21eaab337ae3fd3`. ## Geometry method and limits

`compare-boundaries.py` joins by normalized source unit name and parent, with only the sourced Marchigüe/Marchihue alias. Invalid shapes are repaired with Shapely `make_valid`, simplified at 0.001 degrees preserving topology, then compared with planar longitude/latitude intersection-over-union. This is useful for triage and not an equal-area, ellipsoidal, or legal line test. The IGN geometry is already generalized to 0.001 degrees and rounded to five decimal places; the Subdere DPA product is SIRGAS-Chile geographic while geoBoundaries uses WGS84. Neither coastline edge semantics nor international delimitation instruments were independently adjudicated. Do not directly transplant source polygons from these overlays.

The `geometry-summary.json` and `geometry-comparisons.jsonl` results use baseline IDs from the pinned v5 parts in commit `d6a860ae2cff80af4ca0604bbc26e90dee56fc3b`, plus the current source captures recorded above. `source-inventory.json` gives exact whole-file SHA-256 values for `world-index.json`, `hierarchy.json`, and both baseline parts; `check-packet.py` refuses mismatched files. For a full reproduction, download the exact IGN query URL in `source-inventory.json` to `source-cache/ign-argentina-south-generalized.geojson`, verify its digest, download the Subdere archive to `source-cache/subdere-dpa-2023.rar`, verify its digest, and extract it under `source-cache/` (the expected member paths are in the inventory). Then run `python3 compare-boundaries.py` with Python 3, PyShp 2.4.2, Shapely 2.0.7, and GEOS, followed by `python3 build-assessments.py` and `python3 check-packet.py`. The oversized geoBoundaries files in the inventory are original-source restoration references; the comparison reads the frozen WorldAtlas part files rather than re-importing those source files.
