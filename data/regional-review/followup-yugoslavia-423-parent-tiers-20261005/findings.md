The precise input hashes, sizes, WFS collection URLs and attribution are listed in `source-provenance.json`. The original licensed files remain in the predecessor packet; this packet references exact hashes rather than duplicating them.
- SURS says the 12 NUTS3 statistical regions group into Vzhodna (8 named regions) and Zahodna (4 named regions). Its 2019 territorial-units page supplies the explicit 8/4 lists; its 2026 update confirms the current total of 12 regions and 212 municipalities. Joining those 8/4 official names to current GURS region polygons and assigning each GURS municipality by its maximum-area NUTS3 intersection gives 148 eastern and 64 western municipalities. All 212 have at least 99.9966% of their polygon in the winning statistical-region polygon; 60 touch more than one due to small positive-area edge overlaps. This is a reproducible geometry crosswalk, not a substitute for a complete official municipality roster or a boundary-approval decision.

## Geometry results and limits

`reproduce-geometry-overlay.py` uses complete unmodified retained GeoJSON geometries, longitude/latitude coordinates, Shapely 2.1.2 and PyProj 3.7.2, projected with `always_xy=True` to EPSG:3035 (ETRS89 / LAEA Europe), then calculates union/intersection/symmetric-difference areas in square metres. A (10°,45°) forward/inverse control round-trips within 4.7e-9 degrees. It rejects invalid source polygons rather than repairing them; all inspected source features were valid. The script also reports a deliberately discordant control: an out-of-scope 740 m² second 2017 feature named Maribor overlaps only 0.00013% of the current GURS Maribor geometry. No result is a legal or geographic approval.

- For the 12 SRB districts, the issue-scoped child-union versus same-named 2017 ADM1 symmetric-difference share ranges 0.009%–3.053% of parent area. This measures internal consistency of one 2017 OSM-derived source, not current boundaries or official current roster completeness.
- The Atlas-assigned 147/61 SVN child unions differ from their 2021 NUTS2 parent shapes by 7.594%/8.957% symmetric-difference share. Dissolving current GURS NUTS3 polygons by the SURS 8/4 cohesion grouping differs from those 2021 NUTS2 source polygons by 7.613%/8.436%. These large cross-vintage/source differences prohibit reusing the old parent geometry as if it were a current municipality union. The comparison is not evidence that either dataset is legally wrong.
- The three coastal 2017 municipality polygons contain 99.57%–99.88% of their matching current GURS municipality areas but have low intersection-over-union (24.9%–37.8%). Their own parent IDs have no same-named ADM1 polygon. The current official Obalno-kraška neighboring tier contains eight GURS municipalities by maximum-area geometry crosswalk: Ankaran, Divača, Hrpelje-Kozina, Izola, Komen, Koper, Piran and Sežana. SURS places Obalno-kraška in western cohesion region Zahodna Slovenija. This establishes current municipality identity and neighboring statistical granularity; it does not certify a historical replacement geometry.
- The full 2017 SVN ADM2 collection contains 213 features while GURS has 212 current municipalities. It has two distinct `Maribor` IDs. One is the in-scope Maribor municipality; the other, `gb:SVN:ADM2:79292919B38849654156102`, is a 739.7 m² out-of-scope sliver overlapping about 0.00013% of current GURS Maribor. Do not silently assign or import that unmatched record. Route a separate source-identity review before claiming national 2017-to-current completeness.
