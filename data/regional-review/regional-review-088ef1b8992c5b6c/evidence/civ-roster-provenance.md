# Côte d’Ivoire Cavally roster provenance and access limits

The official AfDB final CGES report is titled *Projet multisectoriel d'appui au Programme social du Gouvernement*, with filename/date `..._25oct19.pdf` and a final-report date of 2019-10-25. The indexed official text at `https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/ci_projet_appui_psgouv_rapport_final_cges_25oct19.pdf` states that Cavally consists of departments Guiglo, Bloléquin, Toulépleu and Taï and 17 sous-préfectures. It groups the names by parent department as retained in `civ-cavally-2019-project-roster.csv`. Its text says five were “non encore ouvertes” at that report vintage: Bédy-Gouazon, Nizahon, Diboké, Tinhou and Nézobly. This only records 2019 operational status; it cannot be carried forward as current status.

The full original PDF was not retained. Direct binary requests to the AfDB host returned HTTP 403; the original ESA host URL returned HTTP 502 during reconstruction. No upstream whole-file SHA-256 or byte count was therefore available, and none is fabricated. Restore from the official URL above, verify the report title/final date and exact passage, then hash the original bytes before claiming current code or geometry correspondence. The local 17-row transcription SHA-256 is recorded in `source-receipts.json`; it is a derived source extract, not a hash of the upstream report.

An additional government-hosted final report was inspected from the official URL. Since no reuse license was identified, it is not redistributed. Its exact SHA-256 and byte count were recorded at inspection: `sources/official-civ/CI-SCIO-Agroforet-EIES-2026.pdf`, whole-file SHA-256 `b848d76f0508647665131d5f9a6ffa7ac2d64a42758754543e467d0d570610fd`, 10,779,871 bytes. Its printed page 81 says Cavally has four departments (Guiglo, Bloléquin, Taï and Toulépleu) and 17 sous-préfectures; page 72 places Guiglo, Bedy-Gouazon and Kaade under Guiglo, and Bloléquin and Zéaglo under Bloléquin. It is a 2026 final environmental and social report for the Scio agroforest project, whose stated scope covers project-affected departments/localities, not a national or full-Cavally boundary/codes register. Zéaglo is current government spelling support, differing from 2019 `Zéglô`; it does not resolve Bédi-Gaozon versus Bédy-Gouazon.

The official 2025 Ministry of Interior appointment document `https://interieur.gouv.ci/uploads/publications/175397417134.pdf` was reported in the prior source inspection as naming the sous-préfecture of Tinhou under Bloléquin. Direct retrieval was blocked then and no local original/hash is available in this reconstruction. Retain that limit; the 2019 “not yet open” flag for Tinhou is not presented as current.
