# Turkey ADM2 evidence packet (issue #72)

Research snapshot: 2026-10-04 (America/Los_Angeles). Exact scoped IDs are preserved in `issue-scope.json`; baseline is `9f51f7aa535b8570a20bf7e9d31d247e1d89a437`. `district-assessments.json` reports one row for every declared ID, and `reproduce.py` regenerates that inventory and its input hashes from the repository root.

## Scope and result

Issue #72 owns 226 of the geoBoundaries TUR ADM2 features in 17 named province scopes under the Turkey area. Every subject has exactly one pinned geoBoundaries ADM2 feature and one baseline atlas feature; all 226 atlas names equal the source `shapeName`; all 226 atlas `parent_id` values point to one of the 17 declared province scope IDs. These are identity and declared-parent-chain checks. They do not establish each district's legal status, boundary accuracy, completeness, or correct province placement.

The prior whole-country `granularity-review-evidence.json` screening includes three scoped weak source-parent overlap flags: Kozlu (71.583%), Kilimli (74.798%), and Ereğli (70.830%). It also includes multipart-footprint flags for Ayaş (3 components) and Güdül (2). These are inherited screening observations, not fresh source-backed correction findings. The weak-overlap records need comparison with contemporaneous district/province registers and boundary evidence; multiparts require inspection to distinguish legitimate islands/territorial components from data artifacts. All five remain unresolved. The exact IDs, parents, names, flag values, and source feature IDs are in the assessments table.

## Territorial meaning and hierarchy

The issue's source is geoBoundaries gbOpen TUR ADM2, canonical label “Districts,” derived from OpenStreetMap / OSM Boundaries. Its source metadata says reference year 2021, source data update 2023-01-19, build 2023-12-12, and 999 source units. The upstream source describes an administrative-level dataset; the metadata and name match alone cannot establish that all 226 geometries represent the legally applicable Turkish districts at the issue's 2021 reference date, nor does the source metadata give each ADM2's province parent. Atlas parent IDs are recorded as hierarchy references; they have not been certified here against the Turkish legal register.

The Turkish Ministry of Interior's current governor-office directory describes 81 provinces and 922 districts. TurkStat's *Turkey in Statistics 2021* table also reports 81 provinces and 922 districts and footnotes that province centers (central districts) are excluded from its district count. Adding the 51 province centers explains 51 of the difference between TurkStat's 973 center-inclusive units and geoBoundaries' 999 units; the remaining 26 feature-count difference is unresolved. Neither count is a territorial correctness check, and the counts may describe different administrative conventions/vintages.

The Ministry of Interior's 2021 Address Based Population Registration System bulletin states that population, municipality, district, village and neighborhood observations reflect the National Address Database as of 2021-12-31. This anchors an official statistical reference date but does not itself establish legal boundaries or the ADM2 parent mapping. The 2021 official district roster/parent crosswalk was not recovered in a form that permits a full row-by-row validation in this packet.

## Methods and remaining work

`reproduce.py` verifies exact subject-set presence in the pinned source and baseline atlas files, unique mapping, name equality, parent membership in the declared issue scope, and inherited screening flags; it writes stable whole-file hashes for the source files and baseline inputs. It does not download data, repair geometry, infer legal parentage, or certify spatial correctness. A meaningful next reproduction is a source-backed 2021 row-level district-to-province crosswalk and polygon comparison for the five flagged subjects, with all discrepancies assessed; acquire an official roster/parent record or document the unavailable authoritative evidence. The national 26-unit count residual also needs a source-ID/name crosswalk and a convention/vintage explanation. Any geometric mismatch that affects neighboring scopes needs a coordinated correction proposal, not a unilateral change here.

No geographic approval, full Turkey/region certificate, production migration, publication, historical import authorization, or boundary correction is claimed. The packet supports a bounded source identity and declared-scope audit with unresolved territorial meaning, parent overlap, source-vintage and country-completeness questions.
