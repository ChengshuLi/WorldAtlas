## Source evidence and findings

### Cabinda, Angola — 4/4 correction-needed

The pinned source is geoBoundaries gbHumanitarian AGO ADM2, vintage 2018, CC BY 3.0 IGO. Its four scoped polygons and source IDs reproduce exactly from the immutable URL in `audit-summary.json`; all four source names match the atlas. The official Gazette's [Law 14/24, 5 September 2024](https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Lei_14_24_de_5_de_Setembro_Cabinda_5c86d750e1.pdf) repeals the prior political-administrative division (opening page), and Article 5 (Gazette page 9803 / PDF page 5) lists ten Cabinda municipalities: Cabinda, Cacongo, Buco Zau, Belize, Miconje, Massabi, Necuto, Tando Zinze, Liambo, and Ngoio. Article 5 describes the province's limits; Articles 6 onward describe municipal limits; Article 3 says the provincial summary tables/maps are integral annexes. The old four-polygon ADM2 roster therefore cannot substantiate current complete Cabinda coverage. The current law provides municipality names and boundary descriptions, but legal text and maps have not yet been transcribed into geometries here.

The law PDF was retrieved and SHA-256 checked (`dfaab2fa7059d8447aee3cab492deb94793971883b6a4998dc5353bf3d6e6e6a`). No open reuse license was found, so the evidence packet gives a restoration URL/hash and does not redistribute it. Do not reuse its map images or digitize boundaries until copyright/reuse authority and suitable georeferencing are established. The geoBoundaries source has CC BY 3.0 IGO; retain attribution if it is later used.

### Cameroon — 4 correction-needed parent assignments; 222 insufficient-evidence units

The pinned source is geoBoundaries gbOpen CMR ADM3, vintage 2017, ODbL 1.0. All 226 scoped feature IDs are in that file; it contains 360 ADM3 features overall. The exact old bytes are identified by SHA-256 in `audit-summary.json`; the file is not copied into the packet and can be restored from the pinned URL. Within this scope, the source has 221 polygons and five multipolygons; the atlas has 218 polygons and eight multipolygons. Five exact component counts differ: Campo 1 source part/2 atlas parts; Isanguele 7/4; Kombo-Abedimo 3/2; Banyo 1/2; Akwaya 1/4. These are island/disconnection review triggers, not proof that components are wrong or missing. All five comparisons and IDs are in `audit-summary.json`; individual source and atlas counts are in the CSV. The current atlas representation has fewer coordinate vertices than the corresponding source bytes (9,732 versus 127,881 across scope). It is therefore a generalized derivative; its vertex structure cannot be treated as a direct boundary-by-boundary reproduction. This count comparison does not measure positional error or accuracy.

The Cameroon Ministry of Territorial Administration ([MINAT, Services Locaux](https://minat.gov.cm/presentation/services-locaux/)) describes 10 regions, 58 departments, and 360 arrondissements and assigns those administrative roles to governors, prefects, and sub-prefects. A further parent crosswalk finding is correction-needed: four Yaoundé arrondissement IDs currently point into neighboring departments. MINDDEVEL's [Mfoundi department page](https://www.minddevel.gov.cm/mfoundi/) lists mayor-election instruments for Yaoundé I–VII under Mfoundi (2020 records); the exact mismatches are Yaoundé I, II, and VII currently parented to Lékié, and Yaoundé V currently parented to Mefou-et-Afamba. The four exact IDs and proposed Mfoundi parent ID are in `audit-summary.json` and `unit-review.csv`. This is a sourced parent-correction proposal; it does not establish those polygons' boundary coordinates or current footprint. An engineering/source follow-up must reconcile the parent and examine source-vs-official geometry before any change.

The UN [SALB Cameroon record](https://salb.un.org/en/data/cmr) identifies the national geospatial authority as the Institut National de Cartographie and lists a validated polygon layer with temporal validity to 2024-08-07. SALB is explicitly second administrative level, so it does not verify the claimed third-level arrondissement polygons. The [SALB data specification](https://salb.un.org/sites/default/files/wysiwyg_uploads/docs_uploads/SALB_DataSpecifications.pdf) also documents attributes only through ADM2. Its [Terms of Use](https://salb.un.org/sites/default/files/wysiwyg_uploads/docs_uploads/TermsOfUseSALB2021.pdf) restrict use to non-commercial purposes and prohibit changes to contributed geometry/content without contributor consent; no SALB geometry is redistributed here.

National count agreement (360 in MINAT and 360 in the 2017 source) is only a completeness cross-check at the total-count level. Accessible authoritative evidence did not provide an up-to-date complete arrondissement-to-department legal roster and boundary geometry with cleared public reuse terms. The four Yaoundé parent mismatches are classified `correction-needed`; the other 222 Cameroon unit rows are `insufficient-evidence`: a sourced geometry role/tier and matching source ID do not prove the individual boundary, name, parent, island completeness, or current status. The pinned hierarchy's source-overlap field is below 0.80 for 24 units; the exact ID/parent/overlap list is in `audit-summary.json`. Treat that threshold only as a weak-parent inspection signal, not as a legal boundary test. The four Yaoundé records are within those 24; 20 other signals remain unresolved without suitable administrative boundary evidence.

Thirty-six Cameroon source `shapeName` values are mojibake relative to the atlas names. For every mismatch, a Latin-1-to-UTF-8 decoding roundtrip equals the atlas spelling (for example source `NgambÃ©` → `Ngambé`). This is evidence of a reproducible encoding defect in the pinned source text, not an independent official spelling authority. Preserve original bytes and flag the text field for source-level investigation; do not rewrite source names or infer boundary changes from this.

## Per-subject and structural coverage

- All 230 in-scope IDs were found in fresh-main data and all 230 matched an original feature ID in the pinned source files.
- Four Angola features are polygons. Cameroon source/atlas geometry classes and component differences are recorded separately in the output; see the five component discrepancies above. This avoids treating source-to-atlas topology changes as a verified boundary correction.
- The aggregate CSV covers Cabinda and all 39 Cameroon province/department portions included by the issue. It does not include or make claims about the other 134 Cameroon units, which are scoped to #461.
- The 226 Cameroon features all come from one pinned ADM3 layer, so no mixed source levels appear in this portion. Named urban subdivisions at this tier include Yaoundé I–VII, Douala I–VI, Limbé I–III, and Bafoussam I–III; all have individual CSV rows. The Yaoundé source-parent discrepancy shows why matching tier labels and neighboring urban granularity do not validate hierarchy by themselves.
- A spherical area screening estimate ranks Yoko as the largest Cameroon scoped source polygon at about 15,758 km²; the next nine are enumerated in `audit-summary.json`. This is an outlier-review cue for oversized lower-level districts, not a determination that the source is wrong. The reproduction script's spherical ring calculation is a size-ranking estimate, not survey area or an accuracy metric; the current source and official neighboring boundaries still need comparison.
- No scoped display name contains `unknown`, `unnamed`, `other`, or `remainder`. This label screen cannot rule out omitted territory or an improperly generalized polygon. No source IDs, names, geometries, parents, hierarchy, footprints, or release pins were modified.
- `reproduce-review.py` fetches the three original byte sources into a temporary directory, checks SHA-256, reconstructs all scoped ID/name/parent/geometry rows from current main, and writes the CSV/JSON results. The GeoBoundaries files carry the source licenses above; the law is restoration-only because reuse rights are unclear.
- Government web pages and PDFs without an explicit open reuse grant are referenced with original response/file SHA-256 values and restoration URLs; no page/PDF copies are committed. Exact pinned GeoBoundaries source bytes are also restored temporarily for audit and removed after hashing; no raw GeoBoundaries files are committed.
