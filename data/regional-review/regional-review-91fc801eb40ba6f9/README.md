## Scope and result

This is a partial Nigeria area packet: all 210 exact issue subjects in nine full province scopes, within an area of 774 locations. Each assigned subject has an individual source, role, parent-chain, source-vintage overlap, scale, settlement and geometry-component assessment in `assessment.csv` and the linked diagnostic tables. Every outcome remains **insufficient evidence**; no membership transfer, boundary correction, administrative promotion, political ownership, region approval or import authority is inferred.

The pinned 2022 geoBoundaries layer describes 774 Nigerian Local Government Areas (LGAs); the current OCHA COD-AB layer has 774 ADM2 units and 37 ADM1 units. The 1,305 positive-area intersections between the pinned layer and the current layer are recorded as source-vintage candidates, not selected transfers. Every retained current polygon is inventoried, including validity, components, code, parent and date fields. Nine province scopes are complete: Enugu (17), Abia (17), Katsina (34), Kaduna (23), Akwa Ibom (31), Ebonyi (13), Nasarawa (13), Kano (44), and Cross River (18).

The all-Nigeria remainder is accounted for in `area-partition-inventory.csv`, which identifies each location's assigned issue and parent. The sibling issue scopes are preserved verbatim with hashes in `sibling-area-partition.json`. Their Nigeria assignments partition 774 locations without gaps or overlaps: #475 (158), #476 (227), #477 (210), and #478 (179). Rows owned by other packets are inventory/ownership references only; this packet does not assess their evidence.

208 parent screens have one current parent label matching the published parent name. Two duplicate-name cases remain unresolved, despite candidate intersections: Obi (`gb:NGA:ADM2:59680162B90915593246984`) has Benue and Nasarawa candidates; Nasarawa (`gb:NGA:ADM2:59680162B9133954487337`) has Kano and Nasarawa candidates. These are not resolved by choosing the largest intersection. Across 37 current ADM1 code-linked comparisons and four Nigeria neighboring-country edges, source geometry screens show differences at their respective vintages; their small positive overlaps with Chad/Niger are 0.000003 and 0.000006 km² and are not evidence of an actionable shared-boundary inconsistency at the recorded precision. They do not establish legal parent purpose, physical accuracy, sovereignty, or a shared-boundary change. Any material inter-region inconsistency must be documented and coordinated with affected neighbors before proposing a shared-boundary correction.

The 60,246 retained WCA settlement observations have no exact current ADM2-code matches in this subset; 12,062 observations join to assigned candidates through normalized ADM2+ADM1 labels, a weaker code-vintage fallback, for 208 of the 210 locations. There are 223 fallback-joined points outside candidate polygons in six locations: Kajuru (`gb:NGA:ADM2:59680162B79502957035648`, 95), Sandamu (`gb:NGA:ADM2:59680162B54666922464494`, 50), Safana (`gb:NGA:ADM2:59680162B21692666530817`, 37), Kauru (`gb:NGA:ADM2:59680162B79526209894783`, 35), Mashi (`gb:NGA:ADM2:59680162B19433829654146`, 3), and Boki (`gb:NGA:ADM2:59680162B37931278966472`, 3). The point observations, names, fallback join, candidate containment, and source modification dates are retained row by row in `settlement-point-anomalies.csv`. These are unresolved source-vintage/geometry candidates, not transfers. A bounded source-code/coordinate review should resolve these six clusters before using the points to argue for location changes.

`city-name-spatial-screen.csv` has 185 COD-AB capital-point/name candidates (181 point geometries fall inside a candidate polygon) and 249 WCA place-name-token rows. All rows are point/name screens; even an in-polygon capital point does not define its settlement footprint. `source-vintage-crosswalk.csv` preserves all 1,305 positive intersections, including the many small overlaps created by adjacent units; the candidate matching method and coverage fields are per pair, so the full inventory must be read instead of interpreting the distribution of all intersection pairs as one selected match per location.

## Sources and retained material

`acquisition.json` records canonical catalog/download URLs, source roles and vintages, retrieval dates/metadata vintages, licenses, retained file paths, compressed and restored byte counts and hashes, plus restoration instructions. Original source bytes are preserved under `sources/`; generated tables and their byte/hash accounting are listed separately in `generated-data-accounting.json`.

- **geoBoundaries NGA ADM2, 2022**, role *Local Government Areas*, source identified by the catalog as GRID3; CC BY 4.0. The pinned source commit is `9469f09592ced973a3448cf66b6100b741b64c0d`. Its 774-feature GeoJSON and contemporaneous retained API metadata are preserved with gzip and restored hashes in `acquisition.json`. The current API endpoint is not an immutable metadata snapshot for that old release; the issue-declared vintage and license are kept distinct from current API metadata.
- **OCHA HDX Nigeria COD-AB**, CC BY-IGO, canonical catalog `https://data.humdata.org/api/3/action/package_show?id=cod-ab-nga`; retained package metadata says it was reviewed 2025-10-30, humanitarian-valid from 2019-04-17, and that the contributed boundaries were created/last edited 1996-10-01. Resource bytes and exact archive hashes are recorded in `acquisition.json`. ADM3 has only 714 wards and is explicitly partial; this packet does not treat it as comprehensive settlement evidence.
- **OCHA WCA settlement points**, CC BY, retained region subset and inherited source register with original publication/update and hash records. The subset contains 60,246 Nigeria point records. 60,243 geometries match the source's LAT/LONG fields under the recorded axis check. These are dated point observations, not a complete settlement inventory or urban footprint. The code-vintage and normalized-name-plus-parent joins remain distinguished; outlier rows do not imply reassignment.
- **OCHA COD-AB administrative capitals**, retained within the same COD-AB source archive. The capital names/coordinates are point/name candidates only. They cannot establish municipal, urban or metropolitan extent. The source's ADM2 code vintage differs from current polygon codes for some records, so fallback joins require normalized ADM2 and ADM1 labels together.
- **National Nigeria settlements catalog**, metadata only; the catalog reports license `Other`. Its settlement rows are neither retained nor used. **GRID3 Nigeria Settlement Extents v4.1**, CC BY-SA per retained catalog metadata modified 2026-10-01, has a 2,136,305,664-byte GeoPackage and is not retained or analyzed here; its canonical catalog and restoration URL are recorded in the metadata. See `sources/access-limitations.json`.
- **NOAA GSHHG** is needed for independent physical coastline, land-fragment and named-island screening. The inherited source-access record says NOAA's information page was reachable but its linked latest-data directory/readme returned HTTP 404. No archive bytes or verified license are available in that record. The exact prior record and bounded restoration/review instructions are retained in `sources/access-limitations.json`. Administrative polygons cannot close this gap.

## Methods, uncertainty, and next work

`build_assessment.py` maps each of the 210 assigned source IDs to its pinned feature, preserves all positive-area legacy/current intersections, retains duplicate-name candidates, and reports their complete five-level parent chains. It inventories all 774 current COD-AB units and geometry components, compares every assigned province-scale candidate and the 37 current ADM1 cross-source shapes, checks WCA point coordinates and containment, includes capital-point and WCA place-name evidence, and records the four same-source country-neighbor edge screens. `verify.py` asserts the ancestor snapshot hashes and subject/sibling partitions, retained-source and generated-output hashes, row coverage, positive and negative controls, and offers `--rebuild` for two-run deterministic reproduction.

The full builder completed once in this container after streaming the retained WCA file and dropping temporary spatial indexes before output serialization. A subsequent two-run reproduction attempt was interrupted by the shared host memory limit (the cgroup was near its 17.2 GB ceiling and the process was OOM-killed before rebuilding). The single build's outputs pass `verify.py`; deterministic two-run reproduction remains pending a run with sufficient shared execution memory and must not be reported as passed yet.

Provisional source observations and point screens cannot account for every settlement, independent physical land/island geometry, all historical administrative changes, or the legal/purpose meaning of each administrative tier. Three bounded, dependent follow-ups have been created and are blocked until #477 completes: [#755](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/755) restores/verifies NOAA/GSHHG or a NOAA-documented mirror and screens physical fragments/islands for these 210 subjects; [#756](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/756) reviews the WCA code/coordinate vintage for the six exact point clusters before any reassignment proposal; and [#757](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/757) resolves the named Obi/Nasarawa candidate pairs against authoritative state/LGA records and full geometry. Each has its own owned path and reservation; no child is ready or claimed. No neighboring region boundary is altered in this packet. EU5 counts are context only and are not quotas; Antarctica is excluded.

This research packet proposes evidence for engineering review. Geography remains bottom-up: assigned locations determine parent footprints. The top-down order is only a review sequence. Engineering must integrate any accepted corrections, validate and publish the complete regional branch, and issue the applicable regional certificate before location-attribute imports are eligible.
