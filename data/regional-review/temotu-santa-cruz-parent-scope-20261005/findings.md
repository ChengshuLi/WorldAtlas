# Findings and handoff

## Decision

**Classification: unresolved parent scope; insufficient evidence for a whole-Temotu parent correction.** The current Atlas area edge is a real assignment in the pinned release, but no inspected source defines the intended perimeter and complete membership of the Atlas “Santa Cruz Islands” area. Preserve the present subject, geometry, IDs, and parent while the convention is unresolved. This is not an approval of the existing edge.

## Evidence

1. **Administrative meaning.** The Solomon Islands Provincial Government Act 1997, Schedule 1, calls Temotu a province and describes its included islands within a boundary from 9°S/164°E east to 170°20′E, south to 12°30′S, and west to 164°E, with the Makira Ulawa common boundary. Section 3 says the provinces comprise the islands named in Schedule 1 and provides seaward extents. This is an official legal administrative description, not a surveyed island polygon; the Ministry cautions that its posted consolidated copy should be checked with the Attorney General for conclusive legal advice.
2. **Current administrative context.** MPGIS’s Temotu page is dated 4 September 2018. It says the province was formerly called Santa Cruz Islands Province, comprises two scattered island chains, and Santa Cruz/Nendo is its largest island. The historical name is evidence of administrative continuity, not proof of present geographic equivalence.
3. **Different geographic scopes.** Solomon Islands subsidiary legislation LN 40/1979 separately lists “Santa Cruz Islands” and “Duff Islands” as archipelagos for the Delimitation of Marine Waters Act. ICOMOS’s Pacific cultural-landscape study describes a broader “Reef Santa Cruz Islands” group, including Nendö, Utupua, Vanikoro, Reef Islands, and Duff/Taumako, and describes Tikopia as nearby and separately profiled. The statutory group and cultural landscape have different purposes and scopes; neither defines the Atlas area.
4. **Atlas assignment and source vintage.** At baseline `e760d68ff48a679b08f6fbf0581d1035de35105d`, the target location is Temotu ADM1, and its metadata names area code `SCZ`, a 0.44143 area-overlap value, and a 0.997522 administrative hierarchy overlap. The source register describes Solomon Islands ADM1 as a Natural Earth-derived, public-domain 2021 reference with ten features; the source product has nine province-named features plus `Capital Territory (Honiara)`. Its retained source bytes hash to `bd22f4cdce1c19066950b8c384c4d484a21a0dcaee5f207e363179f68c58c69d` and are retained in the parent #454 packet. The source is generalized, not a legal provincial boundary. The overlap fields document how the current relationship was screened; they do not establish geographic truth.
5. **Completeness and neighboring scale.** The Ministry’s official provincial-government list identifies nine provinces and treats Honiara separately. The geoBoundaries/Natural Earth ADM1 roster’s tenth feature is a capital territory, not a tenth province. That distinction matters when interpreting the source’s generic ADM1 / canonical Province metadata. Available evidence does not establish island-by-island completeness for Temotu’s full statutory extent or the Atlas area.

## Controls and limits

- Positive scoped control: the immutable world index finds the exact issue ID once, in part 22; the current feature name and retained source identity both say Temotu. The release membership ledger links that one location to the named Temotu province and Santa Cruz Islands area.
- Negative scoped control: a deliberately altered, out-of-scope ID is rejected by the complete indexed-subject reader. The script does not accept a name-only match as identity.
- Semantic negative control: the decision remains unresolved when the only link is the historical provincial name, and also when an administrative province boundary exists but the Atlas area extent is undefined.
- No area, distance, or boundary-validity metric was computed. No administrative count or overlay is treated as proof of island membership. The direct Temotu geometry remains the generalized Natural Earth-derived reference; this packet neither validates its legal boundary nor partitions it.
- MPGIS page retrieval bytes changed between 5 and 8 October 2026 while the response length stayed 141,006 bytes; both hashes are preserved in acquisition metadata, and no page bytes are redistributed. Terms for the ministry, parliament, FAOLEX, and UNESCO-hosted materials are not established here. The Parliament plan’s searchable island roster is an unverified contextual lead; its 14.7 MB PDF was hashed transiently but the local environment had no PDF text extractor.
- The FAOLEX copy of the 1979 subsidiary instrument is a restoration location. Its text was inspected, but the copy was not authenticated against the Gazette. The MPGIS consolidated 1997 Act copy itself warns about conclusive legal reliance; current amendment status must be confirmed with the Attorney General if a legal correction depends on it.

## Handoff

Geography integration/Main owns the remaining semantic choice: document the intended scope and island membership of the Atlas Santa Cruz Islands area, including whether Duff/Taumako and other Temotu groups belong. If the chosen area deliberately excludes parts of a province, create a separate, sourced geographic-portion design with an explicit crosswalk. Engineering owns implementation only after that decision and a new scoped authorization. No data correction is proposed by this packet.
