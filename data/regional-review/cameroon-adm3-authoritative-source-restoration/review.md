# Cameroon ADM3 authoritative-source restoration research

**Issue:** #897, exactly 226 existing `gb:CMR:ADM3` subjects. **Retrieved:** 2026-10-05. **Scope:** research and engineering handoffs only; this packet does not alter IDs, geometry, hierarchy, release pins, or production data.

## Finding

No accessible current national legal ADM3 boundary download was located. The strongest lawful operational reference located is OCHA’s Cameroon COD-AB, sourced to the Institut National de Cartographie (INC). HDX metadata retrieved 2026-10-05 marks the package fresh, gives an annual update frequency and due date 2026-10-30, and reports dataset coverage through 2025-10-30; it records an accuracy/completeness review on 2025-10-30 and humanitarian validity since 2019-01-04. This supports describing it as the latest published operational reference found at retrieval, while the end of its declared coverage and older source boundary vintage remain explicit currency limits. It contains 10 ADM1 regions, 58 ADM2 departments, and 360 ADM3 arrondissements, with unique ADM3 P-codes and an ADM2 parent code on every scoped feature. Its metadata gives the source boundary creation/last edit date as 1987-08-22. OCHA describes CODs as authoritative reference datasets for humanitarian operations and decision-making. That status supports operational use and lawful source comparison, not a claim of current national legal boundary authority. The underlying boundary vintage remains old.

The retained exact HDX API metadata response and original resource ZIP are in `source/`. Metadata declares CC BY-IGO 3.0. The ZIP contains edge-matched ADM3 and matching ADM1/ADM2 layers; the reproducible analysis used `cmr_admin3_em.geojson`, with the neighboring tiers inspected for granularity. The separate INC-attributed WRI Forest Atlas layer has 360 returned features, but explicitly says its global view is not official, has no legal validation process, and uses 1:200,000 topographic mapping. Its metadata description claims 359 arrondissements, every `code_arr` is null, and it directly joins 154/226 scoped subjects. It is retained only as a comparison.

UN SALB’s validated Cameroon polygons extend through 2024-08-07 but stop at ADM2 and carry noncommercial/no-unapproved-modification terms. MINAT’s South roster and MINDDEVEL/Prime Minister records provide useful current name/parent roster evidence, but not national georeferenced ADM3 boundaries. The INC mandate page confirms institutional responsibility for mapping and administrative delimitation without exposing a current ADM3 release.

## Subject-level reproduction

`reproduce.py` checks the issue’s exact subject ID set against the existing 226 Cameroon rows in #460’s reviewed `unit-review.csv`, then reads the pinned 2017 geoBoundaries feature geometries and every OCHA ADM3 polygon. It first counts Unicode-normalized name+current-parent joins. It then uses Shapely STRtree to find intersecting spatial candidates and PyProj `Geod(ellps="WGS84")` to calculate geodesic polygon area, intersection-over-union, and each side’s coverage. Axis order is x=longitude, y=latitude in EPSG:4326. Invalid input geometry is made valid in memory only for measuring; original files remain byte-preserved. Positive identical-square and negative disjoint-square controls passed. Two full runs produced identical `candidate-crosswalk.json` SHA-256 `bed3c32dc5280767fcaa34add87f1ae27973385618f3aa64dae0b6fecc3ddaa4.

All 226 subjects received a unique best intersecting OCHA spatial candidate with at least 0.10 IoU separation over the runner-up. This is a reproducible candidate crosswalk, not evidence that source polygons are legally identical. 157 subjects have a direct normalized name+current-parent match; the remaining rows require map/roster identity review. OCHA’s 360 unique P-codes and 58 ADM2 parent codes show source-file completeness at the stated operational level, but no independent legal roster proves the country’s current lawful boundary completeness.

The pinned 2017 geometry versus OCHA candidate IoU is at least 0.95 for 217 subjects. Nine fall below that threshold: Douala IV (0.9117), Limbé III (0.9401), Bamusso (0.9472), Douala I (0.8334), Kombo-Itindi (0.8558), Isanguele (0.9100), Kombo-Abedimo (0.9328), Douala VI (0.9421), and Idabato (0.8175). These differences may reflect source vintage, generalization, islands, or real boundary changes; this evidence does not decide which. They need targeted primary map/decree review. Candidate and historical component counts are recorded per subject, including the seven-component Isanguele historical geometry matched to a one-component OCHA candidate.

Thirty-six labels in the historical source differ from Atlas labels and have a reproducible double-encoding roundtrip signature. Nineteen directly match OCHA after normalized name+parent comparison. The other 17 are still individually represented with spatial candidates; a geometry match alone does not certify the label mapping.

Eleven best spatial candidates have parent labels that differ from the Atlas parent label. Six are spelling variants for Nyong-et-So’o (accent/apostrophe rendering); these should be normalized as candidate label variants pending source roster confirmation. Four Yaoundé commune-arrondissements (I, II, V, VII) are listed under Mfoundi in official MINDDEVEL mayoral-order records while the Atlas parents are Lékié or Mefou-et-Afamba. Ebolowa II is listed under Mvila by MINAT and official decrees while Atlas currently assigns Sud. `engineering-handoff.json` proposes those five parent-only follow-ups, preserving IDs and polygons. The six spelling cases remain research notes, not hierarchy changes.

## Territorial meaning, neighboring granularity, and uncertainty

“Arrondissement” here is treated as the Cameroon ADM3 operational unit, beneath a department (ADM2) and region (ADM1), as modeled by the source. OCHA’s edge-matched ADM1/ADM2/ADM3 layers provide neighboring-tier context and consistent source P-codes. The WRI comparison has no feature codes and an internally inconsistent advertised count. No coast/island or disconnected-piece legal roster was found that would independently establish completeness; component counts and low-overlap subjects are exposed in the crosswalk rather than explained away. The packet therefore cannot certify legal territorial meaning or prove all island/disconnected components current.

No data license was assumed for official pages without explicit terms. No SALB polygon was retained. Exact hashes and retrieval dates are in `source-inventory.json` and `evidence-quality.json`; the full original OCHA and WRI inputs are retained in `source/`.

## Engineering handoff and status

Five parent-only proposals are listed in `engineering-handoff.json`; implementation should be a separate scoped engineering task that validates them against the active hierarchy and parent/neighbor rules. No geometry correction is proposed because the source vintage/legal authority gap and nine overlap differences remain unresolved.

Research is partial for the issue’s full acceptance: a lawful operational dataset and per-subject candidate comparison are now reproducible, but a current legal ADM3 boundary roster, the nine lower-overlap cases, 17 encoded-name mappings, and legal disconnected-piece completeness remain unresolved. This packet is not regional approval or an import/publication authorization.
