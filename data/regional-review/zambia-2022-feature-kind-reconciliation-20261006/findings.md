## Supported findings

- The retained OSG/GRID3 layer is a polygon feature layer identified as `Zambia districts 2022`; its item description says national, 10 provincial, and 116 district boundaries and cites Office of the Surveyor General endorsement. The item is dated as a 2022 layer, created 2024-02-16 and modified 2024-03-26. The live FeatureServer reports `dataLastEditDate` 2024-02-16. Item narrative dates are internally mixed: the description says district narratives through 2017; `licenseInfo` says through 2017 and 2021. This does not prove the boundary data are current after the 2022 source vintage.
- The item states CC BY 4.0 and names Zambia Government institutions and GRID3 as contributors. The item text has a malformed license link (`.../by/4.0and`); attribution is retained, and the canonical CC BY 4.0 text is recorded in `SOURCES.md`. The upstream item’s license assertion was inspected; no independent rights audit was made.
- The `FEATURE_TY` field is nullable NVARCHAR(15) with `domain: null`, no default, and no explanatory field description. Current item resources list zero resources. The service has one polygon feature layer and no tables. Searches of official ZamStats/government sources for the field name and observed strings found no codebook. Thus the field's use as administrative unit kind, settlement/seat role, cartographic label, or another classification remains unresolved.
- The source contains exactly 116 unique district names and exactly the four literal categories: District Town (100), Provincial Town (11), City (4), and District Tiwn (1). `feature-type-crosswalk.csv` identifies every record separately and preserves its exact source spelling and category. The 2020 geoBoundaries source has 116 unique names and native IDs; the normalized-name join is bijective and all IDs agree with the issue's 116 subject IDs. This is identity crosswalk evidence, not authority or geometry validation.
- `District Tiwn` is the single value on Shiwang'Andu. Its spelling is strongly suggestive of a source typo for “District Town”; no author correction, codebook, or service history was obtained to prove the intended correction or category semantics. Leave the source literal unchanged.
- `Area_km` is a nullable floating-point field with no coded domain or method description. Neither the live schema nor item metadata documents its derivation, measurement units, precision, coordinate system, or whether it is computed from polygon geometry. Keep every value unvalidated; do not use sums, rankings, or the name of the field as measured geographic area.
- geoBoundaries records all 116 IDs as ADM2 and metadata describes the canonical units as districts (boundary year 2020; source-data update date 2023-01-19). ZamStats' 2022 Census National Analytical Report supports 10 provinces and 116 districts at census date. These references are consistent with the OSG layer's district-boundary role. They do not explain `FEATURE_TY` or establish legal currentness after the relevant vintages.
- The 2022 source and geoBoundaries reference are both coarse district-level neighboring granularity for this review. No higher/lower administrative units, district adjacency, legal district roster after 2022, or linework comparison were audited.
