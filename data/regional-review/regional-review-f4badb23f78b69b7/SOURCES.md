## Authoritative and contextual research

- **INE, Mozambique 2017 Census microdata catalog**: https://mozdata.ine.gov.mz/index.php/catalog/24 . INE says 2017 had 161 districts and Maputo City includes seven municipal districts. The catalog identifies INE as producer. This is roster/count context, not boundary geometry.
- **INE, Censo 2027 cartography announcement (2026-03-30)**: https://ine.gov.mz/en/b/ine-avanca-com-a-cartografia-censitaria-para-o-censo-2027 . Search-result text says the current cartographic effort covers 161 districts. Page fetch timed out during this review; retain as a source pointer and verify directly before using it to approve a correction.
- **INE/UNOCHA, “MOZAMBIQUE: Admin Level 2 Boundaries” metadata**: https://services.arcgis.com/iQ1dY19aHwbSDYIF/arcgis/rest/services/Mozambique/FeatureServer/4/metadata?f=html&format=default . Created 2019-04-02, published 2019-06-07; source owner INE, provider OCHA; geometry-only quality; lineage states an Admin-2 Pemba polygon was merged into Metuge on IMO instruction. Metadata links to COD/FOD Registry terms and does not establish a general redistribution grant. The live service source remains subject to INE's separate ownership condition below.
- **INE/Mozambique GIS, 2017 “Distritos” layer**: https://www.mozgis.gov.mz/server/rest/services/Data/UnidadesAdministrativasCenso2017/MapServer/2 . It says its base remains in correction/harmonization and “belongs exclusively to INE.” No live geometry was downloaded, retained, or redistributed for this packet. To recheck, inspect layer metadata and query the official endpoint under INE's terms; do not copy its features into this repository absent permission.
- **INE Cabo Delgado 2024 Statistical Yearbook**: https://www.ine.gov.mz/documents/20119/334647/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_PROVINCIAL_CABO_DELGADO_2025.pdf/83a3f6a4-06f4-87b0-1c03-d46969945a89?download=true&t=1753700111320&version=1.0 . The 2024 territorial table lists Cidade de Pemba and Metuge separately among the province's districts. This helps describe current units but does not reconcile the 2019 lineage note or supply a redistributable boundary source.
- **INE Nampula 2024 Statistical Yearbook**, territorial table 1.2.1: https://www.ine.gov.mz/documents/20119/334650/Anu%C3%A1rio%20Estat%C3%ADstico%20Prov%C3%ADncia%20de%20Nampula%202024.pdf/d8f2bf53-fbe9-af23-358f-148c4d184564?download=true&t=1751285084904&version=1.0 . The table lists Cidade de Nampula alongside district names and identifies lower administrative/statistical tiers separately (postos administrativos, localidades, municipalities, enumeration areas). This supports the source's ADM2/district versus finer-granularity distinction for Nampula, not boundary accuracy or every other province.
- **INE 2024 statistical yearbooks by province**, with restoration links: Manica https://www.ine.gov.mz/documents/20119/334662/anuario-estatistico-provincia-manica-2024.pdf/ffb14c2a-33de-6522-a129-f3e4c0ed05f8?download=true&t=1751099992955&version=1.0 ; Sofala https://www.ine.gov.mz/documents/20119/334659/Anu%C3%A1rio%20Estatistico_Provincia%20de%20Sofala_2024.pdf/56529f80-ac52-2dbc-3e6a-eb8dfa0d30f0?download=true&t=1751964515631&version=1.0 ; Inhambane https://www.ine.gov.mz/en/d/anuario-estatistico-provincia-de-inhambane-2024 ; Niassa https://ine.gov.mz/documents/20119/334644/Anuario%20Estatistico%20Provincial%20Niassa%202024.pdf/762b7598-23f3-2948-acee-d28736f5f05f?download=true&t=1751536048634&version=1.0 ; Zambézia https://ine.gov.mz/o/adaptive-media/image/340725/Preview-1000x0/Anuario%2B2025%2BZambezia-2024_101751.pdf?t=1751286065530 . Metadata/catalog pages identify each as an INE 2024 statistical yearbook; file contents were not retained in this packet. Restoration links are provided for a later row-level official roster check. They do not grant redistribution rights over the district boundary data.
- **ZamStats, 2022 Census National Analytical Report**, chapter 1, “Geographic Overview / Administration”: https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/2022-Census-National-Analytical-Report.pdf . States ten provinces and 116 districts at the time of the 2022 Census; aggregate support only.
- **Zambia official 2022 OSG/GRID3 ArcGIS item**: https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=58c57f0ae33249bd8948dbf253f4e49e . Item title “Zambia districts 2022”; public Feature Service; last modified 2024-03-26; the retained query result was retrieved 2026-10-05 23:59:48 UTC. Item description/license states CC BY 4.0, 1:10,000-scale compilation and collaboration with OSG, Local Government and Housing, ECZ, CSO/ZamStats, and UNZA. The item reports government validation/endorsement; it has an unresolved internal `PROVINCE`/numeric-code conflict for Chama, Chirundu, and Itezhi-Tezhi.
- **Chirundu Town Council, district history**: https://www.chirundutowncouncil.gov.zm/ . Search result states Chirundu was formed within Lusaka in January 2012 and relocated to Southern Province in 2021. This supports a dated parent lead; reconcile with the 2022 layer's `PROVINCE=Southern`, `PROV_CODE=109`, `DIST_CODE=105002` before any ID-parent change.
- **Current official Zambia provincial/parliamentary evidence for disputed parents**: Eastern Province Administration's 2024 Provincial Implementation Plan (https://www.eas.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Eastern-Province-Provincial-Implementation-Plan.pdf) and Muchinga Province government page (https://www.muchingaprovince.gov.zm/) have both been found listing Chama under their respective provinces; INE's 2022 census migration table places it in Eastern. Southern Province Administration (https://www.southernprovince.gov.zm/) lists Chirundu and Itezhi-Tezhi; Zambia Parliament sources include a 2021 historical statement that Itezhi-Tezhi moved to Central in 2012 and later Southern references. These conflicting, differently dated narratives are unresolved, not evidence to select whichever matches the current source row.
- **GRID3, 2020 Zambia harmonization history**: https://grid3.org/news/grid3-zambia-administrative-boundaries . Describes historical multi-agency boundary harmonization and the role of the Office of the Surveyor General. Process history is not a substitute for current row-level adjudication.
- **Zambia Eastern Province roster evidence independently inspected in packet #413**, merged in the current PR base `bd2c2aeed9fcaa0b209a4bec1fe18b7129bb7f60`: `data/regional-review/regional-review-9203cb61c3883e07/ASSESSMENT.md` and `source/PROVENANCE.md`. Its provenance ledger gives source URLs, inspected pages and whole-document SHA-256 values for ZamStats's 2022 Census Preliminary Report (Appendix 3.3 lists 15 Eastern districts including Chama), the 2023–2047 Eastern Province Census Projections (includes Chama), and Eastern Province Administration's current district list (includes Chama). The 2025 Chama District Council IDP is only search-indexed in #413 and its TLS validation failed; that document says the district returned to Eastern in 2021 and was not independently retained. Those current ZamStats/provincial sources support Chama's current Eastern parent, while OSG/GRID3 `PROV_CODE`/`DIST_CODE` conflict remains a code/linework restoration issue. The original reports are not reproduced here; use their immutable URLs and recorded digests before an engineering correction.
- **Chirundu Town Council**, https://www.chirundutowncouncil.gov.zm/ ; current Southern Province Administration, https://www.southernprovince.gov.zm/ . Council history says the 2012 district in Lusaka was moved to Southern Province in 2021; the current Southern government page includes Chirundu. These support a current parent correction candidate but do not resolve the 2022 OSG/GRID3 `PROV_CODE`/`DIST_CODE` discordance or independently validate boundary geometry.

All government and source-vendor claims above are summarized, not quoted. Review the linked documents themselves before making an engineering correction. Their current accessibility and online content can change.
