# Southern Africa batch 2: source and semantic review

Issue [#436](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/436) is a read-only evidence review of 196 South African local/metro municipal IDs. Baseline: `origin/main` commit `93c901e1c0b44073233fd3d48d403985a0cf2c52`. The issue's exact JSON scope is retained at `sources/issue-436-scope.json` (SHA-256 `90a4d40af9a9dcb53bd7c7d206c2f3251a59da6bf987a4be3784ee68d7aa7188`). No shared geography, IDs, releases, or live data were changed.

## Findings

- **Official role, name, and parent crosswalk:** Stats SA's 2022 municipality table contains 213 entries across these nine provinces: Eastern Cape 33, Free State 19, Gauteng 9, KwaZulu-Natal 44, Limpopo 22, Mpumalanga 17, North West 18, Northern Cape 26, and Western Cape 25. The 196 issue IDs map one-to-one to distinct table codes and all province/parent entries match the current table. Their current roles are seven Category A metropolitan municipalities and 189 Category B local municipalities. The original pinned source describes its ADM3 collection as “Local municipality”; its name/category semantics need the Category A exception for the metro units. See `statsa-review.tsv` for every ID, source name, current official name/code/category, province, district, parent match, geodesic-area comparison, and conservative per-unit classification.
- **Completeness, with a precise limit:** the #436 196-ID roster and the Eastern Cape complement's 17 IDs from #435 are disjoint and together exactly exhaust the pinned 213-feature 2020 geoBoundaries ADM3 roster. The nine per-province totals also match Stats SA's 2022 table. This supports roster completeness for the named municipal tier in these packets; it does not prove that the polygons include every legally assigned island or current boundary detail.
- **Eight name corrections are needed; one name linkage remains uncertain.** Eight IDs have names differing from the 2022 Stats SA table: Greater Tubatse/Fetakgomo → Fetakgomo Tubatse; Ventersdorp/Tlokwe → JB Marks; Mbombela → City of Mbombela; Mafikeng → Mahikeng; Sol Plaatjie → Sol Plaatje; Kheis → !Kheis; Kai Garib → Kai !Garib; and Modimolle/Mookgophong → Modimolle-Mookgophong. Stats SA documents the 2018 mergers for the first three and Modimolle/Mookgophong; the current table supplies the official forms for the others. Preserve all existing IDs and provenance; engineering should update labels only after matching the unit's exact source identity. The ninth difference, Mbizana → Winnie Madikizela-Mandela, is an **unverified candidate name linkage**: the current unique candidate is in the same Eastern Cape province and Alfred Nzo district, but this review did not find a primary source tying the old name and exact source ID to that current label. Its row stays `insufficient-evidence`.
- **Parent tier/metro duplication:** 48 owned `framework:province:*` parents map to source ADM2 units. Stats SA and the source roster identify 41 as Category C district municipalities and seven as Category A metros. The seven single-child metro pairs are Buffalo City, City of Cape Town, City of Johannesburg, City of Tshwane, Ekurhuleni, Mangaung, and eThekwini. Their pinned ADM2 and ADM3 geometries are topologically equal for five pairs. The other two are not equal in coordinate geometry, though their WGS84 source-edge land areas differ by less than 0.0000005%; exact current territorial equivalence remains unproven. South Africa's Constitution says a Category A municipality has exclusive authority, while Category B shares authority with a Category C municipality; Stats SA lists each metro as Category A. This is strong evidence that the parent/child representation repeats a metro territory and merits an ID-preserving engineering decision. It does not authorize deleting either record. See `parent-review.json` and `metro-parent-review.json`.
- **District names and changed boundaries:** Stats SA documents 2018 district name changes Sisonke → Harry Gwala, Uthungulu → King Cetshwayo, Eden → Garden Route, Siyanda → Z F Mgcawu, and Greater Sekhukhune → Sekhukhune. In this packet, the three stale parent labels are Eden, Sisonke, and Uthungulu. It also documents district/municipal boundary adjustments across the scoped provinces and multiple mergers/amalgamations. The source's 2020 year and January 2023 update date do not show which local/district polygons reflect each legal redetermination. Keep the older parent IDs and source labels in evidence; do not repin or silently rename.
- **Polygon part loss and boundary-area flag:** all 196 source and Atlas geometries parse as valid, but valid geometry and row counts are not boundary approval. Eight subjects have different multipart counts between the pinned source and Atlas copy. Three possible omitted detached territories need a current licensed official boundary crosswalk: Saldanha Bay (7 source parts, 1 Atlas part; source's six smaller parts total about 1.43 km²), Swartland (2, 1; detached source part 2.60 km²), and Overstrand (3, 1; detached source parts 0.49 km²). Other count differences create extra Atlas parts or tiny slivers. Treat these as flags, not proof of lost islands. Separately, Atlas's WGS84 geodesic area for Sol Plaatjie differs by 5.24% from Stats SA's reported 2022 area (3,138.2 vs 3,311.6 km²); the 2018 factsheet reports a slight Sol Plaatje boundary adjustment. Resolve with dated official geometry rather than an area threshold.
- **Boundary decision remains open for every unit.** The geometry source is a pinned 2020 collection. The Stats SA 2022 list independently establishes current labels, codes, categories, reported areas, and district assignments but is not a coordinate dataset. An MDB feature service layer was inspected; all 213 row timestamps are 2016-08-17, its temporal provenance is unclear, and no reuse license was exposed in the service metadata. It cannot safely substantiate current boundaries or be copied into the packet. Obtain an explicitly reusable official MDB boundary vintage that postdates the 2018 demarcation; compare each exact ID, perimeter, island coverage, and district relationship before proposing geometry changes.
- **Other requested checks:** this packet reviewed named role and parent relationships for all IDs and reports every multipart and official area comparison. It does not find a province-sized subject. The province-wide unit list does not establish offshore-island geometry completeness, so islands and detached municipality fragments remain explicitly unverified. No history attributes or regional approval are implied.

All 196 units have a row-level status in `statsa-review.tsv`. Eight are `correction-needed` for their current official names; one (Mbizana) and the remaining 187 are `insufficient-evidence` for a complete current territorial claim. Every row separately records `boundary_status=insufficient-evidence`. The 48 parent rows are likewise marked `insufficient-evidence` for semantic tier/boundary approval even where their dated source identity is verified.

## Reproduction

From the repository root, with Python 3.12, `shapely==2.1.2`, `pyproj==3.7.2`, `pypdf==6.19.0`, and `certifi==2026.7.22` installed in the packet scratch path. Install the pinned processing dependencies into local scratch with `python3 -m pip install --target data/regional-review/regional-review-1f8d0c3f746b0f8e/.scratch/python shapely==2.1.2 pyproj==3.7.2 pypdf==6.19.0 certifi==2026.7.22`. Reproduction commands (with `PYTHONPATH=data/regional-review/regional-review-1f8d0c3f746b0f8e/.scratch/python:scripts`):

```sh
python data/regional-review/regional-review-1f8d0c3f746b0f8e/reproduce.py --output data/regional-review/regional-review-1f8d0c3f746b0f8e/.scratch/source-crosswalk.json
python data/regional-review/regional-review-1f8d0c3f746b0f8e/reproduce_statsa.py > data/regional-review/regional-review-1f8d0c3f746b0f8e/statsa-screening-summary.json
```

The ADM3 run verifies the whole original source SHA when the restored original file is present; otherwise it consumes the two retained, checksum-listed source-feature partitions. Both paths reproduce the same complete source crosswalk byte-for-byte. The partitioner preserves original CRS metadata, properties and coordinates. `reproduce_statsa.py` restores and checks the non-retained Stats SA PDF, parses all 213 published rows, verifies every issue member against a unique code, checks the exact nine area counts and #435 Eastern Cape complement, and writes the per-unit table and measurement ledger. Metric screens are measurements, not correctness thresholds.
