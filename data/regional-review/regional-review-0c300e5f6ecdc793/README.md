# Southeastern Europe interior batch 9 evidence packet (#424)

**Research date:** 2026-10-05. **Atlas baseline:** `dab6ef29468eb97226f642783127720444f299c9`. **Issue scope:** exact 33 frozen IDs in [`issue-scope.json`](issue-scope.json); source issue snapshot: [`issue-api-snapshot.json`](issue-api-snapshot.json). This packet reviews only those IDs and the two named areas in the issue. It does not certify region #425 or authorize hierarchy edits.

## Findings

- **Scope and current structure reproduce exactly:** 33 unique IDs: Istanbul whole-province city aggregate (1), 31 geoBoundaries Turkey ADM2 district units, and the geoBoundaries Slovenia ADM2 unit currently named `Hodoj` (1). The 31 Turkish children are under Çanakkale (3), Edirne (9), Kırklareli (8), and Tekirdağ (11); the Istanbul city aggregate is the full Istanbul province; `Hodoj` is the only child of a retained-reference area called `Yugoslavia`. The 3 Çanakkale features are only a subset of that province's 12 districts; the other three Turkish provinces' scoped children reproduce their full 9/8/11 unit rosters at the pinned Atlas ADM2 level. See [`findings/area-assessments.csv`](findings/area-assessments.csv). See [`findings/individual-assessments.csv`](findings/individual-assessments.csv) and the reproducible [`findings/audit_scope.py`](findings/audit_scope.py) / [`findings/audit-results.json`](findings/audit-results.json).
- **Turkey-in-Europe is not pinned to a single official administrative definition by the reviewed sources.** The Turkish Culture and Tourism Ministry describes Thrace as Turkey's European side, separated from Anatolia by the Istanbul straits, Marmara Sea, and Dardanelles. GoTürkiye's tourism page defines Thrace by Edirne, Kırklareli, and Tekirdağ. Those are tourism/semantic descriptions, not a legal territorial boundary. The current Atlas assigns Eceabat, Gelibolu, and Imbros as open Europe semantic reviews, but nearby Bozcaada as an open Asia semantic review; Bozcaada is not in #424's exact IDs. Çanakkale Governor's official roster lists both Bozcaada and Gökçeada as separate province districts, while the issue area selects only three of 12 Çanakkale units. The frozen Europe–Asia macro record's eastern Aegean crosswalk lists 19 Greek ADM3 IDs and does not list these Turkish islands. This exposes a neighboring-granularity and island-consistency gap; it does not itself decide either island's continental association. A frozen macro rule reserves named-island membership and warns that uncovered source land does not mean an island is absent, but that policy's named 19-island crosswalk covers Greek eastern-Aegean units and is not evidence for these two Turkish features. The issue set also includes three Çanakkale districts and Istanbul province, so it does not match that narrower three-province tourism convention.
- **Istanbul must not be represented as wholly European on this evidence.** Atlas change 38 of `data/semantic-report.json` shows that `atlas:city:TUR-2265` replaces all 32 Istanbul districts and covers 4,215.58 km² with zero reported coverage error. This is a province-wide aggregation. GoTürkiye and the Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality both describe Istanbul as spanning Europe and Asia. Thus whole-province inclusion in a strictly European area is semantically inconsistent, even though it is a faithful Istanbul city/province feature. A boundary definition, split geometry, or changed area purpose requires engineering and policy review; none is made here. This handoff is now a bounded follow-up, #982, blocked on #424 and the Istanbul-roster prerequisite #821; #425's integration dependency list was updated to include it.
- **Çanakkale's 3 district-level members need a declared geographic convention.** The issue units are Gelibolu, Eceabat, and `Imbros` (Gökçeada). The Tourism Ministry's broad physical description uses the Dardanelles as part of the Europe/Anatolia separation; the narrower GoTürkiye administrative list omits Çanakkale altogether. Neither source determines whether every scoped unit, especially the island, belongs to the issue's Europe area. Membership is therefore left unresolved rather than inferred from an administrative count or display geometry.
- **The Yugoslavia area is historical in name and currently has one Slovenian municipality child.** GOV.SI identifies the municipality in bilingual official form as Hodoš/Hodos and lists Hodoš/Hodos and Krplivnik/Kapornak as its settlements; SURS places it in Pomurska statistical region. The retained 2017 geoBoundaries record calls the feature `Hodoj`; the ID is stable but its source spelling conflicts with the current government form. GOV.SI's history source places Slovenia in postwar Yugoslavia and records the independence acts of 25 June 1991. The present-day single-child link must not be read as current national sovereignty or a current regional boundary. The retained WGSRPD context identifies a botanical reference framework and its area semantic/boundary review remains open. The correct long-term disposition (retire historic parent, relabel a documented historical unit, or establish a better current parent) belongs in regional integration #425 after its bounded Yugoslavia packets have been reviewed; this packet does not duplicate that shared-parent decision.
- **Administrative source limitations:** geoBoundaries' pinned source metadata describes TUR ADM2 as “Districts”, source year 2021, OSM-derived, ODbL 1.0; the retained raw file contains 973 features while metadata says `admUnitCount=999`. SVN ADM2 metadata describes municipality features, source year 2017, OSM-derived, ODbL 1.0; its file contains 213 features while SURS currently reports 212 municipalities. These are vintage/count mismatches, not grounds to silently edit the scoped identities. The pinned raw sources and metadata are retained byte-for-byte under `sources/geoboundaries-9469f09/`; hashes are regenerated by the audit. The official Interior Ministry 2018 PDF's pages 24, 30, 53, and 78 list the four relevant province district names; text extraction with `pypdf==5.9.0` reproduced the names and creation dates for the subsets discussed above.
- **The Turkish General Directorate of Mapping (HGM) 2026 district-boundary product is only a geometry screen here.** HGM states the display boundaries are approximate, derived from various sources, have no official status, and that legal boundary establishment/change/publication is governed by Law 5442. The retained layer has 2,499 unlabeled boundary line segments and no district names or IDs for matching to this issue. Its source page limits redistribution to non-commercial activity with attribution. It cannot validate any particular member boundary or resolve Europe-side meaning. Archive and extracted files are retained under `sources/hgm-2026/` with exact hashes in the audit results.

## Scoped granularity checks

- All 31 raw Turkey source features are named ADM2 `Districts`; the one raw Slovenia feature is named `Hodoj` ADM2 `Municipality`. The 33 reviewed current features are named; no anonymous remainder occurs in this exact packet. This says nothing about missing features in the nationwide source.
- Istanbul is not a normal ADM2 district: the cited Natural Earth feature is explicitly `type_en=Province`; Atlas replaces 32 former Istanbul districts with its full 4,215.58 km² city/province aggregate. This is a mixed-tier area, not evidence that district-level coverage is complete.
- Source/Atlas component comparison found two changes needing engineering review. Meriç's retained 2021 geoBoundaries source is one Polygon, while Atlas is a topology-reconciled two-part MultiPolygon; its second four-vertex Atlas component has a 0.00117° by 0.00098° bbox. Marmaraereğlisi's raw source is a two-part MultiPolygon but Atlas is a single Polygon; a 7-vertex source component at 27.8967183–27.8968915 E / 40.9608326–40.9609227 N is absent from the Atlas feature. It may be a boundary sliver or omitted land; the evidence here does not decide that. Atlas metadata says topology reconciliation can cause boundaries to differ from source. Preserve the raw components and ask #425 to inspect against a named, current boundary source before any adjustment. Geometry validity and component counts do not establish legal boundaries, island completeness, or source completeness.
- Çanakkale has 12 districts in the official 2018 list, while the area holds three: Eceabat, Gelibolu, and Gökçeada/Imbros. It omits nearby Bozcaada and other Çanakkale districts. Whether that is correct depends on the area purpose; #982 carries the paired island/area review. No target-count or province-rollup assumption is used.
- `Yugoslavia` is a weak present-day parent for one municipal child named by a 2017 OSM-derived feature as `Hodoj`; current GOV.SI uses Hodoš/Hodos and Slovenia is independent. The history and valid new parent require dated/source-backed integration in #425.
- No scoped subject is an anonymous remainder; all named units are at Turkey ADM2 / Slovenia municipality ADM2 except province-level Istanbul. The 31 districts repeat the same admin tier under their four administrative provinces, as expected for these selected provinces. Nonetheless, the Turkey area mixes a Province and District source granularity, and completeness remains distinct from geographic correctness. The exact open and correction-needed rows are in the assessment CSVs.

## Area and neighboring granularity

The issue mixes one province-scale Istanbul whole-city aggregate with Turkish district (ADM2) members, while its Slovenian subject sits in a historic area via the only current municipality child. Consequently its two area scopes do not express a common, consistently current administrative level. The named `Turkey-in-Europe` area also has no cited operational authority or area-purpose standard in the issue data that selects among a physical continent boundary and the tourism convention. Administrative completeness is reproduced; geographic correctness is not established by that fact.

Evaluate Çanakkale and island membership against the selected line. Do not apply a member correction until the convention and evidence are reviewed.

3. Reconcile source vintage differences for Turkey and Slovenia before a future boundary update. HGM's unlabeled 2026 display layer is not suitable for name-based unit matching; obtain a licensed, named current source with stated lineage and status or supply documented restoration instructions.

The regional Yugoslavia/Hodoš shared-parent disposition remains with #425; the source-role correction and Turkey-in-Europe area review are linked follow-ups #425 and #982. ## Uncertainty

The reviewed authoritative sources do not supply one operational definition of the `Turkey-in-Europe` area or settle Çanakkale's island/strait membership. The HGM geometry product cannot be joined to names and expressly lacks official boundary status. The Atlas inherited city aggregation and historical Yugoslavia relation need semantic engineering review; this evidence packet does not choose the future hierarchy outcome. Turkish national directory counts conflict internally (921/922), and the pinned 2021/2017 OSM-derived counts are 973/213 despite metadata/current municipal counts of 999/212. No evidence here establishes parcel-level, legally operative, or fully contemporary boundaries for the Turkey districts.
