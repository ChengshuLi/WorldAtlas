# Western Indian Ocean source review — issue #633

This packet reviews the exact 24 locations declared by #633. It advances source identification, source-to-unit crosswalks, reproducible comparisons, and explicit source-access/completeness findings. It does not certify regional geography, approve a boundary, or authorize any geometry, hierarchy, or import change.

The issue acceptance is only partly settled. This review resolves that a coarse GSHHG non-detection is not evidence that St Brandon or Agaléga contains no land; it identifies lawful but incomplete official and physical-source candidates; it reproduces the source identity and parent crosswalks for all 24 targets; and it records where current complete evidence could not be obtained. St Brandon membership and footprint completeness, Agaléga cross-source membership, the full Seychelles outer-island footprint, and complete current settlement coverage remain unresolved. Keep #633 open for those exact follow-ups.

## What the sources establish

The pinned 2017 geoBoundaries Mauritius ADM1 release contains nine Mauritius Island districts and three records with different roles: Rodrigues, St Brandon, and Agaléga. Its canonical label is “Districts and Outer Islands of Mauritius”, so twelve features are not twelve same-granularity districts. Statistics Mauritius’ 2022 census distinguishes the nine Mauritius Island geographical districts from the five Municipal Council Areas and seven District Council Areas; the latter comprise 30 municipal wards and 130 Village Council Areas, with about 30 VCAs crossing two or three districts. Rodrigues is six local regions with named localities. Those units are administrative/census evidence, not a physical island list or geocoded settlement layer.

I retrieved the official Mauritius ONSDI `geonode:districts` WFS layer (ODbL 1.0). It has nine main-island district polygons and joins one-to-one by normalized exact name to the nine geoBoundaries districts. In EPSG:6933, source-to-source symmetric difference ranges from 0.00048% to 1.35156%; compare the per-district results in `district-comparison.json`. It independently supports the nine-unit main-island roster, but the record was last updated in 2022 and does not state a survey date, legal instrument, settlement source, or outer-island coverage. Current Atlas-versus-ONSDI differences are larger, 2.30363%–9.50301%, so do not silently transfer or “correct” them from this one layer. The nine comparisons are triage measurements, not a legal boundary validation.

The same official portal’s ODbL `geonode:islet_parks` WFS response contains eight named point records. Its metadata defines them as islet national parks, and its small bounding box is around Mauritius Island. This is a park point subset, not island outlines or an inventory of all offshore land; it does not settle St Brandon or Agaléga.

The official OIDC/Environment Observatory descriptions say Cargados Carajos/St Brandon comprises low islets, reefs, shoals, and shifting sand features, with counts varying by storm and season. Government sources differ in their general count descriptions, so no source count is substituted for a named feature crosswalk. A 2017 geoBoundaries St Brandon polygon has one component; its prior source/current comparison has 77.41952% symmetric difference, and GSHHG level-1 does not intersect it. Neither GSHHG non-detection nor that older mismatch establishes absence or the correct outline. Mauritius Cartography sells vector mapping under its own terms; its hydrographic charts are not lawful replacement vectors for this packet without a data/reuse agreement.

The two Agaléga records remain distinct: an OSM/Wambacher geoBoundaries outer-island ADM1 record with two components and a Natural Earth named dependency record with one component. The current polygons are disjoint. Government text says the physical island group has two principal low islands with a fringing reef, which is compatible with different source roles/scales but does not tell which exposed land or reef belongs in either record. The prior geometry comparison reports 23.33854% symmetric difference for geoBoundaries Agaléga and 0.00356% for the Natural Earth record against their respective current features. No merge, duplicate, or replacement is proposed.

For Seychelles, the eight pinned geoBoundaries ADM2 subjects are `Regions`; the retained OCHA/NBS-provenance 2019 statistical crosswalk matches all eight region rows and has 27 ADM3 district rows. Four current parent labels differ from the reference: Central 1 Mahe→Bel Air, La Digue→La Digue a, Praslin→Baie Saint, and Other Islands→Outer Isla. The 2018 State House council announcement groups 25 named district administration areas into seven Regional Councils (including “Aux Cap”); the separate 2025 District Administrators PDF lists 27 districts. These different rosters do not prove that council, district, and ADM2 region boundaries are interchangeable. The latter two sources supply names, not legal region geometries.

The 2019 Seychelles “Other Islands” source feature has 1,057 components versus seven in the current feature. This is a material granularity and membership question. GSHHG is too coarse to resolve it. The Seychelles MSP Atlas exposes a physical `Basemap_Islands` candidate layer with 1,315 feature records and a separate 117-point place-name layer. Its service is publicly queryable, but its item has blank `licenseInfo` and credits the Seychelles Ministry of Environment, Energy and Climate Change/TNC. The map product is published as version 1.1 in 2025, but the island-layer data metadata says last edited 2023-11-18. Polygon count is not island count; the 117 place names are not identified as settlements. No geometry was retained because reuse terms were not established.

For Iles Éparses, Mayotte, and Réunion, Shom/IGN catalogues lawful Open Licence 2.0 Litto3D land/sea elevation sources: Eparses 2012, Mayotte 2012, and Réunion 2016. Metadata specifies a 1 m grid and the combined coastal survey methods; the Réunion record reports land/bathymetry acquisition in 2008–2010 despite the 2016 product name. These are much better scale candidates than Natural Earth or GSHHG for coastal screens, but island-specific tile coverage, acquisition dates, masks, and the correspondence to each Atlas polygon were not inspected. A candidate source is not an exhaustiveness result. Shom’s distribution portal requests sign-in; public WMS metadata gives a reproducible, read-only inspection route. Keep the “do not use for navigation” term.

I inspected the exact Shom WMS 1.3 `EX_GeographicBoundingBox` records (capabilities SHA-256 `ed53a1b8fb9ffa50b811e30fa65f5e498f1945153355f539fdeba1755495efbd`) and screened them against each current Atlas feature bbox. Mayotte’s bbox is enclosed by its advertised layer envelope. ATF-5919 extends 0.626701309° west of the Eparses envelope, and Réunion extends 0.010225303° east of its envelope. These metadata envelopes warn that the published service bounds do not enclose two entire Atlas record bboxes. They do not prove elevation data are missing: WMS envelopes are not individual tile/source masks, so no coast/island completeness claim follows.

Natural Earth 1:10m reproduces the current ATF-5919, FRA-4601, FRA-4602, and MUS-5180 geometries closely, with under 0.005% symmetric difference. These remain named territory shapes at a generalized scale, not complete small-island/reef inventories. French administrative status or an EEZ line does not establish physical island membership or sovereignty beyond what the named source states.

## Settlement and locality outcome

No current official, licensed, geocoded settlement gazetteer covering all 24 assigned locations was found. Mauritius Census 2022 provides administrative units and named localities but no complete coordinates. The official Mauritius open portal’s Bus Stops layer says its original data are from OpenStreetMap; that is a transport feature class and not a complete settlement inventory. Seychelles NBS’ 2022 report is discoverable at the current official page, but the prior download attempt reached an account/login wall; the older retained NBS/OCHA ADM2/ADM3 files contain statistical administrative rows, not coordinates. Seychelles MSP gazetteer points are place names with no verified settlement feature type. No settlement completeness claim is made.
