# Kiribati Gilbert / Phoenix / Line source crosswalk — #611

## Result and scope

This is a source-evidence packet for issue #611 only. It proposes **no** geometry, hierarchy, location ID, claim, grid or release change and certifies no region or import. It preserves the exact issue-owned path and reproduces the full 2017 geoBoundaries component inventory. The central result is an unresolved source-lineage and coverage conflict:

- The preserved 2017 geoBoundaries KIR ADM1 source is an OSM/Wambacher product with three multipart features, labeled Gilbert (29 components), Phoenix (11) and Line (5). Its metadata calls the boundary type ADM1, but Kiribati’s current NSO census geography distinguishes four Gilbert districts from a single combined Line & Phoenix district; the 23 current Island Councils are a different system again. Those facts do not validate the source feature names as nested administrative jurisdictions.
- Current baseline ID `gb:KIR:ADM1:97431129B55805139245338` (“Gilbert Islands”) has 14 parts compared with 29 in its source feature. A full 34-file current-geography bbox scan finds 14 source components with current Gilbert component bbox candidates and 15 without any current bbox candidate. This is a reproducible component-level coverage discrepancy; actual polygon coverage and exact census-row assignments remain unresolved. The undated migration archive separately preserves both original paths and migrated `geo:province:*` aliases for Gilbert, Phoenix and Line; those rows have no geometry and their transfer policy forbids transferring historical attributes by overlap. It is not a 19-location quota.
- Current baseline ID `gb:KIR:ADM1:97431129B36644055464690` (“Western equatorial Pacific source remainder”) has two parts. They sit near Banaba and Tarawa by coordinate, and their bounding boxes are candidates within source Phoenix feature components 2 and 4. Source component 1’s bounds are `[-171.7234122,-2.8707411,-171.6151249,-2.7646223]`; the #526 Canton/Kanton/Abariringa assessment independently retains OSM coastline way `22391694`, whose land-component bounds are `[-171.7233574,-2.8706287,-171.6151625,-2.7645001]`. #526 assigns that way to the Kanton source family; the way itself is tagged `natural=coastline`, `place=island`, `source=PGS & Bing` (version 16, dated 2022-07-25). Its major component’s bounds differ only about 0.0002 degrees from source component 1. This is strong source-geography evidence that the 2017 source component 1 represents Kanton; the source feature ring has 685 vertices while the later retained way has 1,801, so exact polygon equivalence and the historical version are not certified. This is not proof of legal jurisdiction. Components 2 and 4 are only bbox candidates for the two Banaba/Tarawa-vicinity remainder parts. The other eight source parts have no bbox candidate in current Atlas geography. Among the eight #526 retained island source families, Kanton is the only one with a bbox candidate in this 11-part source feature; the other seven do not. Bounding-box screens do not establish exact overlaps. The #526 packet independently supports eight named dry-land Phoenix subjects but explicitly leaves source-family completeness unresolved.
- The source Line feature has five components near 157–160°W. It is an essential source neighbor; the 2017 feature is not a complete island-level or local-government crosswalk. The 2022 atlas lists Kiritimati, Tabuaeran and Teraina in the combined Line & Phoenix district, while MCIA lists councils on those three islands and does not list a Kanton council among its current 23. This establishes different census and council coverage, not physical boundaries or omissions.

The evidence supports a bounded engineering handoff, not a safe direct fix: preserve the predecessor source record and all eight #526 Phoenix subjects; have the #140/#139 Gilbert/Line integration owners and #526/#404 Phoenix owners jointly identify the historic source editing lineage and resolve the two Banaba/Tarawa fragments, source-only pieces, and current parent chains before any shared correction. Keep all political ownership unset until a separate authoritative source supports it.

## Administrative, physical, lineage and ownership questions

- **Administrative:** unresolved. Current NSO district grouping and council lists do not reproduce the three geoBoundaries ADM1 feature names/partition. Individual warrants, source editing history, and exact council coverage need verification. A source’s ADM1 label alone is insufficient.
- **Physical:** the census and PIPA/atlas establish broad named groups and reporting rows, but not a complete physical island inventory or exact modern coastlines. The 14/29 count is a source-difference finding only. No exact unmatched dry-land polygon has been proven from component counts.
- **Source lineage:** established as far as the immutable #140 packet proves: two exact current IDs preserve fragments associated with two source features, and B558 is derived from Gilbert while B366 is derived from Phoenix. Exact per-component predecessor editing, clipping/merge history, the full historical claim registry and claim preservation remain unverified. #526 establishes its own eight OSM source families independently.
- **Political ownership:** not evaluated and not inferred from parent/macro membership. No territorial transfer, claim, disputed status or sovereign attribution is endorsed here.

## Reproduction

Run from repository root:

```sh
python3 data/regional-review/kiribati-gilbert-phoenix-source-crosswalk/build_crosswalk.py
```

The builder verifies source bytes and SHA-256 against the #140 manifest; checks the three expected component totals; inventories every part bbox; inventories the exact scoped retained geometry parts, all eight #526 IDs with their retained hashes, and the three subjects’ original and migrated alias rows from the hash-pinned migration archive; and writes `source-component-inventory.json`. `retained_bbox_candidates` are rectangle tests only. A zero candidate is not proof of absent land, and a candidate is not proof of polygon overlap. The source and historical packets are read-only inputs. The archive is read from the pinned Git blob, decompressed in memory, and only six matching unit records are emitted; full source bytes remain unchanged. This reproduces inventory and evidence integrity, not geographic correctness, completeness or region approval.
