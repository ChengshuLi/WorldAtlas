## Findings

- The Kiribati Meteorological Service’s current “Islands We Cover” page names 17 Gilbert, 8 Phoenix and 8 Line physical island groups. The official 2025 Biodiversity Area Report independently describes 32 low-lying coral islands and Banaba as the raised island, with the same 17/8/8 group counts. Neither source supplies boundary geometry.
- The NSO/SPC 2022 Census Atlas Table 3 (printed p.11) has 19 Gilbert reporting rows. North and South Tarawa are divisions of Tarawa; North and South Tabiteuea are divisions of Tabiteuea. The resulting 17 named physical groups do not imply 17 census units or source polygons. Table 5 (printed p.18) is the population-density table, not the island roster. The 2020 census report and the NSO districts page describe statistical geography, not local-government jurisdiction.
- MCIA’s current profiles page lists 23 Island Councils for its 2025–2029 term. The Establishment Warrant index identifies warrants under Local Government Act 1984 s.4 as the records that specify a council, effective date and geographic area. The linked warrant PDFs restored for this inquiry were scans; local OCR did not yield reliable text. Their URLs, retrieval sizes and SHA-256 digests are preserved as restoration receipts only. I make no claim about their legal areas, effective dates, or consequences of a missing index entry.
- The source vintage, license, retrieval date, exact hash, bytes and restoration instructions for each inspected official source are recorded in the JSON. No new official source bytes are redistributed. The 2017 geoBoundaries source and eight Phoenix OSM source families remain in the already retained packets under their recorded licenses.
- A pinned archive search examined all 19,050 location and 16,046 unit names using normalized exact matching against the 42 published names/aliases for the 33 physical groups. It found three global name collisions (Makin, Caroline, Washington), each with an unrelated parent, and no island-specific Kiribati archived name row. The archive retains broad Gilbert/Phoenix/Line source IDs and aliases as already listed in the first packet. The archive is undated and identity-only: neither exact-name misses nor hits establish physical absence, geometry, or claims. The query and all hits are recorded in the JSON.
- The JSON identifies all 29 Gilbert, 11 Phoenix and 5 Line source components, their bbox candidates and the retained inventory digest. Bounding boxes are screening evidence only. No component is assigned to a named physical island, census row, council area or legal jurisdiction on that basis.
