## Official territorial meaning and cross-checks

Primary sources were consulted on 2026-10-05 UTC. Direct HTTP retrieval of the ANStat API failed in this environment with DNS resolution errors; no API response or official PDF is retained. The following URLs are explicit restoration instructions; do not infer that a result was successfully downloaded.

- The [Côte d’Ivoire Interior Ministry’s DGAT page on administrative-unit creation](https://dgat.interieur.gouv.ci/creation-de-circonscriptions-administratives/) identifies Law 2014-451 as the basis of territorial organization and distinguishes regions, departments, sub-prefectures, and villages. It reports a current, evolving structure of 31 regions, 108 departments, and 509 sub-prefectures (475 open). The page is current-vintage context, not the 2021 roster.
- The official [ANStat RGPH 2021 thematic report, Tome 1](https://www.anstat.ci/assets/publications/files/rgpg_tom1.pdf) describes 14 districts as of 2021-06-09, 31 administrative regions, 111 departments, and 510 sub-prefectures. It explains the hierarchy: regions contain departments, which contain sub-prefectures. It notes the special Department of Abidjan configuration and says the 2021 count of 510 sub-prefectures includes units whose sector organization differs. Restore the report from the official URL and inspect its tables before using an exact count as a roster.
- The [ANStat public API documentation](https://www.anstat.ci/public/api) exposes year-parameterized department and sub-prefecture endpoints. Its department-list example reports 111 records and shows `annee=2021`. Its sub-prefecture example lists Anyama, Bingerville, Brofodoumé, and Songon under Department Abidjan (`cod_dep=002`); this exact documented example supports the Brofodoumé role flag. The same documentation reports `total_count=526` for the sub-prefecture list, in tension with the ANStat report’s 510 and the DGAT current page’s 509. Because the live API could not be fetched here, that discrepancy and the example’s effective vintage remain unresolved. Restoration: query `https://api-public.anstat.ci/api/v1/departements` and `https://api-public.anstat.ci/api/v1/sous-prefectures` for `annee=2021`, requesting every page; retain response bytes and hashes if terms permit.
- Côte d’Ivoire’s [2014 Law 2014-451](https://dgat.interieur.gouv.ci/creation-de-circonscriptions-administratives/) establishes the legal distinction between regions, departments, and sub-prefectures; the [official DGAT act list](https://dgp-interieur.ci/fr/publication-lois.php?men=5) lists this law. The district is a special territorial entity. For Abidjan, the official [District Autonome d’Abidjan law page](https://web.cicg.gouv.ci/index.php/ministere/loi_sur_daa) says the autonomous district includes the communes and sub-prefectures of the Department of Abidjan. This does not make the autonomous district an immediate parent for every underlying sub-prefecture.
- The government’s [open-data listing of administrative constituencies and communes](https://data.gouv.ci/datasets/liste-des-circonscriptions-administratives-et-des-communes) was updated 2021-07-12, has an Open Licence, and reports 31 regions, 201 communes, and two autonomous districts. It is useful neighboring-granularity context; it is not a 510-unit sub-prefecture geometry roster.

The convergence of the geoBoundaries ADM3 feature count (510), the official RGPH 2021 sub-prefecture count (510), and the row-level Brofodoumé example strongly challenges the geoBoundaries/Atlas `Departments` role label. It does **not** establish that all 510 ADM3 polygons are a complete legal sub-prefecture set: the ministry reports 509 current sub-prefectures, ANStat’s API documentation reports 526 list entries, the official 2021 roster was not obtained, and the OCHA file has no per-row parent code. Exact ID/name/geometry resemblance cannot resolve those differences.
