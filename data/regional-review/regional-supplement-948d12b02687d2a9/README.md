## Phoenix Islands findings

**The named island-group role is justified; source-family completeness is not certified.** The Kiribati-authored Phoenix Islands Protected Area (PIPA) Management Plan names Kanton/Abariringa/Canton, Birnie, Enderbury, Manra/Sydney, McKean, Nikumaroro/Gardner, Orona/Hull and Rawaki/Phoenix. It calls the Phoenix Islands one of Kiribati’s three island groups, distinct from the Line Islands and Gilbert Islands. The issue’s eight location names correspond to that official list.

The same plan separates the eight atoll/reef islands from the much larger PIPA protected property: 408,250 km² of ocean and terrestrial habitats, two submerged reefs, and seamounts. PIPA’s marine conservation perimeter is not a land location footprint. The plan says the Phoenix Islands have no permanent settlement and records a small caretaker population on Kanton in its 2009–2014 management era. That is dated management evidence, not a contemporary population count.

The OSM responses for all eight locations are already lawfully retained under ODbL in the repository’s macro-coverage source archive. The matching source-profile and decompressed raw hashes, original API URLs, retrieval date (2026-10-02), compressed byte counts/hashes, and full per-way metadata are recorded in phoenix-assessment.json. The full OSM extract families reconstruct **29/29 closed land components**, with no open chains or missing nodes; all 29 components are absent from existing location footprints. The independently retained GSHHG 2.3.7 comparison inventories **33 polygons** totaling a different amount of land per island group. Source vintage, shoreline generalization, exposed-rock interpretation and possible unmapped land remain unresolved.

### Location and settlement findings

- **Nassau and Suwarrow:** the Cook Islands 2021 Census table reports Nassau land area 1.3 km² and population 92; Suwarrow is listed at 0.4 km² with no resident population value (`-`). OSM measures 1.2411 km² for Nassau, a close but non-authoritative comparison. Suwarrow OSM (1.3954 km²) and GSHHG (1.7282 km²) both materially exceed the census value; establish the source definitions, reef/land inclusions and component correspondence before selecting any land. The census dash means no reported resident count, not proof of zero visitors or no seasonal staff. NES reports seasonal Suwarrow ranger deployment in 2026. OPM identifies Pukapuka/Nassau as one Island Government; that supports a local governance association, not a sovereignty or boundary inference.
- **Takutea:** the 2008 Atiu and Takutea regulations define the island separately from surrounding marine waters and place its conservation area under Takutea Trustees, with Atiu Island Council and landowner consultation. This supports separate natural location identity and an Atiu-linked administrative/management context. The 12 nautical mile conservation buffer is marine and cannot be treated as location land. Census table omission is not a population finding; current permanent settlement remains unresolved from reviewed sources.
- **Palmerston:** the 2021 Census gives 2.1 km² and population 25; OSM (2.8919 km²) and GSHHG (2.3607 km²) differ materially. This is an unresolved source-definition/coastline discrepancy; no footprint choice proposed. OPM lists Palmerston separately among Island Governments, supporting its coextensive functional parent role. Environment Ministry material calls it an atoll and mentions Home Island and Cooks Motu. The corrected source identity work tracked in closed #502 is not reopened here.
- **Starbuck, Vostok, Malden, Flint, Caroline/Millennium:** Kiribati’s July 2020 Southern Line Islands Marine Protected Area regulations expressly name Flint, “Volstok” (their spelling), Starbuck, Malden and “Millenium (otherwise known as Caroline)” Island. They establish named land features within a larger protected area, but marine waters and 12-nautical-mile zones are not land footprints. The five remain five named location subjects; the 30 OSM ways/rings and 24 GSHHG polygons around Caroline/Millennium are component records, not a quota for splitting. Settlement status is not certified by this statute or the source topology review.
- **Pitcairn, Oeno and Ducie:** the Government of Pitcairn’s 2017 revised laws name the four-island group Pitcairn, Henderson, Ducie and Oeno. Its official “The Islands” page, retrieved earlier on 2026-10-03, stated Pitcairn is the only inhabited island; the previously retained response hash is `9d660643912b5f0986e415ed89b761bb5cf3b3dfc99877b611b510c75b67b4cf` (894,364 bytes). At review refresh the page URL returned 404. This is therefore dated official evidence that needs replacement/confirmation, not a current settlement census. Henderson is the unassigned fourth member and is named only to account for the full parent scope. Pitcairn’s 27 reconstructed OSM land rings do not mean 27 islands; Oeno and Ducie’s OSM/GSHHG component differences are unresolved source-family differences, not grounds for splitting.

All 12 complete parent chains are in `remaining-assessment.json`. Their endpoint is South-Central Pacific → Polynesia → Oceania. The full assigned-parent membership was also audited: Northern Cook area 2/6 (Nassau and Suwarrow assigned); Cook area 2/9 (Takutea and Palmerston); Line area 5/9 (Starbuck, Vostok, Malden, Flint and Caroline assigned; Jarvis, base Line Islands ADM1, Kingman and Palmyra are contextual neighbors); Pitcairn area 3/4 (Pitcairn, Oeno and Ducie assigned; Henderson contextual). ### Parent roles, remainders and coordination

- OPM’s combined Pukapuka/Nassau Island Government supports the Pukapuka province’s two-member local governance association. The Northern Cook area’s six-member review slice is not treated as a fully audited island roster beyond the enumerated context.

- “Cook Islands” area currently covers nine members while a separate Northern Cook area covers six. This makes the title ambiguous; ask regional integration to clarify whether it is intended as a southern/central grouping. No membership or boundary change is proposed.

These findings separate physical evidence from political ownership and historical claims. Ownership remains unknown in the location rows. Shared original parent scopes are documented, but not edited. Census, protected-area and legal extents are not substituted for coastlines.
