# West Africa interior batch 12: Nigeria, Senegal and Sierra Leone

This is an evidence-only review for issue [#478](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/478). The reserved scope contains exactly 226 published location IDs: Nigeria 179 of 774, Senegal 45 of 45 and Sierra Leone Western Area Rural/Urban 2 of 14. The frozen parent review and the province roster are enumerated in `scope.json`; `baseline-members.json` carries each subject's complete ancestor chain from the published reference. `administrative-scope.csv` accounts for all 3 area scopes and all 24 declared province scopes, including each partial-country remainder, with an individual status, reason, unresolved parent-subject list, and linked country follow-up. All 27 scope outcomes are insufficient-evidence because complete settlement and physical land/island coverage are not established; these statuses do not turn the workload partition into an atlas boundary.

## Findings

`assessment.csv` has one row for every assigned subject. It preserves the exact pinned source feature, role and vintage; the whole published parent chain; every same-name or positive-area current COD-AB candidate; parent labels; geometry overlap screens; and source/settlement limits. All 226 classifications are **insufficient-evidence** for an overall geographic conclusion. The administrative-parent screen supports 224 parent assignments and leaves two name-duplicate cases unresolved: Obi has Benue and Nasarawa candidates; Bassa has Kogi and Plateau candidates. Neither is resolved by name or overlap ranking. All rows remain insufficient because evidence does not establish complete current settlement coverage or physical land/island coverage.

The source-role comparison does not equate ADM2 with an atlas location tier. geoBoundaries describes Nigeria's source as 774 Local Government Areas (2022, CC BY 4.0), Senegal's as 45 departments (2019, CC BY 3.0 IGO), and Sierra Leone's as 14 districts (2017, CC BY 3.0 IGO). Current retained OCHA COD-AB snapshots have 774 Nigerian local government areas, 46 Senegalese departments and 16 Sierra Leone ADM2 units. OCHA describes these respectively as valid for humanitarian use from 17 April 2019, 20 May 2024 and 15 December 2023; the metadata was modified 14 August 2026 and reviewed 30 October 2025. The newer Senegal and Sierra Leone counts do not by themselves establish replacement mappings. Every positive-area legacy/current intersection pair is retained in `source-vintage-crosswalk.csv` (1,340 rows); largest overlap is labeled a screen, never selected as a transfer. `province-scale-screen.csv` compares every current candidate ADM2 footprint with its current ADM1 parent and complete-country ADM2 sibling count, so province-sized and repeated-tier candidates are exposed for review without assuming that an administrative level is an atlas tier.

`current-unit-inventory.csv` enumerates every current COD-AB ADM2 unit across the three countries (774/46/16), including outside-scope remainder units, parents, codes, validity fields, component counts and available capital-row counts. `national-source-inventory.csv` accounts for each entire country source and every current unit whose normalized name has no pinned legacy counterpart; Senegal's additional current candidate is Keur Massar (pcode SN0105, parent Dakar, COD-AB `valid_on` 2024-05-20). That is a source crosswalk question, not an automatic addition to the published atlas. `geometry-components.csv` lists all 258 components of every candidate polygon. These administrative-source components are not proof of named islands or complete physical land coverage. `settlement-source-counts.csv`, `settlement-point-anomalies.csv` and `city-name-spatial-screen.csv` retain the full settlement evidence screens and zero-count subjects. WCA place records are from a regional source dated in the ancestor packet's catalog at 21 April 2026; its point layer reports `last_modif` values through 2021. They are not a census or proof of settlement completeness.

The province-scale screen's clearest candidate is Sierra Leone's Western Area Rural: it covers about 88.16% of the OCHA ADM1 Western Area parent polygon, while the countrywide ADM2 inventory has two sibling units under that parent (Western Area Rural and Western Area Urban). This is a deliberate source/role review flag, not a finding that Rural should be promoted, dissolved or transferred. The ADM1 source comparison also records larger COD-AB versus edge-matched WCA overlays for Adamawa (397.36 km² symmetric difference), Kédougou (130.60 km²), Cross River (128.89 km²), North Western in Sierra Leone (125.34 km²), Sokoto (112.84 km²) and Katsina (108.85 km²). The source-relative IoU values and every other parent comparison are in `admin1-source-comparison.csv`; they are differences between two OCHA source products, not established geographic errors. A source-history and affected-neighbor review is needed before considering any shared edge change.

Nigeria's COD-AB current ADM2 codes do not match the WCA point-layer ADM2 codes. The packet therefore retains exact-code counts separately from the second-tier normalized ADM2+ADM1 name candidate join in `assessment.csv`, `settlement-source-counts.csv` and `geometry-components.csv`. The name+parent join is explicitly a crosswalk candidate and does not become an exact administrative code match. Coordinates were checked against WCA `LAT`/`LONG`; the GeoJSON point geometry, not the inconsistently named attributes, drives spatial screens. Source-vintage and name ambiguities remain unresolved where applicable.

Every current COD-AB candidate's disconnected components are enumerated; the screen attaches source settlement names where points fall within a component, without inferring island identities. A city-name screen searches WCA point names for Lagos, Abuja, Dakar/Pikine/Guédiawaye/Rufisque/Keur Massar and Freetown. It is only a place-name and admin-code presence screen; it does not define city or metropolitan footprints. Senegal's 2017 OCHA settlement points are a separate older-vintage reference. The national Nigeria and Sierra Leone settlement catalogs are labeled `Other` and retained as metadata only. GRID3 Nigeria v4.1 (HDX modified 1 October 2026, CC BY-SA) was too large to lawfully retain within this packet (the catalog resource is 2,136,305,664 bytes); GRID3 Senegal v3.0's resource route returned 403; Sierra Leone route/access was not established. These limitations leave settlement completeness unresolved.

The physical source screen remains incomplete. The ancestor Togo/Sierra Leone packet records the NOAA GSHHG information page as reachable but its linked data directory returning HTTP 404; see `data/regional-review/regional-review-9d08839e1cdb0c8f/physical-source-access.json` in the ancestor commit. This packet makes no independent physical coastline, inland-water, omitted-land or named-island completeness finding. Restore a current lawful land/island reference and complete a feature-level comparison before treating those questions as settled. Three country-specific follow-ups (#749 Nigeria, #750 Senegal, #751 Sierra Leone) were created with exact subject scopes, evidence-quality contracts and a dependency on this review. They remain unready until the parent packet closes and triage confirms the scope; `follow-up-recommendations.json` records the issue URLs and gaps.

`neighbor-edge-screen.csv` checks Nigeria against Benin, Niger, Chad and Cameroon; Senegal against Mauritania, Mali, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau and The Gambia; and Sierra Leone against Guinea and Liberia. It finds same-source OCHA WCA 2026 shared edges and only negligible overlay artifacts; it does not show an inter-region inconsistency warranting a unilateral boundary change. Any future discrepancy in these shared edges must be handled as a coordinated neighboring-region follow-up, not an edit here. `admin1-source-comparison.csv` records all 56 country-admin1 comparisons against the same WCA regional source, including differences for human review; those are diagnostic source comparisons, not corrections or approvals.

## Sources, retention and limits

`sources/acquisition.json` is the canonical per-source register: source IDs, canonical catalog/download URLs, resolved retrieval routes, catalog modification times, issue-pinned roles and vintages, source-reported dates, license, response headers, original byte counts and SHA-256, plus lawful compressed retained-file hashes and byte counts. The WCA archive references are retained in the ancestor packet; `sources/inherited-wca-register.json` gives its stable HDX routes, source dates/license, ancestor archive hashes, subset byte counts and SHA-256. Restore a retained `.gz` original with `gzip -dc INPUT.gz > OUTPUT`; confirm the restored byte length and SHA-256 in its register. `context-extract.json` records exact ancestor archive paths, hashes, filters, output byte counts and hashes, and GDAL version for the WCA point and neighbor subsets. `senegal-settlement-extract.json` records the 2017 archive and extracted byte hashes, feature count, unfiltered national extent and GDAL version. No license-`Other` catalog rows or oversized/blocked GRID3 bytes are redistributed.

The three pinned geoBoundaries feature collections and current geoBoundaries catalog responses are both retained. The latter use a mutable `/api/current/` endpoint and are explicitly not treated as immutable metadata for the pinned release commit. OCHA catalog `metadata_modified` dates are not source-validity dates; reported humanitarian validity, review date, geometry vintage and retrieval date are kept distinct. The active issue scope and claim receipt are retained as `issue-metadata.json`, `issue-scope-pinned.json` and `claim-receipt.json`; `scope.json` hashes the actual ancestor baseline snapshots and names ancestor commit `e570f16236928fd5c5e359ab96e81399b55ccb26`.

No shared hierarchy, geometry, canonical grid, application/schema, certificate or live geography was edited. No political ownership, administrative remainder, historical attribute, land/island completeness, region approval or historical-import eligibility is asserted. This packet supplies bounded evidence and leaves all 226 members unresolved for the complete regional integration.

## Reproduction

The scripts write only inside this owned packet directory. `acquire_sources.py` records/retrieves the named source vintages and catalog snapshots; license-`Other` sources are metadata-only. `extract_context.py` and `extract_senegal_settlements.py` reproduce licensed subset outputs from their retained original archives. `generated-data-accounting.json` separately inventories every generated CSV's exact rows, bytes and SHA-256; the generated tables contain 5,513 evidence rows across the exact 226-subject scope and complete source/context screens. Original source archives and subset bytes are separately accounted in their acquisition/extraction receipts. These complete per-subject and source crosswalk tables explain why the packet exceeds the soft 1,000-line target. Then run:

```sh
python3 data/regional-review/regional-review-0ed7b50d2b74736e/build_assessment.py
python3 data/regional-review/regional-review-0ed7b50d2b74736e/verify.py
```

The geometry method uses EPSG:6933 for area/intersection and WGS84 geodesic length for edge measurements. Name normalization is Unicode NFKD, ASCII-folded, lower-case alphanumeric; it proposes candidates only. Point-in-polygon uses source GeoJSON point coordinates. All outputs are reproducible measurements/screens from the cited source vintages, not evidence of complete administrative or physical reality.
