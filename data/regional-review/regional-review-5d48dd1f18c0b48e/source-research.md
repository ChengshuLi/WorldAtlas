## Benin

The exact pinned source is geoBoundaries v5 `BEN-ADM2`, whose retained metadata records boundary year 2007, canonical role `Commune`, source Map Maker Ltd./Stanford Earthworks, Public Domain terms, and 77 features. All 77 scoped source `shapeID` values and names map exactly to the issue's 77 Benin member IDs and atlas names. Every retained source geometry is a valid, single Polygon. The official INSAE *Tableau de Bord Social 2011* describes 12 departments, 77 communes, 546 arrondissements and 4,386 villages/urban quarters, and lists the twelve departments; the atlas's generic `province` parent tier is therefore a grouping tier, not a claim that Benin's administrative unit is literally called a province.

I downloaded and inspected a separate geoBoundaries `BEN-ADM1` department reference layer, boundary year 2012, Public Domain according to its retained metadata. Each of the 77 commune geometries has its maximum overlap with the department name currently used as its atlas parent. This supports the broad group crosswalk. That layer is five years later than the communes, is from a different source lineage, and does not settle their exact shared 2007 boundaries. Thirty-three commune geometries have less than 95% WGS84 area overlap with the named 2012 department; 95% is only a screening threshold, not a correctness rule. Three of those also have less than 95% source-to-current-atlas footprint overlap. These 33 rows are classified `insufficient-evidence` pending a same-vintage, official membership/boundary crosswalk; no parent reassignment or geometry correction is asserted.

The 2011 INSAE report names the official departments as Alibori, Atacora, Atlantique, Borgou, Collines, Couffo, Donga, Littoral, Mono, Ouémé, Plateau and Zou. Four current atlas IDs need an engineering name/alias decision while preserving IDs and members: `framework:province:atakora:167d1c8e020b` (Atakora/Atacora), `framework:province:atlanique:de71c1be64ff` (Atlanique/Atlantique), `framework:province:kouffo:b9df90330619` (Kouffo/Couffo), and `framework:province:oueme:ee7fc244e72e` (ASCII Oueme/official Ouémé). The accent omission may be house style; it should not be silently treated as a geographic correction.

## Burkina Faso

The exact pinned source is geoBoundaries v5 `BFA-ADM3`, boundary year 2007, with 351 features. Its `boundaryCanonical` metadata field is blank, so the source metadata alone does not establish the territorial role. The inspected Stanford record served through UT Austin Libraries identifies the exact January 2007 Map Maker polygon layer as `Communes`, level 3, public domain, with no use restrictions. The official INSD *Annuaire statistique 2007: Administration du territoire* identifies Table 01.01 as departments by province and region and Table 01.03 as communes by province; the official source therefore distinguishes the administrative department record from commune-level local-government units. This supports treating the layer as communes rather than guessing from `ADM3` or the matching count of 351. The parent layer from geoBoundaries `BFA-ADM2` is a separate 2017 World Bank source, 45 provinces, CC BY 4.0, and is used only as a later-vintage spatial cross-check.

All 147 scoped source IDs and names match exactly, the source geometries are valid single Polygons, and each one overlaps its current atlas province parent by at least 99.9938% in the 2017 reference layer; no member's highest-overlap province name differs from its atlas parent. The remaining 204 of the 351 source communes are outside this packet's declared location scope. The official Ministry of Territorial Administration described a later 2025 reorganization to 17 regions, 47 provinces and 350 departments, so contemporary counts/names must not be projected backward onto the 2007 polygons.

Two scoped Burkina footprints (`Niangologo`, `Kando`) have less than 95% source-to-current-atlas area overlap. The current source metadata says they were topology-reconciled and retains conflict counts, but it does not reproduce the operation or explain these exact differences. Both remain `insufficient-evidence` for exact current-footprint provenance. The overlap screen does not prove that either geometry is wrong.

## Neighboring granularity

Within the same fixed West Africa region, the adjacent-country input screen includes Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Mali, Niger, Nigeria and Togo, with local units published as districts, cercles, communes, local government areas and prefectures. The Benin and Burkina cohorts are both commune-scale local territories even though their geoBoundaries codes are ADM2 and ADM3 respectively. Their current-footprint median areas are about 537 km² and 578 km²; the neighboring layers vary widely (for example, Togo prefectures and Mali cercles are much larger), so tier codes and raw polygon size cannot replace country-specific source-role evidence. Exact inputs and WGS84 measurement method are in `neighbor-granularity-screen.json`.

## Coverage, granularity and boundary limits

- Benin source metadata says 77 ADM2 communes and the packet contains the full set of 77. Burkina source metadata says 351 ADM3 units; the complete regional inventory contains 351 BFA source identities, while this packet owns only the 147 issue-pinned ones. These count comparisons establish alignment to the source roster, not independent proof that either source covers every legally recognized land area.
- All 224 scoped native source geometries are valid, single-part polygons; no scoped location is an intra-city subdivision, province-sized unit, multipart fragment, or anonymous remainder according to the inspected source names and source roles. The units are local municipal/admin territories beneath the cited parent group, not new geographic units created by this packet.
- Benin borders the Gulf of Guinea; Burkina Faso is landlocked. The retained source layers do not establish exhaustive small-island, lagoon-islet or shoreline coverage. No named independent island/territory omission was found in the sources inspected, but the packet does not certify exhaustive coastal land coverage.
- No source-backed correction is currently established, so `correction-needed` count is zero. The complete row-by-row classifications and unresolved boundary findings are in `subject-assessments.json`.
- The West Africa macro envelope, stable member IDs, published geometry and location attributes were not edited. This packet does not approve the region, release, or historical imports.
