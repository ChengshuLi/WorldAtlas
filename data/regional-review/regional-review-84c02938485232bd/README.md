Audit date: 2026-10-03. Scope: all 265 pinned location IDs in `issue-scope.json`. The issue covers Bogotá D.C., Cesar, Meta, Santander, and Boyacá. This evidence packet makes no hierarchy, geometry, grid, certificate, ownership, or historical-attribute changes. ## Issue #580 correction: coordinate axis policy

The pre-correction geometry report treated GeoJSON coordinates as if EPSG:4326 authority axis order and GeoJSON x/y order were interchangeable. They are not: GeoJSON positions are longitude, latitude. `reproduce-geometry.py` now explicitly sets `OAMS_TRADITIONAL_GIS_ORDER` for EPSG:4326 and EPSG:6933, evaluates the immutable baseline commit `6c6271af6b0dac49b82eaafb2db4de8b9c4611f2`, and uses the same pinned 265 IDs and original 2020 geoBoundaries source bytes. Exact old outputs and the old script remain byte-for-byte in `superseded/axis-order-v1/`, with SHA-256 records; they are historical, not current measurements.

The independent control transforms (-73°, 5°) to EPSG:6933 at approximately (-7,043,498.458 m, 637,078.744 m), matching pyproj `always_xy=True` within 0.001 m. The authority-axis negative control is about 10,735 km away. A one-degree cell geodesic-area control differs from EPSG:6933 by 0.00251%, within the 0.2% model tolerance; five large real features also agree between WGS84 Geod and EPSG:6933 within 0.000161%. GDAL OSR/API and pyproj references, versions, retrieval metadata, and page hashes are recorded in `axis-order-correction.json`; their HTML bytes are not retained because redistribution terms were not established.

Corrected results: 262 of 265 source/current overlays are valid; three current shapes are invalid (`Agustín Codazzi`, `La Jagua de Ibirico`, `Curumaní`). `Curumaní` is newly classified `insufficient-evidence` because correct-axis validity checking exposes its self-intersection. The resulting full scope classes are 259 justified, 3 correction-needed municipality name variants, and 3 insufficient-evidence geometry cases. No other classification changed. Corrected maximum relative area difference is 5.8065%; maximum valid-overlay symmetric difference is 14.3572%. The same five subjects remain the largest symmetric differences in the same rank order, with corrected exact values in `axis-order-correction.json`. Parent union symmetric differences range from 0.2595% (Meta) to 2.5839% (Bogotá D.C.); every department's corrected figures are in `department-union-comparison.json`. These measurements are alignment diagnostics only; no boundary correction is proposed.

## Findings

* All 265 assigned subjects are direct Colombia ADM2 municipality features. Every shape ID exists in the pinned 2020 geoBoundaries source, has source tier ADM2, is a single Polygon, and maps uniquely to a 2024 DANE DIVIPOLA entry. DANE’s current department code/name roster gives the complete scoped unit counts: Bogotá D.C. 1, Cesar 25, Meta 29, Santander 87, Boyacá 123. Counts total 265 and exactly match issue scope. Each of the 265 subjects is individually classified: 259 justified, 3 correction-needed (municipality name variants), and 3 insufficient-evidence (invalid geometry overlays). All DANE municipality types in scope are `Municipio`; no municipalized-area remainder was assigned here.
* The 261 routine normalized name matches and four department/name crosswalk exceptions are individually recorded (three location-name variants plus the Bogotá parent-label alias) in `audit.jsonl`. The three municipality variants have unique DANE DIVIPOLA codes but require name review: `Becerrill` → `Becerril` (20045), `Jordán Sube` → `Jordán` (68370), and `Güicán` → `Güicán de la Sierra` (15332). These are proposals only. Separately, the Bogotá parent assessment flags “Bogota Capital District” for a canonical-name review against DANE’s “Bogotá, D.C.”; the location name itself matches DANE after case/punctuation normalization. The ADM1 grouping is an organizational level and should not be taken to make Bogotá a department. See `parent-assessments.json` for all five assigned parents.
* DANE’s 2024 official DIVIPOLA Centros Poblados roster yields 1,015 assigned center records: 265 `CM` (cabeceras municipales) and 750 `CP` (centros poblados). Every assigned municipality has its CM record and each individual row has the code/name/category counts in `audit.jsonl`. These records support a sourced settlement assessment; they do not establish completeness of villages, dispersed households, unlisted settlements, or historical settlement status.
* Source and current indexed geometries are each single Polygon for all 265 IDs. No detached municipality geometry or island-specific municipality was identified in this scoped set. This is not proof that every island, river island, small land feature, or hydrological component is represented inside each polygon.
* Parent chains are present for every subject: location → the assigned department-like `framework:province` group → Colombia area → Western South America region → Andean South America subcontinent → South America continent. This follows the published baseline and does not verify the legal or exact geometry of those envelopes. The current ADM1 boundary metadata cites 2017 OpenStreetMap/Wambacher (ODbL 1.0); the DANE DIVIPOLA roster validates department names/counts, not that old parent geometry. geoBoundaries ADM2 cites DANE MGN 2020, CC BY 4.0.
* Corrected longitude-first EPSG:6933 comparison finds relative area differences up to 5.8065% among all 265 areas, and symmetric-difference fractions up to 14.3572% among 262 valid overlays. Three current geometries remain invalid for overlay (`Agustín Codazzi`, `La Jagua de Ibirico`, `Curumaní`); `Curumaní` is the newly exposed geometry uncertainty and is explicitly classified insufficient-evidence. Parent unions differ by −2.3267% to +12.1094% in area and by 0.2595%–2.5839% in symmetric-difference fraction. Union calculations use `MakeValid` on local clones for invalid features; they do not change source or published geometries. The same five largest symmetric differences remain La Victoria, Palmar, Jordán Sube, Tununguá, and Iza (13.08–14.36%). These are boundary-alignment uncertainties between generalized application geometry and the detailed 2020 source, not proof of erroneous area or a gap in the official administrative roster. A topology-safe neighbor review is recommended before claiming a gap-free municipal tessellation.
* The Colombia area is partial in this packet: 265 of 1,122 members. Issue scopes #490–#495 partition the area into 94 + 190 + 194 + 262 + 265 + 117 Colombia IDs; the retained ID crosswalk verifies these scopes are disjoint and cover all 1,122 published area descendants. Issue #495 also owns 111 Ecuador members outside this comparison. This is a workload partition, not an atlas boundary claim. The five department counts reconcile with DANE’s roster, but this cannot prove no missing physical land, exact shared borders, or completeness at island/settlement scales. No inter-region inconsistency is asserted. Colombia’s outer macro boundary is the published baseline and is not changed here; sovereignty and historical territorial meaning are not inferred from current reference-owner fields.
