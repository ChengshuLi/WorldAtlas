## Uncertainty and handoff

The lower TIGERweb IoUs for several Gulf-coast counties are consistent with changes in vintage, cartographic generalization, coastline detail and offshore representation. The sources do not attribute any individual difference to a specific island, inlet, water claim, legal change or error. Neither the 2018 generalized comparator nor the 2026 statistical service proves legal boundary accuracy or exhaustive coastal/island coverage. The county boundary disposition for all six subjects remains unresolved. Do not replace the Atlas geometry from these IoUs alone.

No canonical edit, import, publication, regional approval, or engineering-code change is proposed. A future sourced decision needs dated reusable state-authoritative descriptions or survey/GIS evidence for the six counties and shared neighbors, with explicit treatment of land, inland/coastal water, islands and offshore jurisdiction; source role, CRS, vintage, license and coverage must be checked before any boundary proposal. The broader #1149 work remains separate and does not expand this six-subject scope.
