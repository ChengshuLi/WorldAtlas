# Croatia batch-4 reproduction erratum (#1194)

Retrieved 2026-10-06. This is an exact-scope reproducibility and preservation repair for the 224 existing #419 subjects. It does not validate or certify Croatian boundaries, Atlas parent assignments, all Croatia, or the regional packet.

## Subject and territorial meaning

The issue scope is the immutable list of 224 existing geoBoundaries HRV ADM2-backed Atlas local-government locations, contained in `data/geography/part-10.json` at the reviewed baseline. They are assessed against the 2021 Croatian town/municipality roster in the eight counties declared by #419. In Croatia, “town” and “municipality” are local self-government units; county and City of Zagreb are regional self-government units. The [2021 Census report](https://podaci.dzs.hr/2021/en/39858) says the census reference time was 31 August 2021 and its territorial framework was based on the DGU Register of Spatial Units. The census reports 21 counties (including Zagreb), 128 towns and 428 municipalities nationally. These counts are contextual, not polygon or parent evidence.

The [Territorial Organization Act](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2006_07_86_2045.html) describes statutory names, seats and area definitions and the legal procedure for territorial changes; it also references cadastral-municipality/settlement boundaries. It is a 2006 legal text and this packet does not claim it is a consolidated statement of law in 2026 or that its text supplies machine-readable 2021 polygons.

The new runner checks those Git object bytes, the complete workbooks/extract/geometry bytes, the pinned raw issue REST snapshot and complete scope JSON hashes (cross-checking embedded issue metadata), and exact subject identities before creating any output.

The original 20,788,018-byte, 560-feature geoBoundaries HRV ADM2 collection has SHA-256 `68a317129a0c295fd8baf7acc0f31ffefdf65ff2f1f62f8ed90a765cc57cf01e`; it is retained read-only in the prior packet, with the same bytes verified against upstream. Metadata calls 2021 a representative year while reporting a 2023-01-19 source update, so it is not a proven legal boundary snapshot at census reference time. Its upstream metadata claims CC BY-SA 2.0 and references OpenStreetMap. The exact redistributability of the geometry is unresolved; this erratum does not duplicate it and preserves the existing exact-source restoration location and hash instead.

DZS states that its published datasets are reusable without restriction under the Croatian Open License, subject to attribution ([DZS Open Data](https://dzs.gov.hr/o-zavodu/pravo-na-pristup-informacijama/otvoreni-podaci/1812)). The full original detailed workbook is not in this packet; its prior lawful extract and explicit restoration instructions remain the available evidence. The DZS roster establishes names, types and county labels, not coordinates or boundary completeness.

DGU currently lists an anonymous ATOM service for INSPIRE Administrative Units and describes open-data reuse on its [Open Data page](https://dgu.gov.hr/proizvodi-i-usluge/otvoreni-podaci/6596?big=0) and [Geoportal services page](https://geoportal.dgu.hr/cms/podaci-i-servisi/). The service response itself was not successfully retrieved for this work; no claim is made about layer contents, completeness, date, or the terms attached to a particular geometry distribution. DGU’s [Register of Spatial Units page](https://dgu.gov.hr/registar-prostornih-jedinica-172/172) describes registered unit types and formats and cites current implementing rules. This is a lead for the separately blocked historical boundary/lineage work in #1028, not evidence that resolves it.

## Findings preserved, with limits

The two historical reproduction runs in `evidence/run-1` and `evidence/run-2` produce byte-identical versions of the original three reports. At the fixed historical baseline, the 224 issue members resolve; the source has 560 distinct shapes; 220 rows have name/type matches when grouped by the existing Atlas parent’s county mapping; four labels remain sourced correction candidates; seven DZS local-unit rows remain unmatched in the eight counties; and 13 scoped rows have geometry-type differences. The county mapping is inherited from the Atlas baseline and is not independently validated. Name/type agreement does not establish parent correctness. A geometry-type difference or component count is not boundary evidence.

The 2021 census roster is finer than county reporting but does not expose official municipal polygon coordinates in the retained evidence. No same-vintage official boundary set was acquired and compared, and no coastal/island completeness conclusion is supported. The four candidate label changes remain engineering follow-ups; this erratum itself does not modify core geography or source reports.
