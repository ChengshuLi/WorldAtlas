## Geometry lineage finding

Every scoped Atlas record links to the unique matching pinned source `shapeID`, and all 228 names match. However, only 3 Atlas geometries equal their source geometry after rounding the source coordinates to four decimal places. The other 225 have fewer vertices: 12,905 Atlas vertices versus 14,634 source vertices, a reduction of 1,729 vertices (11.8%). All 228 source and Atlas geometries are `Polygon`; 226 have one ring and 2 have one interior ring, a structural comparison only. This establishes that the scoped Atlas geometries are materially transformed relative to the retained geoBoundaries file. It does not establish why they were transformed or whether any transformed polygon is wrong. No multi-part geometry is encoded in these 228 source or Atlas features. That does not show that an administrative territory has no detached islands or remote pieces; omitted parts cannot be detected without an authoritative reference. This packet did not run polygon validity, cross-boundary gap/overlap, shoreline, or completeness comparisons. The evidence lacks a pinned transformation recipe, a per-feature geometry-change receipt, a present-day authoritative boundary reference, and topology/completeness comparisons against that reference. The crosswalk records the IDs and vertex counts so engineering can trace the exact rows.
