# Anatolia and Eastern Mediterranean packet 1 (issue #71)

This directory is a source and semantic review for the exact 226 location IDs and 27 province workloads pinned on issue #71. It is not a geographic edit, region approval, or permission to import location attributes. The Turkey scope is partial: 198 of 911 location members. The area rollup is Cyprus (10/10), East Aegean Islands (18/18), Turkey (198/911). Province and area rows are workload scopes, not new geographic units.

## Findings

`assessment.csv` has one row per scoped location, with its identity, parent identity/name, retained source feature ID/name/parent, source role/vintage/license as recorded in the baseline or pinned source metadata, geometry component count, equal-area baseline area, source-to-baseline IoU and source coverage, and the individual outcome. Every row is **insufficient-evidence**. Matching the historical source or a current administrative name does not establish a complete current boundary or that the unit is the right atlas tier. No location is marked justified from its ADM level or structural presence alone.

Three source findings need distinct treatment:

1. Pinned geoBoundaries TUR ADM2 release commit `9469f09592ced973a3448cf66b6100b741b64c0d` has 973 GeoJSON features, but its pinned metadata says `admUnitCount: 999`. All 197 Turkey IDs in this packet match a `shapeID`; that does not establish source completeness. The 26-feature difference is not localized by the pinned metadata or file, and this packet's 197-unit subset cannot resolve a nationwide source discrepancy. The licensed HGM product is expressly an indicative display product and nonofficial, so it cannot repair that gap. Keep a separate nationwide source-restoration/crosswalk follow-up open.
2. Cyprus's six district names/count agree between the pinned geoBoundaries CYP ADM1 package and the official DLS district service. Yet Kyrenia and Famagusta have very low baseline/source intersection-over-union (about 0.017 and 0.110); Nicosia is 0.616. The official DLS service similarly has low IoU for those three (about 0.017, 0.105, 0.600). These are meaningful spatial differences, not proof of which boundary is legally or semantically correct. The public DLS catalogue identifies the layer as districts; its underlying official dataset describes an administrative-boundaries product with 2016 temporal coverage and a CC BY 4.0 license. The exact service snapshot used here is current as retrieved 2026-10-04; a lawful, dated authoritative crosswalk that addresses the divided territorial context remains needed. Keep a bounded six-district reconciliation follow-up open; do not infer a political-status outcome from geometry.
3. The Greek ADM3 source says `municipality`, boundary year 2010, but its license metadata is internally mixed: `boundaryLicense` says CC0 while `licenseDetail` attributes some underlying sources as CC BY 3.0. All 19 assigned Greek source IDs match their pinned `shapeID`s. Comparison with ELSTAT's official 2021 census municipality/regional-unit files yields multiple current municipality intersections for Samos and Lesbos and imperfect shape agreement across the set. ELSTAT explicitly cautions that its census boundaries are for statistical use and do not prove legal administrative boundaries. Keep this as a time-vintage crosswalk screen, not an identity transfer or legal-boundary test.
4. The official Ministry of Culture and Tourism, İstanbul Provincial Directorate page lists 39 İstanbul districts. All 39 names join uniquely to distinct 2021 geoBoundaries Turkey ADM2 `shapeID`s (the source calls Adalar “Prince Islands”). Seven exact IDs are present under the Atlas İstanbul province parent; 32 are absent from the full current Atlas baseline. The seven present IDs are Şile, Kartal, Pendik, Sancaktepe, Sultanbeyli, Tuzla and Prince Islands. This supports **correction-needed for the İstanbul province scope** because the roster is incomplete. Each of the seven assigned location outcomes remains insufficient-evidence until its full geographic context and source crosswalk are settled. The missing 32 source IDs are candidates for a separate controlled correction review, not permission to create or transfer Atlas IDs. The pinned ADM2 features encode `shapeGroup=TUR` only; the immediate province relationship comes from the official İstanbul-specific district list, not the feature itself. Follow-up #821 owns that exact 39-subject reconciliation and is blocked on #71, #72 and #817.

The four Natural Earth records are distinct map-unit portrayals. Natural Earth 5.1.1 calls Akrotiri and Dhekelia dependencies with the United Kingdom as sovereign; it depicts Northern Cyprus as a “Sovereign country” map-unit type (a dataset label, not an international legal determination), and the Cyprus U.N. Buffer Zone as “Indeterminate.” This source is cartographic/de-facto reference, not a legal boundary authority. The buffer-zone map unit has a stronger geographic description than the Atlas label “Cyprus No Mans Area”; change proposals must preserve neutral status language pending the appropriate sourced engineering review.

`scope-assessment.csv` accounts for all 3 area scopes and 27 provinces. All three areas and the other 26 provinces are insufficient-evidence because their purpose/completeness requires broader regional evidence. İstanbul is correction-needed on the 39-versus-7 roster gap above. `province-scale-screen.csv` records per-province owned/full roster sizes and the largest-to-median member area ratio without pretending a ratio is a tier rule. The largest observed ratios are Rhodes within Notioy Aigaioy (23.70), Şile within İstanbul (11.94), Lesbos within Voreioy Aigaioy (6.08), Bergama within İzmir (4.20), and Acıpayam within Denizli (3.84). These expose scale contrasts for semantic review, not automatic errors. `istanbul-completeness.csv` contains the 39-row exact source-ID/baseline crosswalk and each missing district name. `greece-2021-crosswalk-screen.csv` records every legacy geometry intersecting a 2021 municipality or regional unit above the declared 1% legacy-area threshold. A largest-overlap candidate is not selected as a parent.

`geometry-components.csv` enumerates each separate polygon part for the 226 baseline locations: 17 of 226 are multipart (7 GRC, 4 CYP, 4 TUR and 2 Natural Earth). `neighbor-edge-screen.csv` tests the whole retained 973-feature Turkish source layer: 194 scoped districts have at least one same-layer shared edge over 0.1 m; Bozcaada, Marmara and Prince Islands have none. It finds no pairwise polygon intersection over 1,000 m² among scoped districts. The unshared perimeter metric includes ordinary coastline/island edges and any source gaps, so these values do not establish missing coverage or adjacency quality. The packet has no independent city/settlement polygons; district geometry components and size screens cannot certify urban-fragment completeness.

## Geometry, neighbors and limits

Reproduction uses Shapely 2.1.2 and pyproj 3.7.2 in a private environment, `EPSG:6933` for regional baseline-to-source polygon comparison and `EPSG:2100` for the Greek census screen. Invalid input geometries are repaired with GEOS `make_valid` for measurement only. IoU is intersection / union; source area coverage is intersection / source area. Parent identity in the pinned Atlas tree is preserved, but complete province geometries are not in this packet's baseline files, so parent containment/coverage cannot be calculated here. Component count exposes multipart geometries; it does not identify omitted islands. Equal-area size is only a scale screen. Names, extents, complete settlement/city coverage, omissions, legal status and tier purpose still need authoritative evidence.

The packet does not have an independently validated, complete settlement gazetteer or physical island inventory for Cyprus, Greece and Turkey. The source count discrepancy, current administrative reorganizations, differing Cyprus portrayals and incomplete parent geometry mean source roster membership cannot be called complete. This packet makes no geo-boundary edits, footprint edits, ID transfers, hierarchy changes, or administrative claims.

The HGM product page describes a freely downloadable ESRI shapefile but limits reuse/distribution to noncommercial use with attribution. It explicitly calls country/province/district borders indicative, derived from varied sources, and not official; it says legal delineation/publication is governed by Law 5442. TURKSTAT's administrative-register metadata says provinces, districts, municipalities, villages and quarters use national administrative divisions as of December 31 and are based on administrative attachments and changes. These official descriptions support “district” as a current administrative role, but they do not make the open geoBoundaries geometry an authoritative current boundary source. HGM geometry was not retained because the proposed follow-up needs an explicit reuse review and the product's limited license.

Traceable authoritative source pages used for source role and limitations:

- [HGM Turkey municipal-admin-boundaries product terms](https://www.harita.gov.tr/urun/turkiye-mulki-idare-sinirlari/232) and [HGM announcement](https://www.harita.gov.tr/haber/ulkemize-ait-ulke-il-ve-ilce-sinirlari-ile-il-ve-ilce-yerlesim-noktalarini-iceren-vektor-veri-ile-il-ve-ilce-yuz-olcumleri-bilgisi-internet-sitemizde-ucretsiz-olarak-kullanima-sunulmustur/168): product is for display, indicative and expressly nonofficial; reuse is noncommercial with attribution.
