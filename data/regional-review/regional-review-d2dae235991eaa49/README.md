# Western South America interior batch 8: Peru (#497)

Audit date: 2026-10-03. Scope: all 89 location IDs pinned by issue #497; see `issue-scope.json`, `audit.json`, and `peer-scope-496.json`. This is a review evidence packet. It makes no hierarchy, geometry, grid, certificate, ownership, or historical claim changes.

## Findings

* Every subject has an individual sourced assessment and a complete current parent chain in `audit.json`. The 78 `gb:PER:ADM2` subjects are matched against their source shape ID, country, tier, and normalized name in the retained complete 196-feature source. INEI’s administrative table, updated 2019-12-31, independently lists the province counts for each assigned department: Huancavelica 7, Loreto 8, Ayacucho 11, Ucayali 4, Apurímac 7, Arequipa 8, Cusco 13, Moquegua 3, Madre de Dios 3, Tacna 4, and Puno 13. Ten department child sets match their count and administrative role. These sources support identity/granularity, not exact equivalence of the generalized current geometry.
* Eleven Loreto children are portions derived from five RESOLVE ecological ecoregions, yet the stored `source_role` is `Provinces` and the selection reason calls them provinces. They are not independent province records. They link to three original ADM2 identities (Loreto, Maynas, Requena); INEI lists eight Loreto provinces total. The other five Loreto children are whole ADM2 features. The assignment therefore presents 16 child locations for eight official provinces. Recommend a bounded correction/follow-up to decide the ecological-fragment location grain, fix misleading role/reason metadata, and check exact coverage. Coordinate with sibling #496 and integration issue #489; do not adjust shared boundaries here.
* The three ADM2 source polygons linked by the 11 ecological fragments have near-equal aggregate areas to the fragments in a projected comparison (Loreto 0.99995806, Maynas 1.00008992, Requena 0.99927253 of source area). This is an area-mass diagnostic only: it does not prove gap-free/non-overlapping coverage. Current map geometries are generalized; direct ADM2 comparisons also show small area differences, and one topology overlay fails. Thus exact spatial union, shared boundary alignment, and component correspondence remain unresolved.
* Puno, Tacna, Tahuamanu, and Yunguyo source ADM2 features are multipart (3, 3, 2, and 3 components). The administrative source identifies one unit per feature; component names and whether components are islands, river islands, or other detached mainland pieces are unresolved. The 11 ecological portions collectively contain 420 geometry components, not 420 locations. No authoritative land/island gazetteer was found in this packet to identify every component.
* The subjects are administrative polygons or ecological fragments, not settlement points. Their sources do not establish settlement completeness or the presence/absence of communities. `reference_owner=Peru` is a present-day reference attribute and is not treated as legal sovereignty or historical ownership. The Peru macro area is the published WGSRPD-derived baseline; this work does not revise it. Antarctica is outside this scope.
* The parent chain in the currently published baseline is location → department-like `framework:province` → Peru area → Western South America region → Andean South America subcontinent → South America continent. The current province grouping cites GeoBoundaries PER ADM1, whose retained metadata says 2009, OpenStreetMap/Wambacher, ODbL 1.0; this is weaker and older than the ADM2 child source and does not validate a current authoritative department boundary. Parent footprints remain bottom-up from member locations.
* The 89 #497 IDs and 115 Peru-area IDs in sibling #496 are disjoint and together exhaust all 204 current Peru-area descendants. #496 also has 113 IDs outside Peru (Ecuador/Galápagos); they were not reviewed here. This establishes workload partition only, not geographic correctness.
