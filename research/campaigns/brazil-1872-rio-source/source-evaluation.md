# Brazil 1872 census: Rio de Janeiro source evaluation

## Finding

IBGE Biblioteca hosts the official digital copy of *Recenseamento do Brazil em 1872*. Its catalogue identifies the work as twelve volumes and assigns volume 10 to Rio de Janeiro; Município Neutro is separately assigned to volume 5. This is a useful source lead for Province of Rio de Janeiro and prevents conflating it with the separately published Município Neutro tables.

The reviewed volume is a scanned, 360-page PDF. A visually inspected page headed “PROVINCIA DO RIO DE JANEIRO” and “QUADRO GERAL DA POPULAÇÃO DA PAROCHIA DE SANTO ANTONIO DOS GUARULHOS” displays a multi-attribute parish table, including sex, free/slave condition, race, marital status, religion, nationality, instruction, physical defects, and houses. The visible historical religion labels are Catholic and non-Catholic forms (“Catholicos/Catholicas” and “Acatholicos/Acatholicas”). A separate table identifies the present population as “População de facto” and has another table for absent population.

The source is promising for source-era religion counts, but this review does **not** conclude that a primary religion can be assigned to a territory. The scanned table’s categories and complete denominator need transcription and reconciliation across each relevant geography, including any foreign-population table and totals. Catholic/non-Catholic labels alone do not establish a modern religious classification or the quality/completeness of response. No numeric row is retained: local OCR corrupts dense table numerals, and visual inspection of the page did not support a defensible transcription set. This satisfies the issue’s maximum of 25 illustrative rows without presenting uncertain counts as facts.

## Source, territorial scope, and dates

The source is *Recenseamento do Brazil em 1872*, published by Typ. G. Leuzinger in Rio de Janeiro and catalogued by IBGE Biblioteca with an uncertain imprint year “[1874?].” The census reference is 1872. The catalog record identifies volume 10 as Rio de Janeiro and volume 5 as Município Neutro. The campaign evaluates only the volume 10 scan. It does not treat Município Neutro as part of the province and makes no modern boundary equivalence.

The source is parish-level on the reviewed form. No parish-to-municipality assignment or historical footprint was evaluated. The census year is explicit in the title, but an exact enumeration day was not established from the reviewed evidence. The source manifest records the exact official URL, retrieval date, bytes, hash, scan metadata, and restoration method.

## Tables, denominator, and method

The reviewed general parish table includes religion among many attributes and splits displayed categories by sex and free/slave condition. The volume also distinguishes present (“de facto”) and absent population, so a total from one table cannot be assumed to be the denominator for another. Counts should be transcribed only from legible source images and checked against the printed totals and matching table headers. This review did not perform that full extraction or denominator check, and therefore retained no illustrative count rows and made no primary-religion claim.

Method: retrieve the official volume 10 PDF; record its byte count and SHA-256; inspect PDF metadata and use text extraction only for locating headings; render and visually inspect the page form. OCR output was rejected as a numeric source because the dense figures were corrupted. The manifest preserves the restoration URL and hash so that later work can repeat or improve the page-level transcription.

It does not establish complete coverage, transcribe a usable count series, map parishes to Atlas geographies, assign Atlas classifications, propose boundaries, or import data.
