# Japan 1920 census population source evaluation

## Finding

The official e-Stat historical collection for the Taishō 9 Census contains a one-page scan of table 2, “面積及人口－府県” (“Area and Population—Prefectures”). The scan lists a national row and source-era prefecture rows. Its national row reports total population 55,963,053, men 28,044,185 and women 27,918,868; the sex columns sum to the printed total. One row is retained as an illustrative source example. No prefecture values have been transcribed.

The collection also identifies table 3, “人口－府県別市部及郡部” (population by prefecture, city and county divisions), and table 4, “人口階級別市町村数及人口－府県” (municipality counts and population by population-size class and prefecture). These headings document that the collection contains finer source-era divisions and size-class products, but those tables have not been inspected or evaluated here.

## Dates, source edition, and scope

The e-Stat record identifies the collection as “大正９年国勢調査” and labels the survey year 1920. This evaluation therefore records a single observation in 1920, not annual data. The exact census day was not confirmed from the inspected listing and table scan. e-Stat lists 2014-06-10 as the PDF's public/update date; that is a digital-catalogue date, not the original report's publication date, which remains unverified.

The evaluated table's displayed units are 全国 (national total) and 府県 (source-era prefectures). The one-page scan does not establish all territorial inclusions or define the population universe. It cannot support assumptions about current Japanese territory, prefecture boundary continuity, settlement totals, or a modern location crosswalk. Its separate city/county table heading reinforces that settlement hierarchy is represented by source-era categories, not interchangeable with modern places or boundaries.

## Variables and method

Table 2 presents area, population total, men, women, women per 100 men, persons per square ri, and persons per 10,000 of national population. Only the national total and sex counts were retained. I downloaded the official PDF, recorded its byte length and SHA-256, rendered the scanned page at high resolution, and manually read the national row. PDF text extraction produced no usable text; OCR was not accepted as a numeric authority. The sex totals reconcile exactly. No independent transcription or full-table audit was made.

## Access and reuse

The e-Stat terms of use permit copying, public transmission, translation, adaptation and commercial use with attribution. Edited or processed content must disclose the editing and must not be represented as if produced by the government. The terms state compatibility with CC BY 4.0. They also caution that some content may have third-party rights; the table record inspected here has no item-specific license field. Cite the Statistics Bureau of Japan's Taishō 9 Census table 2 via e-Stat and identify any transcription as independently prepared.

## Limits and next use

This is a source-only evaluation of one national example from one official census collection. It does not establish an exact census day, a population-universe definition, coverage adjustments, the completeness of all territorial units, a prefecture-to-modern-territory correspondence, or a historical population time series. It makes no location assignments and does not import facts. Any later import would require the target region's complete published approval certificate, exact permitted subjects and matching release pins, plus a focused review of the applicable printed source definitions and territories. Unsupported annual values must not be interpolated.
