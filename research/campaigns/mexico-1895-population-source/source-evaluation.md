# Mexico 1895 census population source evaluation

## Finding

INEGI's public historical-products collection provides the *Censo General de la República Mexicana* under product identifier 702825410100 as three downloadable PDF segments titled *I Censo General de la República Mexicana, 1895. Resumen General*. The original title page names the Dirección General de Estadística, Dr. Antonio Peñafiel, and the Secretaría de Fomento printing office; it states “Verificado el 20 de Octubre de 1895.” The official INEGI program metadata independently gives the same observation date and describes an exhaustive enumeration.

The one table evaluated here is “Población por sexos” on printed page 6. It is a summary by named source-era states, grouped under the report's regional headings, with columns for men, women, total, and persons aged 0–1 year by sex and total. The national “Sumas” row gives 6,190,311 men, 6,301,262 women, and 12,491,573 total; the sex counts add to the printed total. The Aguascalientes row prints 50,514 men, 51,834 women, and 102,378 total. The sex counts add to 102,348, leaving a 30-person discrepancy. It is preserved as printed and not corrected or propagated.

The following printed page begins “Población por edades.” This source review does not extract the age table or any of the later attribute tables. Its examples remain source-level evidence and are not assigned to current boundaries, municipal units, or Atlas subjects.

## Dates, source edition, and scope

Observation date: 20 October 1895, stated on the original title page and INEGI's program metadata. The cover imprint reads 1899; the running footer printed on the INEGI scan identifies “Resumen General..1897.” That publication-date conflict is unresolved and retained in the manifest rather than silently selecting one year. The PDFs are three segments of the same 846-page work, not three census years.

The page 6 table labels entities as “Estados” and organizes them under “Del Centro,” “Del Norte,” “Del Golfo,” and “Del Pacífico.” These are the source's own summary groupings. The evaluated table's geographic unit is state-level, plus the national sum. Nothing in this sample supports municipality or settlement totals, boundary footprints, or a modern territory crosswalk. No annual interpolation is made.

## Tables, method, and uncertainty

The evaluated definition is one table family (within the issue's maximum of 10): sex totals and infancy counts by state, with a national total row. A second table begins on the next page with age-specific counts; it is identified but not transcribed. Two illustrative rows are retained (below the issue cap of 25).

I retrieved the three official INEGI PDF segments, recorded each exact byte length and SHA-256, located the table with text extraction, and visually inspected the title page and page 6. The table typography is readable in the scan. OCR output was used for discovery only. The national sex totals reconcile exactly; the Aguascalientes row does not. No corrected value is inferred. The evaluated table's heading does not settle whether it measures usual residents or persons present, and the report supplies no undercount interval in the reviewed pages.

## Access and reuse

INEGI's general *Términos de Libre Uso de la Información* permit copying, publishing, adapting, reordering, partial extraction, and commercial use with requirements to preserve metadata, credit INEGI and the extracted product, disclose transformations, and avoid implying INEGI endorsement. The exact terms PDF URL, bytes, and hash are recorded in the manifest. No item-specific license field was visible on the historical product record. Attribute the product to INEGI and the Dirección General de Estadística, and distinguish the original publication from its current digital scan.

## Limits and next use

This is a source-only evaluation of one table and one adjacent table heading in one official census collection. It does not inventory all variables, transcribe all states, inspect municipal tables, verify historical geometry, make location assignments, create classifications, or import records. No regional certificate with exact permitted subjects and matching release pins authorizes an import.

A later bounded content review would need the exact proposed state or sub-state unit, confirmation of the applicable printed table and population universe, source-row checksums or page images, and territory matching to a complete published regional approval certificate before preparation or import.
