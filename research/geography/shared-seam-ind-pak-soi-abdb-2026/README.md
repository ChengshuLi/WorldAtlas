# Survey of India ABDB source and terms review

This packet checks whether the Survey of India (SoI) 2025 Administrative Boundary Database can supply a new official Indian administrative source for the exact 16 subjects and two retained seam-fragment pins listed in [issue #1108](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/1108). It does not compare or integrate geometry.

The SoI catalogue currently links a Pan-India district/subdistrict boundary RAR and a metadata ZIP. SoI’s FAQ says Administrative Boundary Database downloads are free to all users and that its Quick Access datasets need no login. The metadata ZIP identifies 2025 district and subdistrict products, gives a 2022–2025 creation extent, 1:50,000 scale, Lambert Conformal Conic / WGS84 (EPSG:7755), and a stated horizontal RMSE of ±12.5 m. It describes the source as SoI topographic data with boundary verification. These are metadata statements, not an independent accuracy test or a common observation date.

The redistribution terms are unresolved. The metadata marks both access and use constraints as `copyright`. SoI’s current Copyright Policy says website material requires written permission for reproduction, while the Government of India’s 2021 Geospatial Data Guidelines broadly describe sharing and distribution and say SoI political boundary data should be freely downloadable for display and printing. A separate Online Maps account checkout page has a restriction on sharing downloaded data with foreign recipients except as applicable law permits; its applicability to no-login Quick Access is unclear. The sources do not give a clear, product-specific permission to copy the complete RAR and metadata into this public repository. We therefore retain the RAR restoration reference only and do not include either downloaded archive in the PR. This is an evidence limit, not a legal conclusion.

The metadata ZIP was acquired on 2026-10-06 for local inspection only. Its complete outer archive is 38,879 encoded bytes (SHA-256 `1a14716b73f00fc8391f2708a7975c2f2c1ad4a4e91aafb6dce6546a039e9514`); the four workbook members total 46,328 decoded bytes and nested XLSX members inflate to another 146,660 bytes. The ZIP was kept private in the measured research slot during inspection and is not a candidate output. The product RAR advertises HTTP 200, byte-range support, and 202,524,438 encoded bytes. Its transfers ended early. The final stopped partial was 57,877,142 bytes, SHA-256 `db59c0872614b401211de9526d5784dc4ab87c40eab4e665bb34891ff4ed04ba`; those incomplete bytes were removed after recording the receipt. No full RAR hash, archive-member list, decoded size, or geometry has been verified.

The official metadata describes nationwide products but does not provide the subject-specific native feature ID/name mapping needed here. The exact 16 subject IDs and full-gap feature pins remain scope anchors from #1097, not evidence that SoI records match them. No direct SoI crosswalk was established. In particular, the ADM2 district product cannot replace the previous retained simplified ADM3 members; the 2025 subdistrict product remains only a candidate source pending lawful whole-source access and feature-level identity evidence.

The official sources support a precise next step: obtain written, product-specific confirmation that redistribution of the complete ABDB files and their metadata is allowed for this repository, or preserve only these official restoration references and the unresolved permission requirement. Do not infer legal-border alignment, administrative ownership, physical water/land, or disputed affiliation from this packet.

- [SoI website terms](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/terms-and-conditions) and [Online Maps checkout terms](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/addtocart.aspx) were checked; the latter’s relationship to Quick Access is not established here.

## Validation limits

The current final audit runs 3 and 4 were executed after commit `02b73312bbc091861d6ddd20674ede81598e8d33` (helper SHA-256 `dd0cfa43268137c659fb8c4ab8d5e490db887eec9161a9c3e2d4cd0a30c41b81`) against the privately retained metadata ZIP. They produced byte-identical output (SHA-256 `a6d9f88e7156bc2a5d5023675243a48ccab196f38126a42472c43f891b5fa9a7`). Earlier actual runs 1 and 2 are preserved unchanged but superseded: their mutation/truncation fields compared bytes to expected constants and did not exercise the acceptance gate. The committed `test_source_byte_gate.py` passes pristine, one-byte-mutated and one-byte-truncated fixture bytes through the same length/SHA-256 gate used before archive inspection. This demonstrates gate behavior on a synthetic fixture, not a mutation test of the metadata ZIP. Earlier parser diagnostics are preserved as superseded because an initial numeric parser read the `0.25 mm` plotting note as `25 m`; the corrected parser reads the metadata’s stated `12.5 m`. The verified original ZIP remains in the measured private slot and is excluded from Git because reuse terms are unresolved, so reviewers cannot independently rehash it. The RAR’s advertised length/range headers do not verify its bytes or contents. No geometry, feature membership, subdistrict code, full original crosswalk, water mask or political affiliation was assessed.
