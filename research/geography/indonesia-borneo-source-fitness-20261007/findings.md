## Source fitness and limits

- geoBoundaries is suitable here for a reproducible, source-relative geometric correspondence check. The retained 2020 derivative declares CC BY 4.0 with geoBoundaries and individual-source attribution; its underlying Indonesia ADM2 source metadata records CC BY 3.0 IGO and OCHA/HDX. This does not establish current Indonesian legal authority, effective dates, or local accuracy.
- ESA WorldCover 2021 v200 is suitable for a broad 10 m land-cover context screen. ESA reports 76.7% global overall accuracy for that product, which is not a local East Kalimantan estimate. The data page specifies CC BY 4.0 and required attribution.
- JRC Global Surface Water occurrence is a water-detection signal derived from Landsat observations; it can miss vegetated, small, obscured, or otherwise undetected water. JRC XML metadata and the current download page disagree on Seasonality temporal coverage. This packet labels it Seasonality 2024 and does not claim a full historical series.
- The official BIG 2022 KSP layer metadata identifies a 2022 edition revised December 2022, WKID 4326, and describes RBI, adjudication, Kemendagri, and Permendagri inputs. It also reports `ADMINISTRASI_LN` overshoot/topology errors. The layer metadata has an empty `copyrightText` and gives no open redistribution license. A bounded official-service query returned 12 polygons whose envelopes intersect the exact component roster's WGS84 envelope. The raw response (17,648,743 bytes; SHA-256 `d45aedf8f0f70031804a2666e1a6061cb5c67c1a36add6fee51ed3dd273ee94e`) is preserved only in the workspace's ignored evidence area and is not included in the repository. Direct, unbuffered comparison against the 45 components yields 47 positive-area pair intersections across 43 components; two components have no polygon intersection in this returned subset. The per-component object IDs, names, and relation classifications are in `vintages/run-forty-one/big-ksp-overlay-assessment.json`. This is a geometric correspondence screen only; BIG redistribution rights, layer completeness, legal authority, effective date, local positional accuracy, and explanations for the two unmatched components remain unresolved.
- The issue's source-only scope grants no authority to change, approve, import, or publish boundaries. All 21 numeric-closure flags and every physical or legal explanation remain unresolved.
