# Greenland five-candidate source-fit review

Status: research finding for the Greenland members of the complete GEO5 batch tracked by [issue #1630](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/1630), under campaign [#1202](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/1202). ## Decision boundary

These five cases have exact positive-area support in the pinned GSHHG 2.3.7 route analysis and unique recorded-source coverage by one Qeqertalik feature in geoBoundaries GRL ADM1. Neither fact establishes an accepted physical class, current water/ice status, or a named physical-region envelope. The compatible recorded subject is an administrative unit, not a physical-region source.

The accepted Arctic source-fit precedent in [#1481 / PR #1487](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/pull/1487) requires containment in exactly one applicable named AAFC physical-region envelope, consistent hierarchy/retired-member context, an exact additive candidate, no target loss, and no new positive-area overlap with affected neighbors. [#1520](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/1520) is explicitly limited to its retained AAFC physical-region references and GSHHG mapped-land evidence. Those AAFC predicates do not transfer to Greenland's geoBoundaries administrative source. The five cases therefore do **not** pass the reusable fit-for-reference rule and are not eligible for engineering processing reproduction under that rule. No geometry proposal is made.

The remaining geometric predicates (candidate containment in a physical-region envelope, exact additive result, target no-loss, and affected-neighbor overlap) were not recomputed for these five. The pinned route's empty `unresolved` reconstruction arrays are evidence about reconstruction accounting, not proof of fit, target conservation, or zero new neighbor overlap. These predicates must remain unclaimed until the source-envelope premise is established and the candidate-specific geometry checks are run.

This is a source-fit decision only. It does not decide legal or current boundary authority, historical cause, present-day water/ice, or publication approval. GSHHG 2.3.7 was released 2017-06-15 and has heterogeneous observation dates. The geoBoundaries reference year is 2020; it is not an effective survey date.
