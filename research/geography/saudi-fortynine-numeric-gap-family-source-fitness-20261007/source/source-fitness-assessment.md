# Saudi Arabia 49-component source-fitness assessment

Assessment date: 2026-10-07. Scope: the one complete family, all 49 issue-listed whole components, and all six issue-listed full contacts. This is a source and provenance assessment. It does not approve boundaries, classify coast or water, settle sovereignty or ownership, repair geometry, or authorize a geography change.

## Decision

The consumed geoBoundaries Saudi ADM2 product is byte-authenticated and fully retained for this assessment, but it is not established as the authoritative or legally applicable Saudi governorate boundary source. A strong primary-source pathway exists through the General Authority for Survey and Geospatial Information (GEOSA), yet this review did not obtain or inspect a complete machine-readable ADM2 polygon release with a declared edition, per-feature effective date, precision, and applicable reuse terms. Source fitness for all 49 components and all six contacts therefore remains **unresolved**. Preserve the current geometry and source labels pending those records.

The issue roster was joined against the complete accepted candidate reconstruction, complete routing streams and full numerical-scope selected/complement inventory. This establishes source-record coverage and exact identity/hash bindings for the issue subjects, not the truth of any geographic or legal claim. Each subject retains its own complete feature and routing row in run outputs. Numerical selected and complement memberships remain distinct; complement membership is not clearance.

## Geography-specific findings

- All 49 components remain in the exact complete family stated by issue #1336. The issue roster, full component records, complete route rows, current-feature hashes, current-geometry hashes, and family record are retained in each successful run.
- All six contacts are preserved as full current features from the whole baseline `data/geography/part-21.json`; the baseline world index lists that part. Contact identifiers are not treated as proof of land continuity, contact geometry, source fitness, or source authority.
- Original-source identity and current feature/geometry digest are separate evidence domains. The source's 147 whole features are included; only matching IDs and subject bindings are joined. No geometry was changed or compared through an overlay.
- Existing numerical diagnosis membership is reported per component as selected or explicit complement. Neither bucket establishes source fitness, legal accuracy, water classification, or permission to alter a line.
- Any mapped-land context recorded for three local contradictions remains a context observation, not an alternative authority. Other siblings remain unknown. No global interpolation or clearance is inferred.

## Required evidence before boundary decisions

1. Obtain the complete official GEOSA (or designated competent authority) governorate vector layer and release metadata, with publication and legal approval references, feature roster, administrative level crosswalk, effective date or interval, and a durable download/response record.
2. Obtain applicable reuse and attribution terms for that exact release and confirm derivative redistribution rights for its source inputs.
3. Obtain documented horizontal reference, datum realization, transformation parameters and accuracy, capture/compilation scale, positional accuracy and generalization tolerance for the exact delivered geometries.
4. Obtain authoritative coastal/land-water evidence and source dates adequate for the six contacts and any mapped-water or coastal contradictions; keep terrain/bathymetry context distinct from administrative authority.
5. Reconcile each of the 49 source features and six contacts to authority records while preserving exact source, geometry and current release identities. Submit any proposed correction as a separately scoped reviewed change; this assessment authorizes none.

## Reproduction and limits

See `frozen-execution.json`, the two sequential execution receipts, per-run output hashes, `reproducibility.json`, and `evidence-quality.json`. The extractor authenticates immutable file bodies, reconstructs existing candidate records using retained accepted reconstruction code, validates whole routing bodies and numeric scope, and emits full issue-subject features and receipts. It does not invoke a geometry operator. One failed first-version control attempt is retained in `history/`; it detected a harness defect before any output was written. The current frozen version passes two sequential full runs. Its geometry negative control changes a candidate point, rebinds the changed whole-feature transport hash, then calls the exact production candidate-to-route binding gate while preserving the original geometry digest; the control therefore reaches and exercises the production pointset guard. These checks establish reproducibility and binding integrity only. Human/source review is still required, and limited primary-source access is reflected as unresolved source fitness.
