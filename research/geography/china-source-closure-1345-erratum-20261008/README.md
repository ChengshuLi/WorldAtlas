# China #1345 source-closure custody correction

This additive packet fixes how the accepted #1337 source closure is reconstructed and retained. It keeps the earlier 28-file packet intact and adds a guarded runner, actual legacy-CLI controls, two new full runs, and a bounded evidence manifest. All generated files are under this issue's owned path.

## Scope and geographic limits

The accepted handoff contains two complete source-fitness families: Dalian and Qingdao, with 50 candidate components, 14 full contact features, and 34 available numeric sibling bindings. Both complete family bodies and every candidate, contact, source, current-atlas, routing, and diagnostic binding remain in the reconstructed report. Contact features and positive-length-neighbor IDs remain distinct fields. The 34 numeric rows are diagnostic bindings, not a license to infer missing values or assign boundaries.

The inherited source is the simplified geoBoundaries gbOpen CHN ADM2 GeoJSON recorded by the original handoff. It is labeled ADM2 / “County Level” and boundary year represented 2017. The pinned metadata gives integration date 2023-01-19, build date 2023-12-12, source attribution to China's National Administration of Surveying, Mapping and Geoinformation, and 2,391 units. The complete encoded payload is 2,096,437 bytes, SHA-256 `7bcd6bb51042f63c2f4f1717b25d701874cfcd6f12eb79dc36cdff29f3b62f30`; its decoded GeoJSON is 7,018,287 bytes, SHA-256 `8c7dfa8e40842f9162453d9b0b614276a48bf635559ba371c7982cb288e7b303`.

The source is a generalized ADM2 reference; its 2017 represented vintage and global source role do not establish current county boundaries. The inherited packet cites China's Administrative Regional Boundaries Management Regulation, Map Management Regulation, Ministry of Natural Resources' public-map specification, and 2020 NBS code clarification. Those materials describe the need for approved boundary instruments/maps and date-qualified statistical codes; this work did not obtain a locally applicable county agreement, approved map, marker record, local change notice, or authoritative county geometry for these components. The packet retains complete source-family context but does not independently recheck every component parent edge or compare adjacent ADM1 granularity. Administrative meaning, parent relationships at the relevant date, precise boundaries, present validity, and ownership remain unresolved.

The pinned boundary metadata says Open Data Commons Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL) v1.0. The separate pinned geoBoundaries citation-and-use text says geoBoundaries code and derivative works are CC BY 4.0 and requires attribution. Both statements are retained; their application to these particular derived geometries has not been legally determined. The source is GeoJSON, which RFC 7946 defines using WGS 84 / CRS84 longitude-latitude coordinates in decimal degrees. The pinned metadata provides no positional-accuracy statement, boundary tolerance, survey scale, or per-feature observation date. Decimal digits do not establish precision.

Physical evidence remains inherited and unmodified: the prior packet identifies GSHHG 2.3.7 (released 2017-06-15, with heterogeneous observation dates) as a global shoreline/lake screening source, and JRC Global Surface Water v1.4 as Landsat-derived 30 m water history over 1984–2021. Neither settles local legal boundaries, ownership, narrow channels, current island registration, or present land-water conditions. No raster, overlay, or geometry science was run for this correction. Parent issue #1202 remains unfinished. All 50 component-level source-fitness unknowns remain in the original records.

This work establishes safer custody and repeatability only. It does not certify geographic correctness or source authority and does not approve a repair, import, production change, or publication.

## Controls and engineering handoff

`legacy_controls.py` executes the original assembler entry point in an isolated fixture. It records that a changed candidate fails with the accepted lock but succeeds after the legacy `--write-lock` recomputes from that changed handoff; that a changed whole-family neighbor body survives a copied accounting checksum when the lock is regenerated; and that the original CLI overwrites a sentinel and follows dangling-leaf and parent symlinks. It also records rejection of source-byte, current-contact, and subject-roster mutations under the unchanged original lock. The private fixture and all redirected targets are inside this owned directory and are removed after the receipt is written.

The original assembler should be amended by its engineering owner or retired as a trusted reproduction entry point. A durable fix should bind candidate geometries, full family bodies, contacts, source/current bytes, and subjects to the independently accepted originals before extraction; a recomputable lock over mutable inputs cannot establish custody. It should admit all inputs and output destinations before work, reject symlink/escape/collision cases, write exclusively, read back the complete output set, and install a complete receipt last. The new runner applies those controls without modifying the predecessor packet or shared producer. Independent review of that producer change remains a separate engineering task.
