### MyGOS / JUPEM District or Jajahan Coverage

The official [MyGOS service](https://mygos.mygeoportal.gov.my/gisserver/rest/services/MyGeomap/Msia_Coverage/MapServer) exposes the District/Jajahan layer; the exact [layer metadata endpoint](https://mygos.mygeoportal.gov.my/gisserver/rest/services/MyGeomap/Msia_Coverage/MapServer/2?f=pjson) and service metadata were inspected on 2026-10-07. The Terengganu query and its raw response SHA are reproducible from `mygos-diagnostic.py` and the two `mygos-runs/` outputs. Response bytes were used in memory and are **not retained or redistributed**, because the `PPM` data-sharing field is null and no open reuse license was established. The two query runs returned the same report and source-response hash `e86bd17f5a33b3b941a3867bf4f94697759e9d3edfea52f29f50fa0405131964` (1,349,692 bytes).

All 8 returned district records identify `Sumber=JUPEM`, `Tahun_Kemaskini=OGOS 2019`, and `Tahun_Terima=2019`; `PPM` is null for all. The layer reports polygon geometry, extent 98.9285–104.5556 E / 1.2531–6.7330 N, WKID 4742, and service units `esriDecimalDegrees`. Its layer spatial-reference record says 4742 (GDM2000 geographic); the service document-info `Subject` instead says the coordinate system used is projected RSO/GDM2000 (GDMRSO). A GeoJSON query requested output SR 4326, but the server's datum operation was not explicitly chosen or pinned. The unresolved metadata conflict and unspecified server transformation prevent treating this as a registered accuracy-grade overlay.

One raw source polygon, **Besut (code 01)**, is invalid: Shapely reports `Ring Self-intersection[102.440777538722 5.36159337551909]`. No repair or `MakeValid` was applied. The recorded Besut intersections for components `a5dbeb12…21733e9` and `e9dd7858…602c70d5` are explicitly diagnostic only; their area sign and full eight-district coverage are not reported as established. For the seven valid district polygons, the read-only query-coordinate comparison finds one component covered by their union and seven not covered. This is an incomplete subset, not a conclusion about the full district coverage. These findings also mean the earlier issue progress statement that treated all eight service polygons as valid and reported two covered / six outside is superseded; see the correction comment linked from the issue.

The [MyGDI data-sharing guidance](https://www.mygeoportal.gov.my/sites/default/files/2020-12/Guidelines%20For%20Sharing%20Of%20Geospatial%20Data%20Through%20MyGDI%202018.pdf) describes provider/custodian sharing, including G2G controls. ### 2025 JUPEM harmonization described by DOSM

The [DOSM 2025 administrative-district technical notes](https://www.dosm.gov.my/uploads/release-content/file_20261005142912.pdf) say JUPEM updated/harmonized state and administrative-district boundaries in 2025 using finalized land/maritime surveys, PHN low-water-line chart information, NDCDB, and shoreline data derived from 2021–2026 IFSAR/World Imagery. The notes explain that area changes can arise from geometry adjustments, topology alignment, line positioning, and coastline references. This is an important newer lineage/method lead. The release provides no exact downloadable Terengganu vector, native CRS/precision/registration report, candidate-level feature lineage, or reuse grant; the appended administrative map remains labeled 2020. It cannot be used to validate these candidate overlays.

## Physical/water and source fitness

MyGDI/JUPEM source listings and the sharing guidance distinguish shoreline, coast, low-water line, water bodies, and their custodians. They identify candidate datasets and providers but do not provide an authenticated, dated candidate-scale Terengganu land/water geometry with usable bytes, resolution, registration/accuracy, and terms. No such physical/water claim is supported here.

The 2019 MyGOS features, 2020 simplified geoBoundaries product, 2023 Atlas build metadata, 2025 JUPEM harmonization description, and 2026 retrieval date are separate dates and products. They must not be collapsed into one effective boundary vintage. The complete source requirements that remain are: provider-confirmed geometry for the 2025 (or otherwise exact chosen) boundary; valid raw geometries and documented native CRS/datum transformation; effective and observation dates; precision/scale and registration/accuracy; whole-file source bytes and stable hashes; applicable attribution/reuse terms; the upstream explanation for the 159/160 feature count; exact lineage from the pinned simplified product to current part-16 geometries; and dated physical/water/coast references fit for each complete component.

All classes, numeric-closure siblings, physical land/water interpretations, source lineage/cause, legal authority, effective date, reuse applicability, and rightful ownership remain unknown or unapproved. This is source research only.
