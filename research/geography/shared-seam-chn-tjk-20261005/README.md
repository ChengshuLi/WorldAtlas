# China–Tajikistan shared seam source investigation

This packet answers only the bounded source question in [issue #1096](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/issues/1096): did the retained physical-gap fragment already fall between the three native 2017 ADM2 source members, or did Atlas reconciliation create it? It makes no administrative or territorial assignment and does not review the regional interiors in #380 or #319.

## Finding

The broad uncovered seam predates Atlas reconciliation in these administrative products. The exact retained candidate fragment is about 931.768 km². The three complete original source members cover only about 3.473 km² of it in union, leaving about 928.295 km² (99.627%) uncovered in the source polygons. The current reconciled Atlas polygons cover zero polygon area of the fragment, leaving all of it uncovered. In this limited sense reconciliation increases the uncovered part of this already-present gap by the source polygons' small overlap with the fragment; it does not explain the broad gap.

This does not establish that the entire polygon is dry land. The fragment was a generalized audit candidate; its no-hit against a major-lakes layer is only a coarse diagnostic. No independent terrain, river, seasonal-water, hydrology or field evidence was retained. The conclusion is therefore about administrative source polygons and their representation only.

## What changed in reconciliation

The source geometries are valid without repair and each native member is uniquely matched by full-source `shapeID` and name. The Atlas geometries are the same at the current baseline and the retained 2023-12-13 historic snapshot. Their per-subject symmetric-difference areas from the native members are about 79.165 km² (Tashenkuergantajike), 278.716 km² (Anketaoxian), and 169.859 km² (Murghob District).

The native CHN pair shares about 188.570 km of boundary and has no polygon overlap. The native Tashenkuergantajike/Murghob pair overlaps by about 36.785 km²; the native Anketaoxian/Murghob pair overlaps by about 351.752 km². Those pairwise overlaps are present in the full 2017 source geometries, outside the question of assigning territory. Atlas reconciliation turns the latter pair into about 134.419 km of shared line and removes its areal overlap. For Tashenkuergantajike/Murghob, Atlas still retains a tiny positive areal intersection of about 0.00000182 m² alongside line and point components; it is recorded, not rounded away. The source-to-Atlas symmetric difference for Murghob is a GeometryCollection with polygon and line remnants; those are preserved in the result.

The three Atlas `metadata.original_geometry_sha256` values do not match canonical JSON hashes of the retrieved native members. Identity and names match, but neither metadata nor the source packet specifies the old digest recipe. This mismatch remains unresolved; it is not treated as proof that the source files are wrong or that Atlas used another source version.

## Source and authority limits

The complete geoBoundaries `9469f09592ced973a3448cf66b6100b741b64c0d` CHN and TJK ADM2 GeoJSON distributions, both metadata files, and the shared citation/use file are retained as lossless deterministic gzip files. Each manifest descriptor records both the stored gzip bytes and the exact decoded original byte count and SHA-256; the comparison decompresses and verifies before parsing. The metadata describes 2017 boundaries, with a 2023-01-19 source-data update and a 2023-12-12 build. CHN lists the National Administration of Surveying, Mapping and Geoinformation of China and a PDDL-1.0 boundary license; TJK lists OpenStreetMap/Wambacher and ODbL 1.0. The provider's shared citation notice describes the geoBoundaries product as CC BY 4.0 and requests citation of the individual boundary source in its metadata. These are administrative products; the source metadata does not say they are a joint international-boundary authority.

The Tajikistan Ministry of Foreign Affairs' 2013 page says the China boundary's delimitation and demarcation issues were resolved and lists the 1999 and 2002 state-boundary agreements, a 2010 demarcation protocol, the 2012 boundary-regime agreement, and the 2011 crossing-points agreement. China's official treaty database records the 1999 state-boundary agreement as effective 2000-07-04 and lists the 2002 supplementary agreement. These official records support the existence and dates of a bilateral boundary framework. The actual demarcation protocol and authoritative large-scale maps/coordinates were not retrieved or matched to this local seam, so this packet cannot place the legal line against the fragment or infer ownership. See [Tajik MFA boundary summary](https://mid.tj/en/main/view/150/state-border-of-the-republic-of-tajikistan-with-the-peoples-republic-of-china), [China MFA treaty record for the 1999 agreement](https://treaty.mfa.gov.cn/Treaty/web/detail1.jsp?objid=1531876798495), and [China MFA land-boundary instruments index](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/ziliao_674904/tytj_674911/tyfg_674913/200904/t20090409_9867602.shtml). The official sources were consulted 2026-10-06; they are cited as records, not used as downloadable geometry.

## Bounded proposal

Keep this seam unresolved for map correction. A later decision needs the authoritative bilateral demarcation materials and suitably detailed land/water evidence, followed by an independent geospatial review. Do not use either ADM2 source, the generalized candidate, or a nearest-owner rule to assign the fragment. No snapping, buffering, `MakeValid`, proximity fill, or area cutoff was used here.

## Reproduction and retained evidence

`compare-seam.py` reads the three full native source files, pins the complete current and historical Atlas inputs, restores all 45 original input-envelope members at their recorded source commits, verifies the complete compressed candidate file and original full-feature hash, and performs direct overlay operations on unmodified EPSG:4326 geometries. It uses the repository's `worldatlas-evidence-geometry-v1` WGS84 area and geodesic-distance helpers. `vintages/final-one-20261006/` and `vintages/final-two-20261006/` are separate process runs after the source code was committed. `vintages/repro-final-20261006/reproducibility.json` compares every corresponding JSON artifact by whole-file SHA-256. Earlier `run-one-20261006`, `run-two-20261006` and `repro-20261006` directories are pre-commit diagnostic trials and are not cited as final reproduction.

Positive and negative controls are hash-bound in each run. The positive verifies a known CHN source-member line contact; the negative verifies that the same intersection operation returns empty for disjoint analytic rectangles and makes no geographic claim. The result retains full output geometry types, tiny overlaps and operations/errors. Reproduction establishes repeatable computation on these bytes, not legal authority, physical land status, source truth, or regional approval.
