# Nordic four-component source investigation

This packet covers exactly the four frozen detector components and four Norway/Sweden ADM2 contact subjects in issue #1229. It uses the merged #1164 inventory at geometry baseline `79ffb2ed04702e16f009e4675a8d74ef9bd09d4f`, while retaining its distinct original comparison vintage `cea80a8aa1f8a55ccb448a8f2ff71e10c49a26f1`. No core geography was changed.

## Findings

- The complete consumed simplified geoBoundaries product bytes are preserved in this directory and match the immutable registry entries at the geometry baseline: Norway, 431 features, 2013, recorded source Geonorge / Mapping Authority of Norway, CC BY 4.0; Sweden, 290 features, 2017, recorded source geoBoundaries / Wikimedia Commons, CC0 1.0. The products are pinned to geoBoundaries commit `9469f09592ced973a3448cf66b6100b741b64c0d`. These are the consumed simplified products; the distinct advertised full-product URLs have not been fetched or authenticated. Registry provenance records the Geonorge catalog URL for Norway and a Wikimedia Commons file URL for Sweden, plus the separate geoBoundaries download URLs and licenses. The packet records the observed decimal-place range in each captured product, but the registry does not supply a positional-accuracy specification and decimal places are not an accuracy claim.
- Independently recomputed against all four exact contact subjects, every Atlas geometry differs from its corresponding consumed-source geometry (the comparison stores both complete directional difference geometries). The symmetric-difference planar areas are 0.004821884249428717, 0.0023781923163845526, 0.0035643234329735965, and 0.040670914282288556 square degrees, respectively. These are geometrical source-versus-Atlas mismatches, not authority judgments.
- All four candidate components have positive-area overlap with the complete NOR/SWE consumed-product union, and most candidate area remains outside that union. Comparing against all 721 features gives the same per-candidate results as comparing against the four contact subjects: no other ADM2 unit in either complete product adds coverage. Complete intersection and difference geometries are retained for each country union, the combined union, and the four-contact union, plus every intersecting individual source unit with its exact intersection geometry and dimension. These four candidates have only positive-area overlaps with source units; the complete scan records no positive-length or point-only source-unit contacts.
- The pinned detector component rows report `water_status=unverified`, `touches_reference_shore=false`, `positive_area_input_overlap=false`, and no administrative assignment for all four candidates. All five bound fragments are measured; none is marked unmeasured. The complete relevant fragment and contact rows are preserved and hash-bound.
- A second exact overlay against the inventory's retained Natural Earth land layer covers all four candidates; its Natural Earth lakes layer intersects none. This is coarse modern physical-reference evidence only. It does not establish land versus water/ice at the relevant historical dates, boundary authority, sovereignty, or a correct territorial assignment.

## Classification and recommendation

All four candidates are classified **supported source mismatch** in the narrow geometric sense: the candidate has area outside the complete consumed simplified Norway/Sweden products, and the four corresponding Atlas subject geometries are not topologically equal to those products. The specific cause linking those differences to each detector polygon remains unresolved. The current `scripts/prepare.mjs` command (`mapshaper -simplify 25% keep-shapes`, output `precision=0.0001`) is only a reproduction hypothesis. The original whole-world input closure, exact executed preparation code/runtime, and all intervening topology/repair/preparation operations have not been bound to this comparison. Therefore this packet does **not** claim a supported processing-loss cause, a confirmed physical gap, or a repairable political boundary defect.

Do not fill or assign these candidates. A defensible next engineering step, only after independent authoritative physical/boundary evidence and exact pipeline replay confirm missing land, is a coordinated closure over every neighboring NOR/SWE feature participating in those seams. Locate the first transformation that changes the source set, then propose one mutually consistent before/after update; require full source-to-output geometry, native-grid, release, identity/crosswalk/content revalidation and independent exact-head review before any core edit. No defect is established here, so no geometry repair is proposed in this source-only packet.

The broader regional packets #336 and #337 remain distinct and open. Their claims are not imported here. No browser, provider, publisher, or production calls were made.

## Reproduction and files

Run `python3 reproduce_source_comparison.py` and `python3 reproduce_physical_reference_overlay.py` from a checkout containing the pinned Git objects and the two preserved source files beside the scripts. The scripts use only exact pinned source bytes, Shapely overlays, and no normalization, snap, buffer, simplify, repair, minimum-area cutoff, or nearest fill.

- `source-and-candidate-comparison.json`: source metadata and byte pins; all four exact Atlas/source subject differences; component, fragment, and contact custody bindings; per-candidate full geometry intersection/difference results.
- `physical-reference-overlays.json`: full Natural Earth land/lakes intersections and differences with exact source bindings.
- `scoped-original-components.geojson`, `scoped-original-fragments.geojson`, `scoped-original-atlas-subjects.geojson`, `scoped-consumed-source-subjects.geojson`: exact extracted feature rows; hashes are recorded in the comparison/evidence manifest.
- `gb-NOR-ADM2-original-simplified.geojson`, `gb-SWE-ADM2-original-simplified.geojson`: complete consumed source products, preserved as retrieved upstream bytes.
- `evidence-quality.json`: manifest of baseline and output byte hashes and the explicit unresolved authority/reproduction limits.
