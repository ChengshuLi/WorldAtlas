# Poland–Belarus–Ukraine cross-border candidate: source fitness

This packet assesses one complete candidate polygon in the single issue-scoped gap family, its one component, and all nine current Atlas administrative contacts. The full candidate and all contact feature bodies are retained and bound to the immutable baseline. No global producer was rerun.

## Findings

Three complete geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM2 simplified products were restored byte-for-byte from the recorded consumed-source products: Belarus (118 features; recorded year 2005 and CC BY 3.0), Poland (380; recorded year 2017 and ODbL 1.0), and Ukraine (495; recorded year 2006 and Public Domain). These dates, source descriptions, and license labels are source metadata claims; they do not establish current effectiveness, positional accuracy, or border authority. Every decoded source file is bound to its compressed ordinary Git source part, including the part's mode, Git blob OID, compressed hash, decoded size and hash.

The candidate has a declared and shared-helper area of 860,328,102.1759481 m² (about 860.3 km²). Overlaying each complete source product with the full candidate finds positive-area contact with 1 Belarus, 3 Poland, and 5 Ukraine source features. After dissolving each country's complete source geometry, candidate coverage is 0.0414%, 1.6152%, and 0.0159% respectively; the union covers 1.6722% of the candidate. These are source-relative diagnostics, not an administrative partition or a physical explanation.

All nine current Atlas contacts join uniquely by source `shapeID` to a feature in the complete matching simplified product. None of the nine current Atlas geometries is topologically equal to the corresponding simplified source feature. Current Atlas feature metadata links to the unsimplified `.geojson` URL, while the pinned baseline acquisition script rewrites that endpoint to `simplifiedGeometryGeoJSON` before download. The URL and geometry lineage differ; this packet does not identify when or why the current geometries diverged. The displayed symmetric-difference areas are diagnostics only.

The retained Natural Earth 1:10m lakes product has 1,355 features and no valid polygon intersects the candidate. Two unrelated invalid features were left untouched; both bounding boxes are disjoint from the candidate. This major-lake reference does not cover rivers, small water, seasonal water, or year-specific hydrology. No hit cannot establish dry land.

The delivered global physical and numeric results are reused as authenticated source-relative context. Their source dates, positional accuracy, narrow channels, registration and physical authority remain unresolved. The prior exact numeric evidence establishes only one local constructed-difference contradiction; it does not explain the complete candidate or partition.

## Limits and next evidence

The retained material does not establish a lawful international boundary, historical ownership, national administrative assignment, positional accuracy, present land/water status, or the cause of this full candidate. It does not approve geometry changes or publication. No country clipping, unilateral affiliation, repair, snapping, buffering, simplification, or `MakeValid` is supported.

A coordinated next investigation should preserve the Atlas and source vintages, trace the current-contact versus consumed-simplified lineage, then obtain candidate-scale authoritative records from Poland, Belarus, and Ukraine and a suitably dated, detailed hydrographic reference. Registration and positional accuracy metadata are needed to distinguish source mismatch from a river/channel or other physical feature. Until then, cause, partition, land/water, and ownership remain unknown.
