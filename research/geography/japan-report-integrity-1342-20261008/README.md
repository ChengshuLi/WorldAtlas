# Japan N03 report-integrity repair

Issue #1496, one additive reporting repair to the retained evidence from PR #1342. This packet owns only this directory. All 75 files in the prior Japan source-fitness packet remain unchanged; the original table and its source overlays are inputs, not replacement targets.

## Finding and method

The prior complete overlay report contains 37 positive finite intersections between the 28 detector components and the retained MLIT N03 2017 polygons. Their reported field is `intersection_area_jgd2011_degrees2`. The table builder reads `intersection_area_degrees2` while constructing its intermediate row and therefore emits null in all 37 `intersection_area_jgd2011_degrees2` fields. `methods/reconcile_source_fitness_table.py` authenticates the exact prior packet roster (75 whole files, 22,072,824 bytes and canonical descriptor SHA-256 `033b6229751721a876f318482081b75121504535f5e61743c69de65ec73c02a2`), the issue-pinned scope, overlay, table and physical-row receipt, all indexed world parts and the hierarchy snapshot before making a corrected in-memory copy. It executes `scripts/evidence/immutable.py` from the exact hash-pinned Git blob at that baseline commit; it never imports the mutable checkout copy.

The method requires the complete 49-target roster (28 components and 21 current Atlas contacts), complete nine-family membership partition, 5,731 unique source/target pairs, the complete retained source summaries and exact candidate/contact intersection counts. Each transferred value is joined on component identity, MLIT source code, source record ordinal/number and intersection geometry hash; the remaining source attributes are compared. It preserves every other old table field. A fresh output vintage is completely admitted before writing; the shared immutable helper rejects occupied outputs and symlink paths, creates outputs exclusively and publishes its completion receipt last. A failure before publication creates no success output.

## Geography and source interpretation

MLIT's retained N03 v2.3 material describes nationwide administrative areas bounded by administrative lines and coastline, with 2017-01-01 reference vintage and JGD2011 geographic coordinates. `N03_001` identifies prefecture, `N03_003` county/designated-city grouping where present, `N03_004` the municipality/city-ward name, and `N03_007` the administrative code. These adjacent administrative levels help interpret overlap but do not establish legal parentage. The 37 transferred measurements remain angular square degrees; they are not square metres. The archived original nationwide ZIP is 242,211,059 bytes with SHA-256 `a649b4099c1b0df80a6fb9ed087b594ed75ae9a92e9511cecdb6463d1480c6fb`; it exceeds the per-file limit and is not re-downloaded or reprocessed in this repair. The 37 values are authenticated only as values in the complete retained overlay report.

The 21 contacts are current Atlas `gb:JPN:ADM2` polygons with 2017 reference labels and recorded municipality/city-ward source roles. They sit under hierarchy records at `province` level, corresponding to prefecture-type parents. These are inherited hierarchy assertions, not fresh semantic validation. geoBoundaries metadata and the old packet note a product label year of 2017 but source update/build dates in 2023; the label is not verified as an effective-date claim. Administrative intersections do not resolve physical geography, sovereignty, cause or legal ownership.

The retained N03 product page marks the 2017 release commercial-use permitted; the captured legacy terms describe copying and redistribution with source/processing attribution and retain limitations. This report relies on the prior packet's captured product page, terms, retrieval headers, exact archive hash, `.prj`, metadata and read-only specification. It does not make a broader rights determination for underlying third-party material or present-day terms. See the original packet's `sources/mlit/` files and manifest for restoration details and exact hashes. No native MLIT bytes are duplicated here.

## Results, limits and handoff

The first eight complete vintages are retained as superseded diagnostics in `vintages/superseded-history.json.gz` (32 exact output and completion-receipt files). Runs one through six rendered the contact year as `None`; runs seven and eight corrected that content but imported the shared helper from the checkout before verifying its pin. `receipts/superseded-vintages.json` maps every archived path, byte length and SHA-256. To restore any diagnostic file, decompress the archive, select its `entries` row by `path`, base64-decode `content_base64`, and verify `bytes` and `sha256` before writing under the original owned path. The final `run-nine` and `run-ten` executions load and execute the exact helper Git blob from the pinned baseline, pass the full strict CLI checks, and have byte-identical table, contact context and validation products; each has a final-written completion receipt. The `late-failure-pinned-helper` control runs the complete current CLI through compute, injects a failure, and records no output directory or success text (`receipts/late-failure-pinned-helper.json`). `tests/test_reconcile.py` exercises exact positive transfer and rejected roster, pair, unit/value, count, source identity, family and output-path mutations. These checks validate custody and transfer behavior; they do not independently establish the MLIT native geometry or original producer execution.

The complete overlay calculations remain the original PR #1342 results. No full MLIT archive or global-world scientific rerun was performed; neither oversized input limits nor evidence closure caps were bypassed. Native source accuracy, physical/legal/date applicability, GSHHG full source custody and historic effectiveness remain unresolved. No source-supported physical classification, correction, ownership, cause or geography approval is made. Existing dispositions and all prior evidence remain preserved. Engineering handoff: keep table consumers on the angular-unit field, and review the upstream producer fix independently before any future raw-source rerun or geographic decision.
