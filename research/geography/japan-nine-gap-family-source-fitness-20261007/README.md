# Japan nine-family source fitness

Issue #1316, one bounded geography research packet. Scope is nine complete Japan ADM2 gap families, 28 whole candidate polygons, 28 original detector fragments, and all 21 full current Atlas contact polygons. The source identity/scope receipts bind every whole feature to the routed inventory and current contact parts. No numeric-first members are present. The original disposition counts remain 11 land-plus-compatible-original-source processing candidates, 10 partial/unbound land cases, 3 mixed-support cases, and 4 outside-source-domain cases. These are routing triage labels, not source approval.

This packet compares the exact whole Atlas candidates and contacts to three distinct administrative source products: the full geoBoundaries Japan ADM2 product; the exact simplified product consumed by the Atlas's pinned administrative pipeline; and the complete national MLIT N03 product dated 2017-01-01. It reuses the 28 already accepted GSHHG source-relative result rows from #1261 and does not rerun the worldwide detector. Per-target source pairs, contact identity bindings, and metrics are in `results/source-overlays.json`; the complete 28-row disposition/reconciliation table and nine-family closure are in `results/source-fitness-table.json`.

## Source findings

The Atlas's actual input was geoBoundaries' simplified product, whose complete retained bytes match the Atlas source registry and upstream simplified LFS pointer. The unsimplified file is a separate diagnostic product. The exact full and simplified bodies share the same 1,742 shapeIDs; geometry differs for 1,741 same-ID features. RFC 7946 defines GeoJSON coordinates as WGS84 geographic decimal degrees in longitude-latitude order; this format convention does not establish the source's positional accuracy or authority. Each product's source interactions are reported separately, with same-ID contact comparisons. The geoBoundaries metadata advertises 1,745 units, while each complete product body contains 1,742 features; that discrepancy is preserved. Metadata labels the product year 2017, but records a source update on 2023-01-19 and build on 2023-12-12. It does not establish that the geometry was effective or observed in 2017.

MLIT N03 v2.3 describes the product as nationwide administrative areas bounded by administrative lines and coastline, with a 2017-01-01 reference date and JGD2011 geographic coordinates. The official product page marks the 2017 release commercial-use permitted; the applicable legacy terms allow copying and redistribution with source/processing attribution and retain stated limitations. The official nationwide archive is 242,211,059 bytes, above the 32 MiB per-file bound, so it is honestly declared restoration-only. Its captured URL, response headers, whole-body SHA256, and six member identities are retained. `python methods/restore_mlit_source.py --download` restores the original complete archive and fails closed unless its exact size and SHA match. No clipped extract is represented as the whole source.

The native N03 shapefile contains 116,024 polygon records, 1,902 distinct nonblank `N03_007` values, and 140 blank-code records. Polygon rows and code values are not treated as a municipal-unit count. The overlay reader scans the complete native SHP/DBF record sequence, constructs only records whose original SHP envelopes overlap one or more exact target envelopes, and then runs exact Shapely predicates/intersections on each retained whole polygon. The envelope is only a query accelerator; it never decides an overlay. Native rings and coordinates are not repaired, dissolved, simplified, or edited. Candidate/contact coordinates are transformed from EPSG:4326 to EPSG:6668 (JGD2011) with `always_xy=True`; the operation and declared accuracy are recorded in the result. Reported areas remain square degrees in the source geographic CRS and are not square metres.

The earlier GSHHG comparison records source-relative mapped-land, inland-water, exterior, and unresolved support for each whole candidate. GSHHG is an old heterogeneous-vintage reference with known shoreline registration/resolution and unrecorded river-width limits. Its source-license text also retains an LGPL version wording conflict. These rows do not establish present-day physical truth, observation-date fitness, or authority.

Official GSI Fundamental Geospatial Information service pages returned HTTP 403 from this request environment; this records only the access result for this retrieval. The captured GSI 1:200,000 page is too coarse to establish finite geometry applicability to these small candidates. No GSI high-resolution geometry or hydrography was used. This packet does not infer dry land or water from absence in any source.

## Findings and actions

The two frozen source-overlay executions are byte-identical (4,355,038 bytes; SHA256 `2c326ee027c6b4f5ea196e39daca657b493a51f9cf7834bee46a4ca7a9973804`). Against the 28 candidates and 21 contacts, each complete geoBoundaries product has 1,742 source features and 153 bbox-candidate pairs; exact intersections occur in 113 full-product pairs and 109 Atlas-simplified pairs. Full and simplified products produce three candidate predicate/coverage differences: for two candidates, a full-product feature intersects but its same-ID simplified feature does not; for a third, the full feature does not cover the candidate while the simplified feature does. The complete table identifies these candidates and source IDs. This establishes that simplification is material for some scoped overlays; it does not establish that either geometry is physically or legally correct.

The N03 parser checked all 116,024 SHP and DBF records, selected 5,174 records by exact target-envelope query, and evaluated 5,425 target-record pairs. Of those, 37 pairs intersect candidate components and 917 pairs intersect the 21 current contacts; no invalid pair was encountered. These are 2017 administrative/coastline source relationships in JGD2011 geographic coordinates, not physical land/water findings. All 28 prior GSHHG rows remain source-relative and unchanged. The detailed outputs preserve every target/source pair, contact identity bindings, intersection hashes, exact N03 source record identifiers, and family closure.

No source-supported geometry repair, physical-land/water classification, ownership determination, cause, or release approval is established. Keep all four original disposition groups unchanged. Continue with an exact source-processing lineage review for the 11 compatible-source candidates; resolve missing/unbound source identity for the 10 partial cases; obtain candidate-scale hydro/coast evidence before interpreting the 3 mixed cases; and retain the 4 exterior cases as unclassified rather than filling them. Any later processing reproduction needs its own complete affected-neighbor, release, and content revalidation.

## Reproduction and controls

`inputs/runtime-freeze.json` binds the complete code, source/input bytes, baseline commit, bundled Python/Shapely/GEOS/PROJ runtime file hashes, and the restoration-only N03 archive SHA **before** the two successful full executions. The initial run-one attempt stopped before creating an overlay due to a metrics accumulator error; its failure receipt and log are retained. The corrected producer and wrapper were frozen in a new commit before the successful run-one attempt 2 and run two. The successful complete outputs are byte-identical, and `receipts/two-run-equality.json` records both whole-file hashes. `receipts/producer-positive-control.json` and `producer-negative-control.json` exercise exact source coverage, invalid geometry uncertainty, wrong source bytes/vintage, incomplete/duplicate/foreign targets, source identity and malformed native ring behavior. Raster affine/NoData controls are not applicable because no raster is read.

The issue contract's encoded and decoded ordinary-file bounds, total closure cap, and descriptor cap are checked in `evidence-quality.json`. The complete baseline source/report bodies, selected prior GSHHG rows, exact source products, captured citations/terms, scoped output records, and runs are separately pinned. Physical authority and source-fitness conclusions remain unapproved/unknown.
