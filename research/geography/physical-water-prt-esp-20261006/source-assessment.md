# Physical-water source appraisal: Portugal–Spain retained component

Retrieved 2026-10-06. This is source access and reference coincidence evidence for the complete retained component `gap:ad2052defbeba8e89287bcc4344e641225c6579f9afd584497b381fe8fd8eedc`; it is not an executed land/water classification, territorial assignment, boundary certification, or repair.

## Scope and source roles

The full component is 297,528,340.703454 m², with CRS84 bounds `[-7.5432, 39.6412, -6.8665, 40.0666]`. This envelope is used only as a conservative query filter; the exact component geometry remains the comparison AOI. The 27,087.655736 m² residual is included inside that component and must not be treated as the sole scope. Its presence as a residual in an administrative polygon comparison does not show that it is water or land.

The prior administrative source comparison and metadata are preserved separately in the cited source packet and issue #972. This appraisal only tests official hydrography service access and line coincidence.

## Portugal: APA/SNIAmb

The APA/SNIAmb `HY.Network` metadata describes the `Rede hidrográfica GeoCodificada`, served by WFS 2.0, WMS and Shapefile, native EPSG:3763, nominal scale 1:25,000. The metadata records publication 2006-07-01 and edition 2015-06-18 and describes derivation from Carta Militar de Portugal M888 and digital elevation processing. Those are dataset lineage dates, not observations of water presence. Metadata page: https://sniambgeoportal.apambiente.pt/geoportal/catalog/search/resource/details.page?uuid=%7B613E780E-052A-4183-99E5-6100D5663081%7D. WFS capabilities: https://inspire.apambiente.pt/getogc/services/INSPIRE/HY_Network_WatercourseLink/MapServer/WFSServer?request=GetCapabilities&service=WFS.

The target collection advertises native EPSG:3763 and GML MultiCurve. WFS paging is advertised. The query-only full hit count was 101,231. Several bounded BBOX diagnostics returned contradictory counts (zero for plausible AOI BBOX forms but all 101,231 for a broad/control request); therefore they do not establish a complete local extraction. The first-feature control response confirms GML coordinate values but does not resolve this BBOX behavior. The exact requests/responses are inventoried in `sources/official-api-responses/` and `response-receipts.json`. No complete target-area HY.Network extract is claimed.

APA WFD river water bodies were queried successfully. The official open-data listing identifies the represented dataset as surface-water bodies, rivers for the second WFD planning cycle (2015–2021) and lists CC BY 4.0: https://dados.gov.pt/en/datasets/massas-de-agua-superficiais-rios-de-portugal-continental-conjunto-de-dados-geografico-sniamb. The service schema advertises `AM.WaterBodyForWFD` as GML MultiCurve, default EPSG:4326. GetCapabilities/schema/raw API responses are retained. An explicit conservative full-component EPSG:4326 BBOX request uses WFS 2.0 axis order latitude,longitude: `39.6412,-7.5432,40.0666,-6.8665`. Request URLs are recorded in the receipts. Page 0 returned 59 members; page 1 at `startIndex=59` returned 0. A fresh, exactly receipted `resultType=hits` query at 15:04:03Z reported `numberMatched=59` and `numberReturned=0`. The separate page timestamps (14:49:54Z, 14:49:56Z, 15:04:03Z) mean this is a cross-snapshot count/exhaustion check, not a transactional result snapshot. Both feature pages report `numberMatched="unknown"`; the count plus exhausted subsequent page support conditional bounded completeness, not a guarantee that the source stayed unchanged between requests. An older retained hits response from the preflight had no preserved exact URL and is explicitly treated as untraceable, not as the exact-query receipt. All 59 unique native `gml:id` values are retained in the comparison JSON.

## Spain: MITECO

MITECO's official PHC 2022–2027 page says its surface-water geometries delimit water bodies defined for Water Framework Directive planning, and the hydrographic network is generated from surface-water bodies. It lists national Shapefile products at 1:25,000, with the hydrographic network and river-line files updated 2023-11-02, and permits free use with attribution to MITECO. The page reports national extents and file sizes (103 MB hydro network, 100 MB surface-water lines, 78.5 MB surface-water polygons), so national bulk downloads were avoided. Official page: https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/cartografia-y-sig/ide/descargas/agua/masas-de-agua-phc-2022-2027.html.

MITECO's official WMS catalogue distinguishes visualization services and lists water-related layers/endpoints, including a 100×100 m DEM-derived basic network and hydrography layers: https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/cartografia-y-sig/ide/directorio_datos_servicios/agua/wms-inspire-agua.html. The official OGC Features collections listing was retrieved in full and has no relevant water/hydrography collection. A GetCapabilities request to the MITECO PHC RedHidro WMS endpoint returned HTTP 200 with a 561-byte XML `ServiceExceptionReport` (`InvalidFormat`, server-side `NullReferenceException`); the official GeoServer WMS endpoint tested from the catalogue could not establish TLS. These are retained failures, not successful vector data. WMS visualization/GetFeatureInfo is not a complete vector extraction. Thus no MITECO vector response was available for exact comparison; this is an access limitation, not evidence of no Spanish hydrography.

## Reproduction and interpretation

`reproduction/compare_apa_wfd_component.py` reads the pinned full-component shard and the retained complete APA WFD response, parses EPSG:4326 GML axis order explicitly, projects to EPSG:25829 with `always_xy`, and computes exact Shapely intersections without buffering, snapping, repair or rasterization. It was run twice; `run-1.json` and `run-2.json` have identical SHA-256. The returned set has 59 unique native members; 10 have nonempty line coincidence with the full component, with a sum of per-member clipped line lengths of 63,474.7103 m; the union of clipped lines is also 63,474.7103 m (reported separately to avoid assuming features are disjoint). This is a metric of returned WFD planning linework inside the component only. It is not channel width, wetted footprint, or a physical water-area estimate. Source object/date roles also do not establish contemporary wetness.

Two transient ASP.NET session-cookie values were excluded from retained MITECO header receipts; response bodies and other captured headers are unchanged. No raster tiles were downloaded. JRC Global Surface Water remains a possible independent, dated observational source: https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/download. The JRC 2024 Data Users Guide states Monthly History v1.4 spans March 1984–December 2021, while v1.5 has 2022–2024 history; monthly codes are 0 no observation, 1 not water, 2 water detected. Preserve no-observation/masked cells as unknown. The guide documents Collection 1/Collection 2 co-registration offsets that can reach or exceed 30 m in some path/rows; v1.4-era and 2022–2024 products must not be assumed perfectly registered. Guide: https://storage.googleapis.com/water-world/downloads_ancillary/DataUsersGuidev2024_v.5.pdf. The JRC page requests attribution to EC JRC/Google and Pekel et al. (2016), doi:10.1038/nature20584.

At the application's roughly 100–153 m grid-cell scale, one cell spans about 3.3–5.1 nominal 30 m pixels on each side. A valid positive monthly pixel supports water detection somewhere in that pixel footprint at that time; it cannot establish that an entire larger cell is water or identify a precise shore. A well-observed negative supports only non-detection under valid observations for that month. Clouds, absent observations, masked never-detected water, mixed months, seasonal water and cross-collection registration remain unknown or ambiguous. For narrow subcell fragments, including the small administrative residual, the source may have no pixel center in the fragment; fractional footprint overlap is alignment-sensitive and cannot support a crisp land/water assignment. Hydrography centerlines likewise do not support width or wetness inference.

## Conclusion and limits

The APA WFD bounded response is a reproducible, useful contextual line reference for the entire component. APA's broader hydrography network source is suitable in principle but local completeness could not be established from the observed BBOX behavior. MITECO hydrography is documented and freely reusable with attribution, but bounded vector acquisition was unavailable through tested official service endpoints and national file exports were out of scope. JRC offers dated surface-water observations suitable for a carefully masked, native-grid follow-up, but no raster comparison was executed here. Consequently no physical water/land fraction or class is determined for the component or its residual. No findings here identify territorial owner, validate a legal boundary, or authorize a geometry change.
