## Reproduced technical findings

The archived `review.md` reports crosswalk SHA-256 `bed3c32dc5280767fcaa34add87f1ae27973385618f3aa64dae0b6fecc3ddaa4`; the actual 319,025-byte crosswalk and its manifest descriptor are `28725b15d2f112f4cfbdc9390f39075164bb191ea91ca9904c63203b7b797fd3`. The earlier prose hash is incorrect. This dated clarification does not rewrite or replace the archived review.

The legacy reproducer accepts the correct 226 IDs with the first ID appended (227 entries, 226 unique). Running both the exact roster and duplicate roster completed and emitted identical crosswalk and control bytes. The current ID list itself is exact; the false pass is a validation defect. The corrected reproduction checks count, uniqueness and set equality against both issue #1101's declared roster and the pinned #908 list before calculating measurements. Its controls reject duplicate, missing and fabricated IDs.

The legacy area routine can cancel opposite-signed parts of a valid multipart polygon. Its self-comparison can report IoU 1 with both coverages 0. The corrected derivative makes valid geometries, normalizes polygon ring winding (CCW exterior, CW holes), and sums positive ellipsoidal areas of polygonal components. Identical square, mixed-winding two-square multipart, and polygon-with-hole controls all have IoU and both coverages 1; the hole reduces area; disjoint squares have all overlap measures 0. Independent recomputation of the 226 selected historical/OCHA candidate pairs agrees with all 678 archived measurements to eight decimals. No selected pair had an orientation-cancellation difference, so the defect does not imply the existing selected scores are wrong.

This is a like-for-like correction of the archived PyProj geodesic-edge comparison method. Its square-metre intermediate values are diagnostic inputs to the historical IoU/coverage results, not canonical land-area estimates. The shared geography helper's straight-source-edge area method is a distinct calculation and would not reproduce the baseline comparison values.

The archived #908 evidence manifest declared no numeric metrics, bindings or summaries. This packet binds all 2,486 numeric values in the 226 archived crosswalk rows, all 678 independently recomputed scores, 14 aggregate counts, source-tier inventories, and numeric controls to exact JSON pointers. Metrics retain baseline vintage and the immutable #908 merge as evaluation commit. These are historical-source comparisons, not current administrative measurements.

## Source meaning, vintage and limits

The geometry comparison pairs a 2017 geoBoundaries CMR ADM3 historical snapshot with the retained OCHA COD-AB Cameroon operational administrative-boundary archive. OCHA's archived package declares CC BY-IGO 3.0 and provides 10 ADM1 regions, 58 ADM2 departments and 360 ADM3 features. Every packaged ADM2/ADM3 parent code resolves to the adjacent tier. Its associated coverage metadata describes 2019-01-04 to 2025-10-30 and a 1987-08-22 boundary vintage. OCHA describes the layer as a common operational dataset. This role and vintage do not establish current national legal boundary truth. The retained WRI/Cameroon Forest Atlas comparison layer is secondary and reports 360 feature records despite a 359-unit description; its arrondissement `code_arr` values are null, and it is not a legal authority. The existing review identifies nine lower-IoU candidates, 17 unresolved encoded-label mappings, disconnected-piece uncertainty, and 11 spatial-parent differences. This packet does not adjudicate them.

Official MINAT material inspected on 2026-10-06 says its local-administration structure includes 10 regions, 58 departments and 360 arrondissements, describes the governor/prefect/sub-prefect administrative levels, and assigns its Department of Administrative Territory work on creation, delimitation, district registers and boundary disputes. These pages establish institutional role and a current count statement, not an authoritative complete georeferenced ADM3 release. Regional overview pages were inconsistent internally and across regions (including a department heading/prose count disagreement); they are not treated as a complete roster. An official 2025 appointment decree confirms named office appointments and cites the 2008 administrative organization decree, but does not supply nationwide polygons. No current legal national ADM3 boundary dataset with verified public reuse terms was located in this bounded inspection. Primary-source availability, current exact roster, legal parentage, boundary completeness and reuse permission remain unresolved.

Sources checked (retrieved 2026-10-06 unless specified):

- [MINAT local services](https://minat.gov.cm/presentation/services-locaux/): unit names, roles and stated national counts.
- [MINAT Department of Administrative Territory](https://minat.gov.cm/departements-directions-du-minat/organisation-du-territoire/): government function responsible for administrative district registers, creation/delimitation and disputes.
- [MINAT regional overview pages](https://minat.gov.cm/presentation/carte-des-regions/): secondary cross-check; contradictory counts/content mean they cannot establish complete roster.
- [MINAT decree download listing](https://minat.gov.cm/telecharger/?tax%5Bwpdmcategory%5D=decrets): restoration lead for decrees 2007-115 and 2008-376. The linked decree files could not be reliably retrieved during this inspection, so their full contents are not asserted here.
- [Official 2025 appointment decree](https://minat.gov.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/decret_n_2025_357_du_22_07_2025.pdf): examples of current appointment/decree evidence; not a national boundary dataset.
- [OCHA COD guidance](https://knowledge.base.unocha.org/wiki/spaces/imtoolbox/pages/2557378679): operational dataset review/maintenance guidance; not national legal authority.

Official page reuse terms were not verified; those pages/PDFs are not redistributed here. The OCHA and WRI retained bytes, their original metadata and licenses remain in the pinned #908 baseline and are not copied. Exact input hashes, paths, descriptor bytes and license limits are in `evidence-quality.json` and `corrected-measurements.json`.
