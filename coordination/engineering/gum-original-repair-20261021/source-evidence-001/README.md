# GUM source-evidence preparation

This is an intermediate evidence packet for the exact original GUM scope: 139 land cells in 86 intervals, owner 7117. The separate 37-cell/29-interval physical companion is excluded. The original package includes 19 members of `gb:GUM:ADM2`; 13 are positive in-scope stage targets and all 19 remain in the complete positive source-family binding.

The stock source-evidence producer retained the complete USA ADM1 (56 features) and ADM2 (3,233 features) families as conservative competitor inputs. Its complete-bounds screen attached two source operands to each of the 13 target records; it reports 3,287 family features disjoint by bounds per target. These are retained candidate operands, not findings of foreign applicability, sovereignty, or legal authority. The input queue records 269 foreign contact incidences; the source-evidence output does not convert those incidences into accepted exclusions.

The source catalogue records the GUM product as represented year 2021 and USA products as 2018, with the catalogue's license label `Public Domain`. Those are retained metadata claims, not independent verification of upstream rights or of the products' temporal/current applicability. The USA source labels and owner/mask observations do not decide contextual applicability.

The reviewed physical report is whole-target GSHHG reconstruction evidence. Santa Rita, Piti, Yigo, and Umatac each have both missing and extra reconstruction flags; the other nine targets have neither. These target-level differences do not establish per-key physical clearance, land/water truth, source applicability, or qualification. All owners, native requests, acceptance, and credit remain unchanged.
