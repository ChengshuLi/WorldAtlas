## Meaning and limits

Answers describe a source-supported body/cover at the native product pixel containing the unchanged original centre, within its original source-version association. Annual categorical predictions are not dated RGB interpretation, present ground truth, whole larger Atlas-cell classification or per-key confidence. Water is not automatically ocean or unowned; tree canopy is not dry ground; crop/bare cover does not certify permanent dryness. Actual local model context helped define this conservative reviewed answer scope, without voting, smoothing or treating uniformity as independent validation. Missing river/lake/sea, bank, tide, subcanopy wetness or legal facts are retained wherever relevant.

The original physical denominator remains **133,846**. The exact known adopted answer set is 36,760; the 97,086 keys outside this known set are **not** asserted to be the global unanswered remainder, because historical adoptions outside this exact union remain unreconciled.
