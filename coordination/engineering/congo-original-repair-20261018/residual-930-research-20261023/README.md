### Bounded Uganda source-context check

The competing `gb:UGA:ADM2` product metadata describes its 151 units as **Counties**, with a represented-year claim of 2006; the residual centers fall in the recorded candidates Ntoroko and Bughendra. The University of Texas Libraries record for a separate Uganda Bureau of Statistics (UBOS) 2006 dataset describes **sub-regional** units as intermediate between regions and districts and explicitly says they do not partition Uganda. That official UBOS record is not the same identified product as the geoBoundaries county family, so it cannot validate those candidate polygons or decide whether the center overlaps are territorial exclusions. Its linked Stanford geometry download returned HTTP 403 in the available reader; no geometry was obtained through that route. The UBOS 2024 census explorer's current administrative hierarchy is also not evidence of the represented-2006 boundaries. Thus the exact Uganda source context is better bounded, but the applicability question and the 116 missing Irumu/Kimvula target-specific source-evidence rows remain open. Sources: [geoBoundaries Uganda downloads and licensing description](https://www.geoboundaries.org/countryDownloads.html), [UT Libraries, UBOS Sub-Regional Boundaries: Uganda, 2006](https://geodata.lib.utexas.edu/catalog/stanford-cb409rb7884), and [UBOS 2024 Census Explorer](https://statistics.ubos.org/nphc/drilldown?county=4011&district=401&subcounty=401113&subregion=45).


### Follow-up: official UBOS 2006 county geometry comparison for Irumu

A later source-specific check used the distinct UBOS dataset **County Boundaries: Uganda, 2006** (creator/publisher UBOS; Level 4 / ADM4; catalogued as public domain). The downloaded archive is 8,325,415 bytes, SHA-256 `9857cf0088b578beccea29c467f9e1a1224b6341ebe944320c975cb9ca984921`; the raw archive and polygons are not included in this packet. The UT Libraries record describes the 2006 coverage and native downloadable coordinates: [County Boundaries: Uganda, 2006](https://geodata-cdn.lib.utexas.edu/catalog/stanford-sv534fc7879).

The check used only the retained 112 Irumu residual centers and 164 UBOS county polygons. Among the already-recorded Uganda ADM2 candidate labels, 66 centers are labeled Ntoroko and 46 Bughendra. Under the dataset's embedded `.prj` definition and a bounding-extent cross-check, the UBOS 2006 county overlay places 31 centers in Ntoroko, 1 in Bughendera, 30 in Bwamba, and 50 in none of the 164 county polygons. This is a cell-center comparison; it is not a whole-cell test or a territorial/legal decision.

There is a source-data metadata conflict: the embedded `.prj` specifies WGS 84 UTM Zone 36N with a custom 200,000 m false northing, while ISO metadata identifies EPSG:32736 and the ESRI metadata also contains a Zone 36S / 10,000,000 m false-northing definition. The `.prj` transform reproduces the shapefile coordinate extents, but the conflict makes these overlay counts provisional. The result documents a discrepancy between the registered geoBoundaries 2006 candidate labels and this separate UBOS product; it does not decide which source applies to any original COD cell. It does not fill Irumu/Kimvula's missing target-specific source-evidence rows or change any hold, disposition, owner, patch, credit, or denominator. Full method, pins and aggregate counts are in `evidence/uganda-2006-county-center-overlay-112.json`.

### Independent UBOS-era district-line source check

To check whether the 2006 county result had a contemporaneous independent source anchor, I inspected the World Resources Institute's publicly served **District administrative boundaries in Uganda in 2006** archive and its embedded metadata. WRI describes the data as provided by UBOS and cites “Uganda Administrative Boundaries GIS Database,” UBOS, 2006. The metadata says the underlying data collection ran from July 1999 through March 2002 and was updated in 2006; it advises contacting UBOS for an updated district-boundary version. The downloaded archive is 1,646,436 bytes, SHA-256 `be2f3ed89101fd68a909f20bc69fc1f097fcb203254ce06ad584588bf4284de5`. Its shapefile contains 5,767 **polylines** for the 80 districts and declares geographic WGS 84. WRI's record also says the dataset is not for litigation and carries no accuracy warranty. Source pages: [WRI Uganda GIS Data](https://www.wri.org/data/uganda-gis-data) and its [district-boundary archive](https://wriorg.s3.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/docs/Ug_District-boundaries2006.zip).

This is an independent source/context check, not a substitute for the 164 county polygons: the WRI file is district-level linework rather than county-level polygons, so it cannot answer the per-key county containment question or validate the provisional UTM county overlay. I did not reconstruct polygons or compare the linework as if it were polygon evidence. This finding narrows the available contemporaneous source record but does not resolve the conflicting county projection metadata, the 116 target-specific Irumu/Kimvula evidence rows, any territorial applicability decision, or any original-key disposition.

### UBOS census-map context and full-polygon comparison

I directly retrieved and examined UBOS's 2002 *Population Size and Distribution* report (1,339,227 bytes; SHA-256 `8b2a9092b173ac3882bc8285a2bf8c97cd02917eb8c340638e4434bec75d0769`) and the 2014 Bundibugyo District profile (1,859,473 bytes; SHA-256 `52230b61ab2f3f8db67aecc867d099ffc87480fac9d3b4579c5f310157cdc48e`). The 2002 report's page 12 map names Bundibugyo and D.R. Congo and depicts an international boundary; the 2014 profile's page 18 population map labels Bwamba and Bughendera counties and their sub-counties. These are official census-map context, not coordinate-level boundary data.

A full-polygon overlay of the two registered geoBoundaries 2006 Uganda ADM2 candidate polygons against the distinct UBOS 2006 county polygons found Ntoroko intersecting only UBOS Ntoroko (853.40 km²; 91.644% of the candidate). Bughendra intersects UBOS Bughendera (542.98 km²; 60.991%), Bwamba (276.45 km²; 31.052%), Ntoroko (23.20 km²; 2.605%), Burahya (6.45 km²; 0.724%), and Bunyangabu (4.81 km²; 0.540%). This comparison is based on the UBOS archive's embedded custom UTM 36N definition; separate ISO/ESRI projection metadata conflict remains. The independent reviewer authenticated both source pins and the method/limits, but could not recompute the area figures because Shapely/GEOS was unavailable; report the table as author-computed, not independently reproduced. A near-zero-area ring noted in unrelated UBOS Bbaale data is outside the intersecting set.

I also found the UBOS 2006 National Boundary catalog record (recorded as Public Domain), but its documented geometry object returned HTTP 403. No geometry was obtained by another identity or alternate source route.
