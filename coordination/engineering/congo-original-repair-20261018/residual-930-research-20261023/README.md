### Follow-up: official UBOS 2006 county geometry comparison for Irumu

A later source-specific check used the distinct UBOS dataset **County Boundaries: Uganda, 2006** (creator/publisher UBOS; Level 4 / ADM4; catalogued as public domain). The downloaded archive is 8,325,415 bytes, SHA-256 `9857cf0088b578beccea29c467f9e1a1224b6341ebe944320c975cb9ca984921`; the raw archive and polygons are not included in this packet. The UT Libraries record describes the 2006 coverage and native downloadable coordinates: [County Boundaries: Uganda, 2006](https://geodata-cdn.lib.utexas.edu/catalog/stanford-sv534fc7879).

The check used only the retained 112 Irumu residual centers and 164 UBOS county polygons. Among the already-recorded Uganda ADM2 candidate labels, 66 centers are labeled Ntoroko and 46 Bughendra. Under the dataset's embedded `.prj` definition and a bounding-extent cross-check, the UBOS 2006 county overlay places 31 centers in Ntoroko, 1 in Bughendera, 30 in Bwamba, and 50 in none of the 164 county polygons. This is a cell-center comparison; it is not a whole-cell test or a territorial/legal decision.

There is a source-data metadata conflict: the embedded `.prj` specifies WGS 84 UTM Zone 36N with a custom 200,000 m false northing, while ISO metadata identifies EPSG:32736 and the ESRI metadata also contains a Zone 36S / 10,000,000 m false-northing definition. The `.prj` transform reproduces the shapefile coordinate extents, but the conflict makes these overlay counts provisional. The result documents a discrepancy between the registered geoBoundaries 2006 candidate labels and this separate UBOS product; it does not decide which source applies to any original COD cell. It does not fill Irumu/Kimvula's missing target-specific source-evidence rows or change any hold, disposition, owner, patch, credit, or denominator. Full method, pins and aggregate counts are in `evidence/uganda-2006-county-center-overlay-112.json`.

### Independent UBOS-era district-line source check

To check whether the 2006 county result had a contemporaneous independent source anchor, I inspected the World Resources Institute's publicly served **District administrative boundaries in Uganda in 2006** archive and its embedded metadata. WRI describes the data as provided by UBOS and cites “Uganda Administrative Boundaries GIS Database,” UBOS, 2006. The metadata says the underlying data collection ran from July 1999 through March 2002 and was updated in 2006; it advises contacting UBOS for an updated district-boundary version. The downloaded archive is 1,646,436 bytes, SHA-256 `be2f3ed89101fd68a909f20bc69fc1f097fcb203254ce06ad584588bf4284de5`. Its shapefile contains 5,767 **polylines** for the 80 districts and declares geographic WGS 84. WRI's record also says the dataset is not for litigation and carries no accuracy warranty. Source pages: [WRI Uganda GIS Data](https://www.wri.org/data/uganda-gis-data) and its [district-boundary archive](https://wriorg.s3.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/docs/Ug_District-boundaries2006.zip).

This is an independent source/context check, not a substitute for the 164 county polygons: the WRI file is district-level linework, not county polygons. A bounded nearest-line check of the 112 Irumu residual centers found all 112 nearest to shapefile record 5488 (zero-based index 5487), whose DBF adjacency fields are `LEFT_FID=-1`, `RIGHT_FID=61`. By directed-segment side, 50 centers lie left and 62 right; nearest-line distances range from 1.248 m to 449.229 m (median 114.737 m). A retained cross-source join found all 62 right-side centers in UBOS 2006 county polygons whose `DNAME_2006` is `BUNDIBUGYO`: Ntoroko 31, Bughendera 1, and Bwamba 30. The remaining 50 left-side centers fall outside all 164 UBOS counties. This supports a local spatial association of WRI `RIGHT_FID=61` with Bundibugyo, though there is no direct WRI FID-to-name lookup table. The WRI metadata disclaims accuracy and litigation use; line-side agreement is corroborative source context, not county containment, a legal boundary finding, or a territorial-applicability decision. No polygon was reconstructed. The method and aggregate result are in `evidence/uganda-2006-district-line-overlay-112.json`; `evidence/reproduce_uganda_2006_district_line_overlay.py` reproduces them from both hash-pinned archives. This check does not resolve the conflicting county projection metadata whole-cell qualification, or any original-key disposition. The separate Irumu 112-key stock diagnostic is in `../irumu-source-target-diagnostic-001/`.

### UBOS census-map context and full-polygon comparison

The 2002 report's page 12 map names Bundibugyo and D.R. Congo and depicts an international boundary; the 2014 profile's page 18 population map labels Bwamba and Bughendera counties and their sub-counties. These are official census-map context, not coordinate-level boundary data.

A full-polygon overlay of the two registered geoBoundaries 2006 Uganda ADM2 candidate polygons against the distinct UBOS 2006 county polygons found Ntoroko intersecting only UBOS Ntoroko (853.40 km²; 91.644% of the candidate). Bughendra intersects UBOS Bughendera (542.98 km²; 60.991%), Bwamba (276.45 km²; 31.052%), Ntoroko (23.20 km²; 2.605%), Burahya (6.45 km²; 0.724%), and Bunyangabu (4.81 km²; 0.540%). This comparison is based on the UBOS archive's embedded custom UTM 36N definition; separate ISO/ESRI projection metadata conflict remains. The independent reviewer authenticated both source pins and the method/limits, but could not recompute the area figures because Shapely/GEOS was unavailable; report the table as author-computed, not independently reproduced.
