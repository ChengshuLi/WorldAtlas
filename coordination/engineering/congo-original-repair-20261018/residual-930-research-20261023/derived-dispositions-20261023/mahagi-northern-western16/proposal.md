## Why propose researched-unresolved

The original center-based source-membership check has been performed against the exact pinned candidate features: all 16 centers hit both their original COD target and original UGA ADM1 competitor. The archived full-release comparison is a separate lineage/representation check, not a substitute geometry for the original matrix. The two most decision-sensitive Western centers have a bounded historical OSM side reconstruction at a release-compatible date and independent verification. The stock diagnostic has screened the complete original request and retained the adverse holds. No specific additional lawful primary-source check is identified in the reviewed evidence that is likely to adjudicate which original competing source representation governs these cells. Reconstructing every relation member or demanding an exact unpublished Wambacher export snapshot would not be a proportionate missing predicate of the center-based contract. The remaining issue is source applicability and representation lineage, not an omitted point-in-polygon test.

Accordingly, propose only that these 16 original rows be recorded as researched-unresolved if root's unified review accepts this rationale. Keep all 16 native `territorial_exclusion_cells` holds. Claim zero repairs, zero owner changes, zero hold removals, zero package completion, and zero accepted credit. Keep Padyere `[14441,129432,153804]` separate and pending because its exact 2006 Commons source file remains unidentified and actionable.

## Clarification: why the 11/3/2 strata are all still proposals

The 11/3/2 counts describe the *later cross-check* (official geoBoundaries 2.0.0 full-release feature membership crossed with source-relative OSM side), not a rule that independently admits or excludes a COD cell. The retained 930-key original center-conflict matrix (`pr2733-local-handoff-20261023/coordination/engineering/congo-original-repair-20261018/residual-930-research-20261023/evidence/930-key-center-conflict-matrix.json`, SHA-256 `1b3805a65d528b24d9327bd4c13f1e0b4a8cc0ef908addd728dc2482ff986174`) contains all 16 exact rows. Each row records both the original COD ADM2 target and its original Uganda ADM1 candidate as center-hit candidates (`center_hit_count=2`, `foreign_family_center_hit_count=1`, original target source vector member true). Thus all 16 centers hit the original pinned simplified UGA feature; the full-resolution official comparison differs for three because the two polygons are not identical.

- **11 later UGA/inside matches:** the archive and OSM side cross-check corroborate the Uganda-side reading of the foreign candidate. They do not choose between that candidate and the simultaneously center-inclusive original COD target, nor supply a source-priority rule. The unresolved question is applicability of those competing original source representations, not whether the newer UGA feature contains the point.
- **3 later DRC/outside matches:** the original pinned UGA candidate still has a center hit in the original 930 matrix, while the denser later official release polygon classifies the identical center outside and the OSM side is DRC-tagged. The geometric flip is explained by the distinction between the simplified pinned ring and its full-resolution release counterpart; it is not an exact-geometry contradiction. Without a source-priority rule establishing that the full release supersedes the pinned source body used by the diagnostic, it remains a representation/source-applicability disagreement, not clearance of the original competitor record.
- **2 conflicts:** the later archived feature itself contains the center while the historical way labels its side DRC. This is the direct representation conflict already described above.

The complete original diagnostic remains adverse: all36 Mahagi rows and all9 Rutshuru rows are held as `territorial_exclusion_cells`. Its source evidence retains the full 15 target-level territorial-exclusion operand roster (including the relevant UGA ADM1/ADM2/ADM3 sources) and whole-target physical record; it does not assign every operand causally to each key. The per-key original center matrix supplies the specific target/foreign center overlaps for these16. No whole-cell test is added as a new eligibility rule: the proposed unresolved rationale rests on unresolved applicability of the original source-relative center conflict, the stock-held result, and the absence of an evidenced source-priority/supersession rule. Physical land support does not decide territorial source applicability. All original holds remain.
