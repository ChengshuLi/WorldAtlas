## Source purpose and vintage limits

The pinned geoBoundaries source metadata for `gb:AGO:ADM1` identifies US Census Bureau as source, year 2021, public domain, and links to a generic Census subnational-population page. The exact Lunda Sul feature identity and geometry are authenticated in the retained original geography corpus. The available catalog metadata does not provide a direct 2021 Census release or exact source-feature crosswalk. Earlier same-family Kimvula research examined official Census archive offerings and found the located Angola products were 2019 and 2024, not an exact matching 2021 file; the official 2016 general Census metadata is not a crosswalk to this feature. See the reviewed same-family evidence in [PR 2724](https://github.com/ChengshuLi/WorldAtlas/pull/2724).

Fresh primary context examined for this slice:

- The U.S. Census Bureau's [2024 Demobase technical document](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/international-programs/mapping-files/time-series/Angola-Namibia-Technical-Document.pdf) says that the study acquired subnational boundaries from HDX (2024a/b) and aligned international boundaries to U.S. State Department LSIB. It describes an 18-province, 162-municipality, 558-commune Angola structure. This is a 2024 study lineage and does not identify the pinned 2021 feature's source release.
- INE Angola's [2024 Lunda Sul census report](https://censo2024.ine.gov.ao/Relatorio_Provincial_Lunda_Sul_Censo2024.pdf) is a 189-page official provincial report. Its title pages identify definitive 2024 census results. The map index names a municipality/commune/locality map under Law 14/24 of 5 September. It establishes a 2024 administrative/census context, not the historical provenance or applicability of the 2021 geoBoundaries ADM1 feature.

These checks support the existence and scope of the source conflict but do not establish that the Lunda Sul feature is an applicable territorial exclusion for the original COD cells. No present-day legal-boundary certificate is imposed by this packet.
