## Evidence and limits

Tanzania retains original source-feature indices and the full owner-level contact records; these contacts are explicitly not represented as per-cell geometry intersections. `source-applicability-questions.json` preserves the exact owner/family questions and source-vintage caveats. `input-custody.json` provides the compressed artifact checksum and the deduplicated public repository paths, commit references, Git blob IDs, sizes, and available SHA-256 pins; no source bodies are copied.

The retained physical issue reports remain cohort-level; no aggregate issue is assigned to one of these keys.
