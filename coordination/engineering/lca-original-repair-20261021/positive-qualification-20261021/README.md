# LCA positive qualification attempt (local evidence)

This packet reuses the existing current-owner observation and the retained 2015 CC0 geoBoundaries ADM1 source. Scope remains 360 land cells in 165 original intervals, owner 7120. A separate local screen matched 326 original land keys against the high-precision DoPDUR representation and 324 against the rounded EPSG:4326 response; 324 keys are common to both sets, with two projected-only keys and zero rounded-only keys. The two projected-only keys are (120729, 86724) Gros Islet and (120852, 86741) Dennery. The other 34 projected mismatches remain held. These are source-comparison strata, not legal-applicability or acceptance determinations.

`physical-inputs.json` preserves the exact ten consumed source geometries from the historical whole-target run and appends the current canonical `atlas:territory:LCA` geometry as an explicit downstream identity binding. `physical-evidence.json` is the unchanged whole-target report; `physical-run-bindings.json` records the exact producer, adapter, comparison module, inputs, output, runtime and limits. The whole-target report found five targets with reconstruction issues but no candidate center in those whole-target residuals. That is not a per-cell physical qualification.

The DoPDUR body is not copied into this packet. Its raw geometry remains task-local because the retained response does not establish redistribution terms. Its 326/324 comparison is corroborative only.

The retained response is from the official Saint Lucia WFS endpoint `https://geoservices.govt.lc/geoserver/stats/wfs`, WFS 2.0.0, feature type `stats:boundries_level_1` (server spelling), with response filename `boundries_level_1.json`. Its GetCapabilities service title, abstract, provider name/contact, fees, and access constraints are blank. The official [Saint Lucia Administrative Boundaries page](https://geoservices.govt.lc/maps/adm-boundries/) publishes the WMS/WFS endpoints and labels the page DoPDUR (Department of Physical Development and Urban Renewal), but gives no layer-specific license or reuse terms. The official [Open Data Policy](https://www.govt.lc/media.govt.lc/www/resources/publications/open-data-policy-final-appr.pdf) says the Department of Public Service selects the license for government open data, calls for free reuse with attribution and no endorsement, and excludes third-party data the Government lacks the right to redistribute (sections 5.6.1–5.6.4). The policy is not a dataset-specific license; neither the layer's rights holder nor the applied license is named in the service metadata or page. Access is confirmed; exact layer reuse rights remain unresolved. No service access or tool denial occurred.
