# Operative city-plan purpose relevant to the 2019–2020 reading

**Verified primary published text: [Decision 1396/QĐ-TTg, 25 September 2012](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?docid=163853&pageid=27160), Government document 163853.** Its title approves the adjusted Nha Trang general plan through 2025; the page records effect from signature, 25 September 2012.

Relevant operative locations, translated/paraphrased:

- **Article 1 §1:** study area 26,547 ha consists of existing Nha Trang natural area 25,260 ha plus 1,287 ha in Diên An/Diên Toàn, Diên Khánh. The wider study area is not a stated municipal annexation.
- **Article 1 §5(b):** bay functional zones include conservation and tourism; island functional construction land totals about 385 ha.
- **Article 1 §7(b), Nha Trang Bay:** “Vùng mặt nước trong khu vực bảo tồn, hạn chế hoạt động du lịch, giữ an toàn rạn san hô.” This explicitly controls conservation-area water surface. Island provisions separately name Hòn Tre, Hòn Tằm and Hòn Trí Nguyên, distinguishing forest conservation from development.
- **Article 2:** the province must publish the approved plan and issue its management rules.

Thus this source's city-planning purpose includes island land and bay-water management. It is not limited to the mainland built-up core. It does not define every maritime administrative boundary or attach that purpose to each original ADM2 polygon component.
