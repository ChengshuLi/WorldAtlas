## Primary-source evidence

The Presidency's *Plan National de Développement 2018–2027*, paragraph 71 (printed page 34; zero-based PDF page index 42, i.e. PDF page 43), says administrative redistricting included the “délimitation et le regroupement” of Bujumbura Mairie communes into Muha, Ntahangwa, and Mukaza. The text is directly accessible in the official PDF at [presidence.gov.bi](https://www.presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/PND-Burundi-2018-2027-Version-Finale.pdf). This is affirmative evidence that the administrative targets changed after the catalogue's 2007 representation-year claim. It does not by itself identify the exact successor polygon for each old source feature or prove where any individual grid cell falls relative to a changed line.

The official 2023 Organic Law 1/05 is indexed as establishing five provinces and 42 communes. Official search-index snippets place Commune Muhuta in the new Bujumbura commune list and show Bugarama in a zone-list context. The 2022 Senate explanatory draft explicitly states that its proposed new Muhuta commune would combine the old Bugarama and Muhuta communes. The draft is not enacted-law proof. The 2024 INSBU census table uses the post-reform structure and includes current names such as Muhuta, Ntahangwa, Gisagara, Muyinga, Rumonge, and Nyanza; it is a statistical table, not a boundary crosswalk. Sources: [2023 Organic Law PDF](https://assemblee.bi/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/n%C2%B05-du-16-mars-2023.pdf), [2022 Senate draft](https://www.senat.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-ORGANIQUE-PORTANT-DELIMITATION-DES-PROVINCES-Expose-des-motifs-1-60.pdf), and [2024 INSBU statistical yearbook](https://api.insbu.bi/storage/documents/01M0WF6F346CZ0KBK8A07YJVSP.pdf).

## Review conclusion and remaining evidence

The mechanical diagnostic does not substantiate a positive repair or completed exception. For Muha and Ntahangwa, the official 2018 plan makes a specific source-vintage/identity conflict concrete for 50 cells. For Bugarama and Muhuta, the draft-plus-current-law index makes a further 31 cells a focused question, but the exact enacted annex relationship is not confirmed. No blanket modern-42-commune crosswalk is required merely because a source claims 2007; instead, the missing evidence should be targeted to the 21 original owner IDs and the particular cells/targets for which present-location membership is asserted. The 2024 name continuity cannot substitute for boundary evidence.

The useful next step is a readable official enacted annex or authoritative administrative lineage that identifies predecessor/successor units for the exact owner IDs, followed where needed by an exact source/canonical membership comparison. The nine whole-target physical-issue targets remain adverse evidence, but they are not categorical blockers under the actual current residual evaluator. The evaluator requires complete support/query rosters and exact matching issue geometries, carries nonempty residual/conflict/hierarchy geometries as veto pointsets, and tests requested candidate centers against them. Thus a nonempty issue can coexist with a candidate whose exact cells avoid it; this does not clear or repair the target-level issue. The older wording in the additive-gap-repair prose that contradictory land/water and hierarchy operations must be exact-empty is superseded by the inspected implementation at commit 94fd166b4e0ced382659e2c15a1a5c14141717f8, blob b56641a48e2c5456999c2ea60dd68d8404a10cd1. Until source applicability and all target-specific predicates are established, 529 means “diagnostic supported,” not “positively qualified”; the 54 held cells are not an accepted disposition either.
