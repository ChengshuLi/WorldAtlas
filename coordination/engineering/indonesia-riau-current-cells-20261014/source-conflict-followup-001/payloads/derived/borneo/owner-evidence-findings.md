## Acquired legal instruments and their scope

[Sarawak Administrative Areas Order 2016, Swk. L.N. 204](https://lawnet.sarawak.gov.my/lawnet_file/Subsidiary/SUB_Issue%20No.%2050_L.N.%20204w(H).pdf), published 25 July 2016 and effective retrospectively from 1 August 2015, supplies the administrative schedule applicable to an asserted 2020 source vintage. [The 2022 Order, Swk. L.N. 65](https://lawnet.sarawak.gov.my/lawnet_file/Subsidiary/SUB_Issue%20No.%2019_L.N.%2065%20Administrative%20Areas.pdf) took effect on 31 March 2022 and expressly revoked it. Their schedules cite MP12/125 for Serian/Tebedu, MP2/125 for Sri Aman and MP7/125 for Kapit/Belaga/Song/Bukit Mabong. These plans are deposited at the respective divisional Lands and Survey offices. Both instruments treat the narrated bearings/distances as approximate and give demarcated ground boundaries priority. The schedule repeatedly follows the existing international boundary; it does not provide a complete surveyed international polyline. Its E/N values lack a declared datum/projection in the inspected document. Do not assume WGS84 or choose a familiar Borneo projection. The 2016 Tebedu entry also contains inconsistent start/closing marker names and printed latitude/longitude labels; preserve them rather than silently repair them.

[The 2026 amendment, Swk. L.N. 313](https://lawnet.sarawak.gov.my/lawnet_file/Subsidiary/SUB_Issue%20No.%2070_L.N.%20313-Administrative%20Areas.pdf) was actually acquired: published 13 October 2026, effective retrospectively from 1 October 2026. It substitutes the Kuching Division item in the 2022 schedule. The five Sarawak district candidates here belong to Serian, Sri Aman and Kapit, so this specific amendment does not rewrite their items. This is a check of the actual amendment, not a claim that no other instrument exists. The [official catalogue](https://lawnet.sarawak.gov.my/lawnet/Law/TLnetPublishedOrdList.jsp?LTyp=Idx&aId=A) lists all three.

[JUPEM's 21 November 2019 notice](https://www.jupem.gov.my/en/info-semasa/berita-terkini/13) identifies the resolved sectors as the Simantipal vicinity and markers C500–C600 in Sipitang. The [same-date NRES notice](https://www.nres.gov.my/ms-my/pustakamedia/Lists/Papar-Berita.aspx?ID=957) identifies the counterpart districts and both government signatories. Neither retrieved notice contains the signed boundary map, its coordinate list, datum, uncertainties or segment extent. Sipitang is a directly relevant district-name match for our Nunukan/Sipitang member, but matching a district name does not establish that the member lies in C500–C600. Simantipal must not be substituted just because it appears in the same MoU.

[The 2023 Defence Ministry technical-committee notice](https://www.kemhan.go.id/strahan/2023/07/28/49th-meeting-of-the-joint-indonesia-malaysia-boundary-technical-committee-on-the-demarcation-and-survey-of-the-international-boundary.html) identifies the bilateral technical forum and approval of both sector Co-Project Director and CBDRF/JBM reports. [Its Southernmost Point inspection notice](https://www.kemhan.go.id/strahan/2023/08/22/kegiatan-inspeksi-dan-supervisi-survei-untuk-penyelesaian-outstanding-boundary-problems-obp-ri-malaysia-segmen-sinapad-sesai-di-kabupaten-nunukan-dan-malinau-kalimantan-utara.html) identifies the SP pillar and participating Indonesian/Malaysian survey bodies. These establish concrete custodians and report names; neither supplies exact surveyed coordinates for our cells.

## Public acquisition route inspected

Its [Survey Plan page](https://elasis.sarawak.gov.my/page-0-29-15-Survey-Plan.html) enumerates DP/SDP/BP/SP/CP cadastral products, which must not be equated with the gazette's miscellaneous administrative plans. No MP7/125, MP2/125 or MP12/125 body was found in the inspected public pages; no account, cart or payment action occurred. The concrete next inquiry is availability of these three deposited plan identifiers, their revision dates, and the signed bilateral line used on them. This research task did not contact custodians.
