## Primary territorial interpretation and limits

The national State page states that the traditional municipalities form the sixteen States: https://www.palaugov.pw/states/ . The national physical-features page lists the ten Babeldaob States: https://www.palaugov.pw/executive-branch/ministries/finance/budgetandplanning/physical-features/ . Its old capital description demonstrates stale narrative content. It corroborates State identity, not present legal lines or current coastline.
