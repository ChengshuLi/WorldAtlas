# Hypothetical grid-centre screen near eastern joins

These numbers describe two specific **unselected** research alternatives against the saved Caucasus line on the historical draft-93/V7 grids. They do not identify current draft-163 differences, approve labels, prove either join, or bound the impact of a different correction. Each result counts centres in the previously polygonized bounded faces that are within a WGS84 geodesic radius of the named join. It is not a new map build. Source and alternative side classifications are separate diagnostics and may be ambiguous.
