# Caucasus boundary audit — research status

**Distance and source-geometry update:** The measurements below are historical research screens; earlier projected distances do not satisfy the project's WGS84-only distance rule. The audit tools now use WGS84 inverse-geodesic distances while retaining coordinate-linear source edges. Subdivided numerical stationing and closest-point searches have limitations documented in `GEODESIC-TOOLS.md`. Sampling each selected edge at its quarter points and midpoint found up to 0.317 m between the corresponding coordinate-linear and shortest-geodesic-arc points. Do not replace the source geometry with those arcs. The WGS84 source-preserving rechecks do not select a new boundary.

This is a source and geometry audit, not an accepted boundary or a replacement
geometry. The selected record in Neon research branch `continent-review-v1`
is `v2-land-mainland-boundary-draft-1 /
caucasus_blacksea_anapa_to_caspian`; its canonical binary64 geometry SHA-256 is
`9f4ae3bc778ebbcd0258a19868acf0fa655763f5e849361196160fb4289c0d52`.
The four sections below are analytical subdivisions of **one** stored
LineString, not four independent source records. See the
[boundary viewer](https://worldatlas-land-diff-review.chengshu.chatgpt.site/boundary-detail.html)
for their extents. Do not equate selection for research with approval.

## Findings by section

1. **Anapa coast:** A previous comparison found the approximately 4.04 km
   straight edge within 1 cm projected tolerance of the shared HydroBASINS
   level-12 boundaries of basins 2120554610 and 2120005980, at 42 sampled
   positions. Those basin IDs and their downstream terminations do not by
   themselves prove named destinations or the intended divide. A western
   approximately 3 km terrain candidate has stronger local cross-profile
   indications but unverified joins and drainage; do not select it yet.
2. **Black Sea/Azov drainage divide:** The first three sections match
   HydroBASINS polygon edges at 500 m sampling, apart from an endpoint
   discrepancy up to 22.3 m. This establishes source-relative alignment, not
   that every matched edge separates the intended drainage systems. Basin
   selections and the original construction inputs remain unrecovered.
3. **Central Caucasus:** The same source-relative comparison applies. JAXA
   terrain tiles provide an additional surface-elevation screen, not full
   drainage routing. Original terrain inputs and construction provenance are
   incomplete; exact raster equivalence with historical V2/V7 is unproved.
4. **Eastern Caucasus to Caspian:** 397 retained vertices match the immutable
   Alexandr Trubetskoy source after binary64 parsing; this is supporting
   evidence, not proof of the construction history. His published method
   explicitly describes an interpreted ridge through Altiaghaj and Khizi and
   acknowledges alternate routes. At 500 m samples, distance to any local
   HydroBASINS edge had median 189.9 m, p90 479.9 m and maximum 4,085.9 m.
   Nearest arbitrary basin edge is not necessarily the correct continental
   divide, and this discrepancy alone cannot select a replacement.

## Independent structural check

On 2026-11-05 a read-only Neon query examined all 2,292 vertices of the selected
LineString in stored order. It found 2,291 edges and zero consecutive
zero-length edges. On a 6,371,008.8 m sphere, the sum of endpoint great-circle
distances is approximately 1,548.9 km; the longest edge is 4,026.1 m and 413
edges exceed 1 km. The longest is edge ending at zero-based vertex 37. These
are vertex-spacing diagnostics, not proof of joins, source interpolation,
watershed correctness, or cell assignment. The sphere-based length differs from
the prior 4,037.35 m WGS84 geodesic calculation. A continuous LineString
necessarily connects consecutive vertices, even if an analytical segment's
separate display endpoints appear disconnected.

## Source and interpretation limits

The [HydroBASINS Europe level-12 v1c source](https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/hydrobasins)
is catalogued in Neon and R2 with SHA-256
`21797d53e0792cae047d25f0c3cfb570c7ef43700ec962b35b2ac92467e29f03`;
its recorded full R2 readback matched. It is a modeled drainage dataset, and
its own polygon-edge match is not independent validation of the model.
Different terminal identifiers do not establish named outlets or seas.

[Trubetskoy's method](https://sashamaps.net/resources/europe-asia-boundary/)
is a cartographic interpretation, not an official surveyed boundary.
His immutable source is catalogued in R2/Neon at commit
`7aa03ec54a262e8e97e8273f60621c530ec4e998`; OSM-derived source obligations
require review. Original JAXA AW3D30 ZIPs and API COGs are catalogued separately.
Use original DSM/MSK/STK where available: mask 0 alone is insufficient to
establish independent PRISM data, and some API conversions map valid original
-1 m values to NoData. Surface elevations, sparse cross-sections and local
downhill traces do not certify the divide. For western SRTM comparisons,
acknowledge OpenTopography and the dataset source; SRTM is not independent of
SRTM-derived HydroBASINS.

## Next work before changing the line

- Recover the original western basin selections, code and shoreline bytes or
  explicitly record their absence. Reconstruct source edges and classify the
  downstream network on **both** sides, including coastal and same-terminal
  cases; do not assume every level-12 edge is the intended watershed.
- Recheck western candidate joins and complete drainage paths, including flats,
  sinks and sensitivity to DSM quality, smoothing and crop extent. Retain
  unresolved results where routing is blocked.
- Trace eastern alternatives against original quality-screened terrain and
  independently sourced drainage, with a continuous end-to-end comparison;
  distinguish a topographic ridge from a particular continental convention.
- Verify coastal endpoints against the selected physical shoreline, test
  topology and center-sampled grid impact, and present the alternatives before
  changing the boundary. Do not infer historical V2/V7 equality from the stored
  geometry hash. Keep raw source bytes in R2, structured decisions in Neon,
  and code/documentation in GitHub.
