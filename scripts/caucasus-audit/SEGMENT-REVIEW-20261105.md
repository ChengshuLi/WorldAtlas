# Caucasus source fidelity and geographic confidence: segment review

Research assessment, 2026-11-05. The selected LineString has four analytical sections; none of the observations below authorizes changing selected geometry or land cells. Source fidelity and geographic accuracy are distinct.

1. Anapa / Azov coast: the initial ~4 km edge was previously found to coincide with a HydroBASINS edge, not independently surveyed as the intended shoreline-to-watershed transition. The physical coastline endpoint and its provenance remain unverified. Retain provisionally; exact shore and drainage inputs needed.
2. Western Black Sea / Azov drainage divide: sampled selected geometry closely tracks HydroBASINS; that source is a coarse modeled basin boundary, not independent proof. The long Abin-region candidate agrees with several named ridge landmarks and modeled drainage screens, but its joins and outlet assignments are not independently settled; alternatives can increase mismatch counts in the historical draft-93 snapshot. No candidate selected.
3. Central Greater Caucasus: HydroBASINS correspondence is evidence of source alignment but does not establish original construction or field accuracy. WGS84 numerical closest-point screening against coordinate-linear selected edges gives 34.84 m to the WGS84 coordinate for Marukh Pass in https://westra.ru/passes/Passes/3468 (source flags that coordinate unconfirmed) and 77.67 m to Mamison Pass in https://westra.ru/passes/Passes/3917 (says its coordinate comes from a general-staff map). These two points are consistent with the broad route, but precision and source independence are uncertain and isolated points do not validate the intervening line.
   
   New Jvari (Cross) Pass lead: https://skitalets.ru/information/books/tepli-dzhimaray-kazbek-2410_4604/ describes the pass as connecting Terek and Aragvi headwaters. A location at 44.45332E, 42.50435N from https://madloba.info/en/region-mtskheta-mtianeti/natural-attractions/krestovyi-pereval/ is 440.85 m from the selected line; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paso_de_la_Cruz_(Georgia) gives 44.453937E, 42.504578N, 389.61 m away; https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Крестовый_перевал gives 44.453056E, 42.500000N, 793.19 m away. These reports may represent different landmarks or share source data; none is an independently surveyed drainage crossing. The geographic discrepancy warrants a targeted audit, not a correction.

Eastern Caucasus: 397 consecutive selected vertices reproduce the published author's source feature exactly as binary64; this establishes source fidelity for that run, not construction lineage or watershed accuracy. A mapped Qusarçay tributary intersects the selected line and screened JAXA elevations descend along it, while the modeled replacement has unresolved drainage and joins; another candidate crosses a provisional historical blue feature. These remain geographic leads, not approved changes. Source data and optional higher-resolution sheet evidence are described in EAST-1978-GRID-AND-FEATURE.md and EAST-IMAGERY-AND-YATUKH-LEAD.md.

Priority for independent follow-up: retrieve original JAXA for Jvari, map natural channels and their outlets across the candidate area, locate a verified road-pass/watershed crossing, and quantify any connected source-supported alternative before making a decision. A public Overpass waterway query returned HTTP 406 and provided no data. Do not infer absence of streams. No assertion that this segment is resolved is warranted.
