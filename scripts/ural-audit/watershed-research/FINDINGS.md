In gap 02, EU polygon 2121045390 and Siberia polygon 3120360000 have an intersection polygon whose signed WGS84 geodesic area is approximately -0.0000024 square metres (numerically near zero). The input anchors selected for local probing yielded one source segment of 1,538.702 m in gap 00 and an eight-vertex source route with WGS84 geodesic segment sum of 7,851.076 m in gap 02. These are DEM D8 drainage roots crosswalked to HydroBASINS at their outlets, not ground survey.
