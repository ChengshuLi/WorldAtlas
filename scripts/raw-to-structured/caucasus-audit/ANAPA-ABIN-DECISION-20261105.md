# Anapa and Abin source evidence

V8 uses the Anapa opening and Abin drainage divide reconstructed by the
[Caucasus source replay](CAUCASUS-WGS84-REPLAY.md).

The Anapa opening uses the pinned Trubetskoy source and OSM coastline.
The N044E037 JAXA tile was used to screen the terrain around the Shingari
opening and its inland join. The terrain profiles support the choice,
with limits from model resolution and drainage coverage.

The Abin joins were located using search anchors from the SRTM-seeded
divide. The selected divide is reconstructed from the published N044E038
JAXA product and named OSM stream seeds. The construction recipe pins the
coastline, terrain, and hydrography sources used by the replay.
