# Demographic evidence staging

Historical data expansion is postponed by the user's latest scope instruction. This checkpoint freezes **396 census religion records for the observation year 2021 only**: 64 from England and Wales and 332 from Australia. Of these, 193 have a derived primary affiliation and 203 explicitly remain unknown because nonresponse and disclosure differences can change the reported plurality. There are **zero culture records**, no fabricated examples, and no ancient or 2026 carry-forward. The staging directory has not been published or imported into the production database by this preparation script.

Every one of the current **49,614 location IDs** has a coverage outcome in `data/demographic-evidence/location-coverage.json.gz`. Its summary covers all **252 represented reference-owner groups**, with 272 owner/continent combinations because some owners have territories on multiple continents. A coverage outcome records presence or absence of evidence; it does not approve the geography or declare demographic coverage complete.

## Frozen source and assignment contract

- [ONS/Nomis TS030](https://www.nomisweb.co.uk/output/census/2021/census2021-ts030.zip) supplies published population counts of self-reported religious affiliation for all usual residents on 21 March 2021. The inspected metadata records publication on 29 November 2022 and statistical disclosure perturbation. [Nomis copyright terms](https://www.nomisweb.co.uk/home/copyright.asp) explicitly license ONS material under the Open Government Licence.
- [ABS G14 General Community Profile](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/datapacks/download/2021_GCP_LGA_for_AUS_short-header.zip) supplies person counts by religious affiliation at 2021 local government area level, observed on 10 August 2021. The [ABS copyright policy](https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer) explicitly licenses the applicable material under CC BY 4.0. Actual source tables, metadata and copyright receipts are retained locally.
- Stable category IDs identify broad census categories. Christianity is not silently reclassified as Catholicism. No religion is an explicit census response, not the residual of missing adherence counts. Broad Christian counts are not added to their component denominations. Australian secular beliefs and other spiritual beliefs remain distinct from explicit No religion responses.
- Primary affiliation means the largest reported affiliation by population count, not a land-area majority or a requirement that more than half the population follow it. A value is left unknown when the winning count does not exceed the runner-up plus all unanswered responses and a conservative disclosure guard. The guard is the absolute category-total discrepancy plus 20 persons. This is an explicit staging uncertainty policy, **not a publisher-certified error bound** or a guarantee of the unobserved true plurality.
- Each record carries integer published counts, total population universe, winning and runner-up counts, unanswered count, reported share, category-total discrepancy, observation/release information, licence, census geography/code, match method and uncertainty. Integer census estimates affected by disclosure controls are not exact person identities.
- Intervals are half-open `[2021, 2022)`. Observation dates are preserved within provenance. This deliberately supports the census observation year only; it does not imply that the affiliation persisted throughout every day of 2021 or before/after that year. Unknown evidence remains a record rather than acquiring a neighbouring or national prior.

## Whole-territory correspondence

The scalar is assigned only when the census geography represents the whole current location. No census count is distributed by land area.

England/Wales uses unique original ONS county/unitary identities, one explicitly documented spelling correspondence (Rhondda Cynon Taf/Taff), and a complete North/West Northamptonshire union. Greater London contains all 33 source borough members and uses the independently published London regional total, avoiding summing perturbed borough counts. A symmetric footprint diagnostic requires at least 99% correspondence between the current location and its pinned complete source territory. Of 141 identity candidates, 77 fail that conservative geometry guard and remain unassigned; Isles of Scilly, for example, retains only 43.4% of its source footprint in the current display geometry. The retained source is a 2019 administrative reference, not an independently validated 2021 or ancient boundary reconstruction.

Australia compares actual official 2021 LGA boundaries against current source territories and requires a unique match with at least 99% coverage in both directions. The official boundaries were simplified with `mapshaper -simplify 2% keep-shapes` for the identity diagnostic. This planar comparison is documented as a correspondence check, not an ellipsoidal historical ownership calculation. All 435 IBRA-adapted partial-LGA locations are rejected: a full LGA affiliation total cannot be copied into each fragment. Other changes, coastal clipping and disconnected residuals without a unique qualifying match also remain unassigned.

There are 730 explicit rejected-location assignments in `unassigned-locations.json`. The 99% threshold does not eliminate all geographic uncertainty; it is disclosed in each retained assignment. Original ZIP hashes, extracted table hashes, the comparison geometry and the transformation command make the checkpoint reviewable without storing the 40 MB Australian boundary ZIP in deployment assets.

[Ghana's actual religion table](https://statsbank.statsghana.gov.gh/api/v1/en/PHC%202021%20StatsBank/Population/religion_table.px) and [ethnicity table](https://statsbank.statsghana.gov.gh/api/v1/en/PHC%202021%20StatsBank/Population/ethnic_table.px) contain 295 national/regional/district areas, with selectable all ages, both sexes, locality and education totals. Christian aggregates overlap their denominational breakdown. The national ethnicity total (30,484,536) differs from the religion total (30,753,327); the exact eligibility and missing population universe require evidence before computing primary culture. Neither unlicensed raw count table is redistributed in staging. No additional research or record expansion will be performed in this iteration.

[GeoEPR's actual codebook](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/epr/geoepr/EPR_2021_Codebook_GeoEPR.pdf) was inspected alongside 1,685 downloaded polygon records. It describes politically relevant ethnic-group settlement areas from 1946 onward, explicitly permits overlap, and says statewide groups need not constitute a population majority everywhere. The predominant share of a group's members residing in a region does not imply that the predominant share of that region's inhabitants belong to the group. These polygons therefore cannot populate primary culture through area overlap. No redistribution licence was established from the inspected material.

[Statistics Canada's actual CD table](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/tbl/csv/98100354-eng.zip), [IBGE's age-limited metadata](https://servicodados.ibge.gov.br/api/v3/agregados/9537/metadados), [US Religion Census definitions](https://www.usreligioncensus.org/data_collected) and [Pew's national 2010–2020 estimates](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/how-the-global-religious-landscape-changed-from-2010-to-2020/) remain documented evidence leads, not local values. Country-level estimates cannot be painted into every location.

## Reproduction and integration

`scripts/prepare-demographic-evidence.py --cache /workspace/scratch/demographic-research` prepares this checkpoint from cached actual Nomis/ABS downloads. Python uses the already installed Shapely; source boundary conversion uses the repository's existing mapshaper. No additional dependencies or database mutations are introduced.

The output contract is:

- `religion-records.json`: hosted-compatible scalar records with stable location/category/source IDs; unknowns have `value:null` and `category_id:null`.
- `sources.json`: source identities with explicit licence, vintage, supported interval and historical status.
- `categories.json`: stable religion categories, each carrying an existing source ID.
- `source-receipts.json`: exact original source and licence receipt hashes and geometric transformation.
- `index.json`: record part/hash, category/source/receipt hashes, canonical `footprintHash` hash, current hierarchy hash and preparation algorithm hash. `location_index_sha256` is separately the hash of raw `world-index.json` bytes, not a geometry hash.
- `location-crosswalk.json`, `unassigned-locations.json`, `source-inventory.json`, `coverage-by-territory.json` and `location-coverage.json.gz`: assignments, explicit rejections and exhaustive evidence coverage outcomes.

The single evidence record file exceeds the 1 MiB public import batch limit, so the independent prepared-evidence importer must split it into bounded batches. The staged original source tables are small; the large unlicensed/unused lead datasets and the 40 MB original boundary ZIP stay outside deployment assets. Do not widen supported intervals or apply assignments after footprint changes without repeating correspondence validation. Existing imported direct evidence takes priority over these derived assignments.

Validation uses the real hosted migrations/importer in an isolated in-memory SQLite/D1 adapter, verifies source/category/location joins and batch bounds, and compares hosted/shared resolver values at 2020, 2021 and 2022. Unsupported years 3000 BC, 1000, 1800 and 2026 return no staged religion evidence. The coverage ledger accounts for every current ID exactly once. The recorded result is in `data/demographic-evidence/validation.json`. Production integration/publication is a separate task owned by the parent agent.
