# Ural delta source evidence

The V8 Ural boundary follows the mapped main-stream route in OpenStreetMap
relation 214415, version 122, through the Zolotoy branch and the Ural–Caspian
canal. The selected geometry is in the editable `v8-mainland` Neon revision
under `ural_kara_coast_to_caspian`.

The Kabiyev et al. delta study supports the main-channel and canal
interpretation. The retained publication is registered in the Neon raw catalog
as `v8-evidence-publications-8c9e3ff101e9`. The
`v8-mainland-decision-source-evidence` document records its role and the
associated R2 and checksum pins. The line follows the connected mapped
channel geometry, and the current build checks its terminal and water
closure against the physical grid produced in that run.

The [V8 Ural source replay](../../scripts/ural-audit/REVIEWED-URAL-20261106.md)
documents the source recipe and how to regenerate the intermediate and
selected lines.
