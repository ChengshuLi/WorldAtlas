# Worldwide region, area and province semantic review

Reviewed 1 October 2026 against the current hierarchy. All six continents, **66 regions, 489 areas, 5,220 provinces and 49,614 member locations** are represented below. Antarctica is excluded. Every regional branch has sourced role research and concrete follow-up; **no region is declared semantically complete**. A structurally complete chain is not evidence that its labels, local scale, source geometry or geographic purpose are correct.

The review proceeds from continent and subcontinent conventions in [GLOBAL_MACRO_REVIEW.md](GLOBAL_MACRO_REVIEW.md), through every current region, then every actual area and province. [region-semantic-review.json](../data/region-semantic-review.json) contains the complete unit-ID inventory, source roles, source vintages, licenses, member counts and flags. Different source ADM integers are never treated as automatic atlas tiers.

## What this review found

- Urban administrative envelopes can hide several independent local settlements. The Tokyo aggregate uses “Metropolis” as a city signal and joins 63 mainland source members; the official FY2025 Tokyo profile distinguishes the 23 special wards, independent Tama municipalities and island municipalities. The same city-envelope audit is required everywhere, including Chinese provincial municipalities, Korean metropolitan jurisdictions, Taiwanese special municipalities, Brazil, Ulaanbaatar and European city aggregations.
- Granularity remains mixed across entire countries, not just the user’s examples. India combines refined subdistrict locations with retained district locations; Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Peru, Bolivia and Uruguay use broader source units than nearby local municipal/county territories. Sparse rural units may be valid exceptions, but their purpose must be documented.
- Many area labels are botanical source remnants or country envelopes rather than coherent broader geography. Foreign-labelled fragments occur across Europe; duplicate/split named sources occur in Mexico, Brazil, Japan and Paraguay. Membership must consider whole named source parents, not only the biggest centroid overlap.
- Reference vintages differ from current administration. Norway’s 2022 source differs from the official 2026 county classification; Vietnam’s 63-era units predate its 2025 reform; Kazakhstan source references predate 2022 splits; Northern Ireland’s old districts and Nepal’s older districts/names require dated identity treatment. Old reference geometry must not be advertised as an ancient or current administrative fact.
- Some missing grid locations are source defects, not merely unavoidable tiny islands. Namibia’s tiny southern polygons carry northern district names; Borama was reduced from about 1,575 km² in the source to about 1.24 km² by later reference masking. Marshall island coastline reductions and the Kiribati “Phoenix Islands” label/footprint mismatch need independent geographic source repair. The report compares all 53 grid omissions with original sources, while explicitly warning that source polygons can include water and marine area.
- The actual IBGE API carries municipality→immediate region→intermediate region→state membership. That supports a functional grouping research path, but does not approve all current Brazilian rural aggregations. The actual French commune API lists 22 French Guiana communes, whereas the atlas retains only three physical local adaptations.

## Evidence and completion standard

Official source evidence was inspected where available: Tokyo government profile/listing, IBGE API and documentation, French commune API, Eurostat NUTS principles, US Census divisions, Canadian statistical/ecological frameworks, ONS, INSEE, Spanish INE, Norwegian SSB, ABS, PNG NSO, Vanuatu, Kiribati, TAAF and Namibia source metadata. Secondary administrative taxonomy is explicitly identified as corroboration requiring primary dated/legal/boundary verification; it is not a current-count or geometry certification. Failed requests, landing pages and rejected domains are recorded rather than counted as evidence.

All 66 regional rows remain open. Every area/province record is source-assessed, including actual member roles, represented years, licenses, weak matches, repeated tiers and known pixel omissions. The matrix does not claim that reading a national taxonomy independently verifies every location polygon. Closure requires a sourced correction or a justified exception for each flagged unit, plus migration and geographic validation.
