# First three time-dependent attributes

These attributes belong to each fixed location at a selected time. They do not change the geography.

## 1. Population

Store one total for everyone within the fixed location boundary, including urban, suburban, and rural residents, regardless of rank. The location is the smallest unit; there are no separate settlement entities or settlement-population records. Estimates are encouraged when defensible: record their basis and provenance, and present them as estimates. Use zero when direct evidence or a justified estimate supports no inhabitants. Use unknown only when even an estimate cannot be justified.

## 2. Location rank

Give the whole location one rank at a time: **Uninhabited, Rural, Town, City, or Unknown**. Uninhabited corresponds to population zero, whether directly evidenced or estimated. Rural means inhabited and non-urban, including nomadic populations. Town and City describe historical character and role, with no population cutoffs. Their geographic area may include urban, suburban, and nearby rural map cells, all included in the location population. A City may have fewer residents than a Rural location. Rank can change over time while the location boundary stays fixed.

Use Unknown only when no rank can be justified. Unknown population does not require Unknown rank when historical character supports a classification.

## 3. Primary culture

Record the resident culture with the largest population share at that time; it need not exceed half the population. Determine it from resident evidence or a defensible estimate. Use Unknown when no primary culture can be justified and not-applicable when population is zero.

Retain supported or estimated culture shares without inventing percentages. A breakdown is **complete** when it accounts for the population, **partial** when only some shares are known, or **unavailable** when no shares can be justified. A defensible estimate may identify the primary culture even when the breakdown is partial or unavailable; store the primary culture separately from breakdown completeness.

Record sources and estimate basis for these values. Other attributes remain for later discussion.
