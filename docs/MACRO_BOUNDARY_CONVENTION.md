# Macro geographic boundary convention and exhaustive review

Reference review date: **2026-10-01**. This is a geographic foundation review, not a historical-record expansion. Antarctica remains excluded.

Every one of the **6 continents, 29 subcontinents and 49,614 locations** in the current reference release is inventoried in `data/macro-boundary-decisions.json`. Each group includes its exact immediate children, all descendant group IDs, every member location ID, member-derived bounds, independently stated geographic convention and source references. Source-policy profiles, owners, labels and political affiliations are never used to compute the proposed continental assignments.

This product proposes changes; it does **not** mutate the hierarchy, location geometries, database, frozen six-continent review files or historical attributes. It records **20 supported reporting conventions and 15 open own-boundary reviews**. An accepted reporting convention is not a surveyed physical boundary, dated administrative membership, or semantic approval of every descendant. All 35 complete-branch semantic statuses remain open. These distinctions must survive integration into the dashboard and publication gates.

## Concrete corrections

- Four reference names become more accurate without changing identity: **Andean South America → Western South America**, **Mesoamerica → Mexico and Central America**, **Western Europe → Western and Central Europe**, and **Southern Ocean Islands → Subantarctic Indian Ocean Islands**. The present members are broader than the narrower physical/cultural meanings of the old names. These are reference label changes, not invented historical aliases.
- The **Azores area, with all 19 locations**, is proposed for the European North Atlantic association through the existing Iberian/Atlantic geographic branch. This is an explicitly chosen archipelago convention, not a conclusion from Portuguese ownership or an assertion that every Azorean island lies on a European plate. Macaronesia may remain an alternative geographic tag because a tree cannot express every overlapping region.
- **Rwanda (28 locations) and Burundi (119 locations)** are proposed for the East African Rift/Great Lakes branch. Country ownership stays separate. Their old botanical Central Africa classification is not treated as an exact Congo-basin boundary.
- **30 locations** have a source-proven whole-location majority on the other side of the selected continental divide: 10 Kazakhstan and 1 Russian location move to Europe; 14 Russian locations move to Asia; 5 Egyptian locations move to Asia. Exact stable IDs, before/after parents, source masks, source hashes, winning shares, uncovered shares and conservative uncertainty bounds are in the machine-readable product. This is a global candidate pass, not a Kazakhstan-only correction.
- The location changes require **2 new geographic steppe areas and 7 new geographic portions of source administrative clusters**. Each new province keeps `derived_from_id` and is labeled as a geographic portion. Original administrative identity and dated records are retained. No whole administrative province is dragged across the continental divide merely because some of its locations cross it.

## How the majority calculation works

The physical river source is the public-domain Natural Earth 10m river/lake centerline dataset at repository commit `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19`. Its SHA-256 is `bb854a900ecbd3b408df46d5e16e3e0f974ba55993f9d8b5c26e855273c0905a`.

The longest substantive Ural main-channel segments join at an exact source coordinate. Short source junction branches are excluded from the divide rather than becoming spurious continental borders. The Suez river/lake source has an approximately 84m endpoint mismatch: it is explicitly treated as source generalization, and a conservative 0.0016-degree corridor (approximately 150–178m at that latitude) is removed from the side's guaranteed coverage before majority is accepted.

The same WGS84 ellipsoid surface integral used by ownership preparation measures the actual clipped land footprint, with 16-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature. Holes are not land. A side must cover **more than 50% of the entire location land area**, after the conservative uncertainty deduction. Land outside the surveyed river reach remains in the denominator. A location can therefore have a supported majority while a minority remains outside that reach; uncovered land can never turn a 49% surveyed share into a majority. Ties, competing majorities and insufficient coverage remain unresolved.

All **570** intersecting Europe/Asia and Africa/Asia candidates in the relevant physical reaches are measured, including Russian territories and Egypt's Port Said/Ismailia/Suez border areas. The 30 changes are not chosen by modern owner. **78 measured locations remain unresolved** because the available river reach cannot prove a strict majority. This is not a reason to manufacture a mountain crest or extend a river through unsurveyed coastal land.

## Remaining physical precision work

Four specific segments remain open: the **Ural mountain crest north of the measured river reach**, the **Greater Caucasus crest**, the **exact Darien watershed crossing**, and the **named near-Anatolian Aegean-island association crosswalk**. Broad narratives establish possible conventions but do not supply a licensed, verified digitized crest. No straight longitude, country border, bounding-box screen or tectonic-plate simplification substitutes for that evidence. The exact open groups and candidate location IDs are recorded, so a later worker can address those segments without repeating the global inventory.

Island associations are stated separately from mainland/plate membership. Socotra retains its African continental-fragment association irrespective of Yemeni ownership. All New Guinea remains Oceanian irrespective of Indonesian sovereignty. Cocos and Christmas retain separately stated Asian Indian Ocean associations irrespective of Australian ownership. Hawaii/Rapa Nui stay Polynesian. Bermuda is already in the North Atlantic mainland-associated branch and is not moved back to the Caribbean. Southern Indian Ocean and southern Atlantic subantarctic islands have explicit reporting associations after Antarctica exclusion; these are not claims that the islands lie on an Australasian or African continental shelf.

An original source remainder mislabeled Phoenix Islands does not contain the real eastern Phoenix archipelago. Its present western-Pacific geometry is retained and the missing eastern source coverage remains open; no existing shape is relocated to hide the omission.

## Source and integration contract

The JSON pins every hierarchy/geometry input file and all six frozen geographic-review files. It also pins the preparation/area algorithms, source retrieval hashes, inspected source-text hashes, source revisions where available and license/context. Natural Earth geometry is public domain. Wikipedia geographic discussions are cited with CC BY-SA 4.0 text provenance; this product supplies factual paraphrases and does not redistribute article text. National Geographic articles are bibliographic/context references under publisher terms, with no media or geometry copied. Failed requests are excluded from evidence.

`python scripts/audit-macro-boundaries.py --output .cache/research/macro-boundary/current-membership-audit.json` regenerates the exact inventory. `python scripts/prepare-macro-boundary-decisions.py` prepares the read-only product from that inventory and pinned research cache. It refuses changed input bytes, changed inspected source text, missing conventions and ambiguous exact group IDs. After applying a release migration, preserve the original pinned reference inputs and this product as the migration receipt; do not silently recompute an old proposal against changed geography.

Integration must check `current_name`, `current_parent_id`, geometry hashes and all input pins before creating the new groups or changing membership. Apply adjacent-tier parents, recompute parent footprints from member locations, keep every original identity/history, and publish explicit before/after crosswalks. The macro `supported-convention` label may map to an own-boundary-supported status only while retaining the declared reference convention and any listed exceptions. The entire branch remains semantically open until all lower-tier evidence is completed.

`python test/macro-boundaries.py` checks strict and partial-coverage majorities, latitude-sensitive area weighting, holes, the exhaustive 35-group/49,614-location inventory, every proposed parent chain and source geometry preservation, input pins and unresolved coverage. It does not mistake a structural test pass for geographic semantic completion.

## Validated staged migration

`scripts/apply-macro-boundary-decisions.py` applies the proposals to the **validated source-territory repair stage**, not to live geography. It first checks canonical-content pins for every original hierarchy/geometry/review document, the source-repair receipt's original byte hashes, disjointness from the 27 source-footprint changes and 25 removed duplicate IDs, exact association member sets, every direct location's name/parent/ancestor/geometry guards, and conservative strict majority evidence. Formatting changes in the staged JSON do not disguise content changes.

The output is `.cache/macro-boundary-repair-stage/{before,after}` with a Git-trackable immutable receipt at `data/macro-boundary-migration.json.gz`. The compressed receipt preserves every original group, all 61 group before/after changes, all 7,631 changed ancestor/name chains, the full before/after properties of the 30 directly reparented locations, all unchanged geometry IDs/hashes, source-stage input pins, new identity definitions, and explicit zero historical-record transfer. No groups are retired in this macro stage; the prior source-repair receipt separately preserves its retired identities.

The staged result has **49,589 locations → 5,133 provinces → 471 areas → 66 regions → 29 subcontinents → 6 continents**. All 49,589 location geometries are unchanged by the macro stage. Counts follow source-backed changes rather than a target quota. Original current-reference owners, dated ownership intervals, attributes and names remain attached to the same stable location IDs.

The full original public-domain physical river source is retained as `data/macro-boundary-source-rivers.geojson.gz` (2.27 MB); its decompressed SHA-256 must equal the pinned Natural Earth source hash. Future workers can inspect and remeasure the source without access to this session's research cache. The decision product and migration receipt retain original source URLs, retrieval/revision hashes, masks and uncertainty details.

`python test/macro-boundary-migration.py` adds seven checks for unvalidated or conflicting source repairs, missing/stale identities, changed source footprints, non-adjacent parents, uncertainty-adjusted majority, separation of own-boundary support from descendant completion, and the complete staged 49,589-location migration. The pre-application macro/source snapshot is deliberately pinned: after integrating this release, validate that immutable snapshot and receipt rather than attempting to apply the old proposal to already changed geography.
