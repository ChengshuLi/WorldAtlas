Deterministic gzip prevents individual tracked files exceeding the Site source-file limit; these complete research ledgers are not added to static website assets.

## New concrete blockers and source-supported proposals

- **Ten live El Salvador `Null` labels**, not zero. Every original shape attribute, source/current geometry hash, bounds, geodesic area, retained-source share, land-mask diagnostic and independent district coverage is embedded. They include land and mixed coastal/border territory; no nearest-name rename or water-only deletion is justified. The previous zero-anonymous summary does not describe current geography.
- **Guatemala has two lake polygons treated as municipalities.** AMSA identifies Amatitlán and AMSCLAE identifies Atitlán; both exact IDs and source attributes are recorded. Natural Earth’s coarse land mask contains their interiors while its lake collection omits both. Correct water-body versus municipal semantics; obtaining complete inland-water proof and an explicit land-location policy precedes retirement or reassignment.
- **El Salvador HDX source counts include water.** Its actual 2024-valid geometry contains 266 district features and 48 municipal features, including four explicitly named lake/reservoir records. Those mean 262 districts + four waters and 44 municipalities + four waters; the provider counts are not legal subdivision totals. The original names, PCodes, areas and geometry hashes are preserved.
- **Trinidad omits Arima.** The original source has 14 features including Tobago; both its same-vintage CLGF profile and the ministry taxonomy identify 14 corporations on Trinidad plus distinct Tobago administration. Exact licensed source boundaries are still required before installation.
- **Guyana’s 27 features are not verified NDCs.** HDX says their role is currently unknown and unreconciled with population boundaries; CLGF documents 65 NDCs in 2017–18. The source-role description can be corrected without inventing local jurisdiction footprints.
- **Suriname source inventory contradicts its metadata.** Fresh pinned geometry contains 58 features, the API claims 62, and active geography has 59 adaptations. Reconcile exact source IDs, codes, legal vintage and source-parent geometry before filling guessed “missing” units.
- Every same-name provincial family is listed with all current IDs, exact children and footprint hashes. West Virginia/Hancock, Alaska/Aleutians West, Buenos Aires/Quilmes, Tierra del Fuego, Guatemala/Sololá, Central-American border portions, Wanica, Norte de Santander, Táchira and Valparaíso are distinguished from the 32 Canadian ecological families crossing different area parents. A shared label alone authorizes no merge.
- Exact archived-member candidates are recorded for Antigua/Barbuda/Redonda, Grenada/Southern Grenadines, Saint Kitts/Nevis, Saint Vincent/Grenadines, Saint-Pierre/Miquelon-Langlade and the three US Virgin Islands. - Province-sized Peru/Bolivia/Uruguay/Belize locations, Brazil’s functional/rural aggregation and Canadian ecological replacements remain independently reviewable decisions. Candidate finer layers and their counts are not an approved replacement geography.

## Reproduce and resume

```sh
python scripts/audit-americas-semantic-followup.py --check
python scripts/audit-americas-semantic-followup.py
```

The committed compressed reports contain source receipts with exact URLs, byte hashes, parsed metadata, inspected facts, prepared-source named inventories, concrete source diagnostic properties and archived candidate memberships. Rebuilding or checking needs no previous-thread cache. New network observations must supply a new `--observations` file rather than relabeling old response bytes as newly fetched. The checks verify every current ID, complete chain, exact hashes, inherited closure inventory and documentation; they do not claim independent semantic approval.

Geometry or membership migration must preserve stable IDs/archives and immutable historical claims, use an explicit source-backed crosswalk, regenerate derived ownership when footprints change, and rerun source-land/overlap/fixed-grid coverage gates. Every unresolved child branch remains open until independently supported; a structurally complete continent or source count cannot close it.
