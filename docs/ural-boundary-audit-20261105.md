# Ural Europe–Asia boundary audit

Status: research assessment, 5 November 2026. This documents the recovered selected line, not a new geographic decision. The selected Neon revision is `v2-land-mainland-boundary-draft-1`, boundary `ural_kara_coast_to_caspian`, geometry SHA-256 `5a6526344cacbc1a23be174f589cec9710a0f317360ec5c8737ba6956c3f7fe2`. Confidence below describes the evidence reviewed, not formal map accuracy.

## Segment findings

1. **Kara coast to the watershed — limited geographic confidence.** Selected ordinals 1–4 are a roughly 75.35 m WGS84 interpolated connector to source ordinal 5. This is an editorial join, not a surveyed ridge or shoreline trace. The physical-grid contact checks are diagnostics of a pinned historical grid, not proof of the present coastline.

2. **Mountain watershed — moderate source correspondence, unproven exact lineage and geographic accuracy.** Retained source sections are ordinals 5–1627, 1896–2320, and 3549–4954; gaps and coast connector are excluded. Against HydroBASINS Europe and Siberia level 12 archives (SHA-256 `21797d53e0792cae047d25f0c3cfb570c7ef43700ec962b35b2ac92467e29f03` and `67ea00df7d14a397f8f78e5b1dc31dcca036018ccbcb36e007ce6854b91d71bd`), 3,200 of 3,454 selected vertices exactly match source vertices; each of the other 254 has a sampled source edge at most 0.5 m away. The candidate edge can be unrelated and segments between vertices were not tested; this is not proof of historic construction, independent divide selection, or positional accuracy.

3. **Northern gap-00 — moderate model support.** Ordinals 1628–1895 exactly equal the archived V5 replacement feature. The contract describes a projected D8 root-divide derivation; exact historic DEM inputs and the former active line were not recovered. An independently retrieved ArcticDEM v4.1 reproduction, using two crop sizes, and Copernicus gave expected opposing basin roots at all 51 sampled stations and all three offsets (100/250/500 m) with the smoothed local tangent. Masked AW3D30 was too incomplete to provide full corroboration. Sampling omits end intervals and between-station behavior. Terrain products may share infill and all tests use the same D8/HydroBASINS framework.

4. **Northern gap-02 — moderate model support.** Ordinals 2321–3548 exactly equal the archived replacement. ArcticDEM and Copernicus gave expected opposing roots at all 237 50 m-spaced interior stations and the same three offsets. Two transects cross the selected line three times, so their local side interpretation is ambiguous. The JAXA masked/unmasked sensitivity and the common model limit independent confirmation. Original historical raw DEM bytes remain unverified. Neither a sourcecut match nor a model screen constitutes a ground survey.

5. **Mountain-to-headwater join — low geographic confidence.** The approximately 2,502.48 m connection from the watershed to the OSM spring is an editorial equal-step WGS84 geodesic interpolation, not a mapped watercourse or surveyed watershed. Chibilyov's [Ural Basin study](https://orensteppe.org/sites/default/files/2008_basseyn_urala_polnyy_tekst.pdf) describes the headwater marsh and subsequent dams, but does not locate the precise watershed junction. The same author's earlier [1988 book](https://elib.osu.ru/bitstream/123456789/2145/1/book0105.pdf), PDF page 23, calls the commemorated spring the river source; the later work distinguishes it from the principal marsh-fed channel. These are differing descriptions by the same author, not independent evidence of a precise junction. Terrain-derived alternatives still have unsupported endpoint joins; do not replace the selected line on that evidence. The public water-register record `12010000112112200001016` names the Ural but does not supply the necessary geometry. A georeferenced survey or registry map, including date, CRS, accuracy, and reuse terms, is the needed evidence.

6. **Ural river main stream — high correspondence to the inspected OSM snapshot, moderate geographic confidence.** All 41,669 selected river edges match the exact coordinate-edge multiset of the 142 main-stream ways in [OSM relation 214415](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/214415), captured at version 122. The original historical capture and construction lineage are not recovered; present overlap does not independently validate every edge or riverbank. A coarse HydroRIVERS downstream screen is consistent with the broad course but cannot resolve cell-scale placement.

7. **Delta and Caspian terminal — moderate support for channel interpretation, terminal details still open.** OSM selects the Zolotoy/Ural–Caspian canal chain; [Kabiyev et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.32523/2616-6771-2021-137-4-74-82) supports the main-branch and canal interpretation, not every exact mapped edge. OSM's node `241421886` has the role `mouth` despite being at the Shagan confluence, approximately 938 km upstream along the selected chain from its coastal terminal. Relation history shows this role and the Shagan tributary were added in version 113; this is evidence of erroneous mouth metadata, not a reason to truncate the river. Mapper intent is unknown. A pinned stage-1 closure screen found 2,956 water cells and one permitted Caucasus-side land terminal among 2,957 unique cells; it does not prove the current grid closure and is not an independent implementation.

## Candidate comparison and downstream rule

The reconciled `v7-list1-wgs84-draft-123` handoff was SHA-256 verified, including all ten listed members. It explicitly did not include a Ural divider overlay. In the mountain bounding box 58.9–67.3°E, 54.6–68.8°N, 3,407 both-land Asia/Europe cells differ from the pinned published V7 grid. Of these, 3,391 lie within 1 km of the sampled selected mountain line: 3,301 candidate-Europe/V7-Asia and 90 candidate-Asia/V7-Europe. Their cell-and-label tuples equal the earlier draft-82 screen. A numerical refinement found the candidate on the same local side at 100, 250, and 500 m half-windows for all 3,391; 54 have a computed line distance below 1 m and 504 below 10 m. Nearby-segment shortlisting and bounded minimization are not a certified global nearest search; local sides do not prove global topology or source accuracy. Sixteen box disagreements are outside this corridor. V7 is a comparison, not the geographic authority. Do not interpret these counts as resolved errors or current results for any later candidate.

A separately prepared draft-111 downstream side-of-divider overlay changed 57 cells, of which 5 became equal to V7 and 52 became different. It was research-only, applied to its pinned input, and its application verifier shares the side-polygon construction. The result is not an approved geographic change, not an island claim, and not evidence of draft-123 overlay application. Re-run against the actual current candidate and physical coastline before any integration, preserving the user-selected cell-side treatment of connected land and the downstream river.
