# Asia: exhaustive semantic decision and correction ledger

Reviewed 1 October 2026. **All 1,947 current geographic groups and all 18,549 Asian location IDs are inventoried** in [the exact decision ledger](../../data/geographic-decisions/asia.json). This includes all 62 reference-owner groups represented on Asian land, including transcontinental/overseas portions. Every old group has one exact disposition; every location occurs once in the local review partitions. Source assessment is not an independent footprint approval.

## Implementable complete-branch corrections

- **Japan:** inspected all 47 pinned prefectural source identities and the official JNTO all-prefecture geographic-bloc listing. Merge all 15 accidental duplicate province fragments, retain the original IDs/history in the archive, and assign all prefectures consistently to the ten published blocs. Chubu becomes Tokai; create Hokuriku Shinetsu; Kinki becomes Kansai. Kagoshima island fragments follow the sourced Kyushu grouping. Both botanical placeholder areas become empty and must be retired. No municipal/local geometry is changed.
- **Azerbaijan:** the official State Statistical Committee list supplies thirteen mainland functional/economic clusters plus Nakhchivan. Create the thirteen mainland provinces and reparent all 71 mainland location IDs; retain all eight Nakhchivan members. This replaces the single 71-location Contiguous Azerbaijan envelope with source-backed local clusters. Original district/city geometry and older names remain reference evidence; no polity ownership is inferred.
- Expand eight source area abbreviations/reference spellings and replace two anonymous Other Territories province labels with the actual named island territories. Naming corrections do not certify their repeated tiers or footprints.

## What is and is not approved

A supported parent decision establishes inspected named grouping/membership and the atlas convention used for it. Parent footprints must be derived from the exact member locations. This does **not** approve every child footprint, municipal reform, settlement classification, primary culture/religion, ancient name or modern rank. Every local review remains explicitly open pending independent source/geometry/purpose outcomes; this document does not silently close thousands of cases based on existing chains.

All Japanese locations were checked against the named pinned source prefecture using their representative points: 1,686 passed and **Tajiri and Ashiya** have small coastal mismatches. That test is diagnostic and cannot approve an entire polygon. The Tokyo aggregate has **53 current source-member IDs**, not the older narrative count of 63; the whole metropolitan envelope is not proven to be the 23-ward urban core. Korea, Taiwan, Shanghai, Delhi, Manila and all other aggregates are subject to the same scope check.

- Oman: Az Zahirah currently contains Musandam territories (Bukha, Khasab, Daba), Masirah island and older Buraimi-related units. The second Az Zahirah province contains only Madha. Inspect every Oman source-parent correspondence against dated primary wilayat/governorate evidence before rebuilding this branch. Country-constrained matching alone did not make these province parents correct.
- Iran: Bandar-e-Gaz is isolated in a second Mazandaran group. A primary dated Golestan county crosswalk is still needed; it must not be merged just because its current name repeats.
- Bayingolin: the Ruoqiang/Alashan physical subdivision is parented under Gansu while other original county fragments are under Xinjiang. Reconcile the full source county/prefecture and physical geography together; never infer a new prefecture from a botanical overlap.
- Greek Aegean islands: Kastellorizo is under the botanical Turkey area. All Aegean/Europe–Asia conventions need one documented geographic boundary policy, independent of owner.
- Coco Islands: the source Cocokyun location is in a split Yangon (South) province in Andaman Islands. The physical northern-Andaman association can be defensible, but a named island-cluster identity/source exception must replace a misleading partial mainland administrative label.
- Southeastern Indonesian islands: Aru, Tual and southern Maluku units appear under Lesser Sunda source geography. Review the complete Moluccan/Timor/Aru physical convention, not a single example.
- India: 3,998 retained refined subdistrict records coexist with 355 district-source locations. Every old state residual, newly refined district province and retained district is included. Full-coverage finer-source evidence or a justified local-purpose exception is required; no equal-area split or district-count quota is proposed.

## Source correspondence and reproducibility

Inspected 52 actual pinned location-source caches and their feature/member identifiers. Metadata canonical names are checked as claims, not assumed correct (Japan labels its municipal source “Subprefectures”; Bhutan uses “Dzongdeys” for named district features). Actual cached feature counts differ from publisher metadata for India, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, Russia, Turkmenistan, Turkey and Taiwan; the exact counts/hashes are retained in the ledger. Such differences require provenance/source-omission explanation and never justify fabricated missing territory. Four Afghan records contain explicitly supplementary OSM border fragments, so they are recorded as mixed-source cases rather than falsely reported as missing all source IDs.

Independent primary pages successfully inspected: [JNTO complete destinations](https://www.japan.travel/fr/destinations/), [Japan Statistics Bureau Statistical Handbook 2025](https://www.stat.go.jp/english/data/handbook/pdf/2025all.pdf), [Azerbaijan official region/member listing](https://www.stat.gov.az/source/regions/?lang=en), and [UN M49 overview](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/). The linked/pinned geodata and current source policy matrices were inspected across every represented territory.

Rosstat and Indian physical-feature requests returned 503; Britannica rejected access; Lakshadweep official history was blocked; Australian territory pages returned 503; a Chinese government deep link redirected to its home page. These outcomes are **not positive evidence**. No correction relies on those failed/redirected requests.
