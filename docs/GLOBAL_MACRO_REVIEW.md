# Global macro-geography review

This diagnostic pass inspected every current continent, subcontinent and region, enumerating all 487 member areas and all 49,614 descendant locations. It identifies corrections across the world; it does **not** certify the 5,221 provinces or the location territories as semantically complete. All branches remain open until their evidence and lower-level reviews are finished.

The exhaustive machine-readable evidence is in `data/macro-review-evidence.json`; it pins the inspected hierarchy hash, source URLs, retrieval results, source hashes, branch IDs, every member area, country/territory counts and specific location artifacts.

## Applied reference corrections

`scripts/apply-macro-review.py` now applies the clearly supported corrections as an idempotent preparation stage. It corrected **713 location name/ancestor chains**, records **83 changed groups and 22 retired groups**, and preserves every location ID and geometry. The canonical footprint hash is identical before and after. The current hierarchy has **49,614 locations → 5,220 provinces → 489 areas → 66 regions → 29 subcontinents → 6 continents**. Before/after chains, original group records, sources and remaining uncertainty are in `data/macro-corrections.json`; the local pre-application hierarchy and all location name/chains are preserved under `.cache/macro-review-before/`.

Applied changes include collective Western Polynesian/Equatorial Micronesian membership, separate Northern Cook/Tokelau groups, Howland/Baker’s Phoenix association, Macquarie’s southwest-Pacific association, English Channel/Irish Sea islands, Bimini and Ragged Island, Saint Pierre/Miquelon, Korean/Taiwan region roles, Sindo’s source parent, Fujian coastal islands, and an owner-independent South China Sea Islands region. Colombia/Venezuela residuals now state their real source administrative portion while keeping macro-boundary review open. Guernsey’s whole-territory modern label preserves its original Sark label and stable identity.

A further source problem was exposed during the geometry checks: the location originally labeled Phoenix Islands retains only western-equatorial-Pacific pieces around 169–173°E, not the real Phoenix archipelago around 172°W. It remains physically in Micronesia and is explicitly named an unverified source remainder; its original label is preserved. Missing eastern Phoenix source coverage remains open. No geometry was moved or invented to hide this discrepancy.

The tables below document the **initial exhaustive diagnostic snapshot**. They must be read together with the correction report; they are not a claim that those initial mistakes remain uncorrected. Neither this stage nor structural checks close the deeper semantic review. All 6 continent conventions, all 29 subcontinents, all current 66 regions and every lower tier still require their recorded closure evidence.

## Evidence and conventions

The actual [WGSRPD second-edition PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tdwg/wgsrpd/52da7828aba9d461dd133c27b3bd7a4407161f54/109-488-1-ED/2nd%20Edition/TDWG_geo2.pdf) and [pinned README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tdwg/wgsrpd/52da7828aba9d461dd133c27b3bd7a4407161f54/README.md) were read. This is a botanical database scheme. Its “Level 1: Continents” and “Level 2: Regions” discussion says that most regional divisions were specially circumscribed for phytogeography or Flora projects, sometimes giving political integrity priority. Azores→Africa is explicitly justified on phytogeographical grounds; all Kazakhstan→Asia favors recognized political boundaries. These are source conventions, not automatic physical-history atlas decisions.

[UN M49](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/) and its [overview table](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/) were inspected. M49 explicitly calls its grouping statistical convenience. It corroborates standard Melanesia/Micronesia/Polynesia roles, but cannot assign every island of a polity to one geographic subcontinent. Kiribati illustrates this: Gilbert and Line islands need their own geographic assignments.

[National Geographic’s continent overview](https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/Continent/) supplies broad continent and island-association conventions. [The inspected boundaries overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundaries_between_the_continents) describes the competing Ural/Caucasus/Aegean/Azores definitions; it is secondary evidence, not a replacement for exact boundary geometry. Britannica requests were blocked, and the former CIA Factbook URL returns a discontinuation page; neither is counted as inspected geographic evidence.

## Highest-priority worldwide corrections

- Split Southwestern Pacific member areas across Melanesia, Micronesia and Polynesia using archipelago roles. Gilbert Islands/Nauru and Tonga/Tuvalu/Samoa/Niue/Tokelau/Wallis-Futuna/Cook groups cannot all be children of Melanesia. Keep original group identity archived and record the replacement graph.
- Remove residual misleading regions: Eastern Canada contains only Saint Pierre and Miquelon; Southeastern U.S.A. contains only Biminis; China contains Sindo plus Kinmen/Matsu/Wuqiu; the Southeastern Asian East China contains only Paracel Islands.
- Correct Isle of Man→Nordic Europe and Channel Islands→Iberia. The Channel Islands’ English Channel/Normandy geography may justify a French-coast regional association while remaining politically distinct; Crown Dependency status alone must not select the geographic parent.
- Investigate label-versus-footprint scope: location GGY+00? is named Sark, but its multipart footprint is about 65.5 km² and spans the wider Guernsey archipelago. Preserve the source label and use a supported whole-territory modern name if confirmed.
- Adopt and document one continent convention before changing Azores, North Caucasus, eastern Aegean islands, western Kazakhstan, Cocos/Christmas or subantarctic islands. Do not use sovereignty as a continent rule.
- Separate Macquarie from a region named Southern Indian Ocean Islands: it lies in the southwest Pacific. Antarctica exclusion does not make a fallback Oceania association a researched source assignment.
- Resolve systemic area-role mixtures globally: entire countries coexist with states/provinces and botanical areas. Rename dated labels only as sourced reference/temporal names; do not silently modernize historical identities.
