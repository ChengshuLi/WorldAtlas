# Top-down geography and regional research handover

This is the workflow for future workers. The initial meta thread completed and published global macro own-boundary approval under #33, with verified final handoff under #36. [MACRO_FOUNDATION_APPROVAL.md](MACRO_FOUNDATION_APPROVAL.md) links the exact certificate, release and frozen envelopes. All 81 regions have entries in `data/macro-foundation/regional-handoffs.json.gz`. GitHub Issues is the status authority. No complete regional branch has been certified under this contract. Antarctica stays excluded; EU5 counts remain scale references.

## Dependency order

1. **Global macro partition approved:** all six continents, 29 subcontinents and 81 regions have fixed sourced reporting conventions and represented-land envelopes. Future changes to an outer/shared boundary require a coordinated macro amendment. Named missing land follows its documented routing and coordinated envelope publication; source shoreline completeness remains regional work.
2. **Review regional interiors in parallel:** after the macro milestone, multiple engineering workers review area → province → location branches inside separate fixed regional envelopes. Every region is accounted for, including small islands and cross-border geography.
3. **Publish each complete regional branch:** validate its sourced decisions, every descendant, migrations and grid representation, and retain a certificate against the actual published release.
4. **Start bounded Luna campaigns for that branch:** targeted population/culture/religion/etc research follows approved territorial meaning. Other regions can remain under engineering review. A campaign covers explicit subjects, attributes, sources and intervals; sparse supported records replace an annual Cartesian expansion.

Source-only collection may occur before step 3, with provisional territorial context. Location-attribute imports require all approval layers in [GEOGRAPHY_RESEARCH_READINESS.md](GEOGRAPHY_RESEARCH_READINESS.md).

## Own-boundary approval versus complete-branch approval

A macro unit's own-boundary certificate states its tier purpose, stable identity, adjacent-tier parent, source-backed boundary convention, named islands/territories, exact shared-edge definitions, approved land domain and published fingerprints. All units of that tier form a consistent partition of the declared domain. Source gaps, contradictory assignments and crossing-location rules must be resolved, not hidden by clipping or a structural pass. Political ownership cannot define this partition.

Own-boundary approval does **not** approve interior locations. Regional workers may change interior subdivisions while preserving the approved outer envelope. The final parent footprint remains derived from approved member locations and must agree with that envelope under documented source precision. A change to an outer/shared boundary requires a separate coordinated macro correction; it cannot be slipped into one region's interior PR.

Complete-branch approval additionally accounts for every area, province and location, sourced corrections or justified exceptions, neighboring scale/purpose, source vintage/license, land/island completeness, grid representation, and preserved identities/history. The branch's permitted subject IDs and current release pins are explicit. A region with an unresolved descendant is not ready for location-attribute imports.

## Small issues, many workers

Keep #7 as the worldwide semantic umbrella; do not reserve it to one worker. Create a macro-approval umbrella and region umbrellas beneath it. Umbrellas track completeness and are unclaimable. Their actionable children have reviewed scope, dependencies and a 1–3 PR budget. A large region is split into source/area/province batches plus a final integration/approval item; a single thread does not hold the entire region indefinitely.

Suggested macro work-item boundaries are: global convention/coverage contract, each unresolved shared-boundary segment or island crosswalk, small subcontinent partition batches, small region partition batches, and final global integration. These are scope patterns, not new approvals or claims that those issues already exist. Boundaries shared by two regions have one joint authoritative item. Do not create hundreds of ready issues before the approved partition establishes their IDs and dependencies.

For each region, retain a complete membership inventory and child-issue crosswalk. Track separately: inventory/screening, own-boundary approval, interior descendants approved, publication, and research readiness. “66 regions screened” is not “66 regions finished.” Child completion cannot close an umbrella whose other branches remain open.

Ready content campaigns depend on the completed macro milestone and their published regional approval, rather than worldwide descendant completion. Source-only campaigns may be ready earlier. Use [WORKER_COORDINATION.md](WORKER_COORDINATION.md) for distinct worker IDs, serialized leases, exact branch ownership, one claimed work item, and squash merges. One publisher serializes geographic publication and revalidates exact global pins after each release; disjoint Git branches do not independently deploy.

## Handover and chat creation

A region handover links its completed approval issue, certificate, published release/pins, full permitted subject inventory, territorial descriptions, migration crosswalks and unresolved source-to-territory questions. Research uses a regional umbrella (for example, Iberia across the timeline), with small children partitioned by **source collection + approved territory/subjects + attribute + supported period**. Examples are one compatible census collection for a bounded population subject batch, or one gazetteer collection for dated names over its supported interval. A child normally produces one PR and at most three; the umbrella does not reserve every historical year to one worker. Unsupported gaps remain unknown; do not invent annual spreadsheets or interpolate merely to fill them. Each research child specifies those partitions and avoids overlap with active campaigns. Engineering defines geography; Luna interprets evidence for those territories and preserves incompatible narrower settlement evidence separately.

Regional completion can make research issues ready and produce a copy-ready prompt. It does not automatically create a new Codex chat, grant private credentials or import facts. Fresh Luna chats require the user's explicit launch/authorized automation; use [LUNA_HISTORY.txt](prompts/LUNA_HISTORY.txt) and select a ready bounded campaign. A worker may continue an already authorized research queue within its scope. Missing credentials are an import blocker, not permission to bypass the gate.
